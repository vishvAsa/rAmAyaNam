Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro

Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War

Sharana continues to describe the individual strengths of the various commanders in the army of monkeys, mentioning their names one by one, in detail.

भागसूचना
  1. वानरसेनाके प्रधान यूथपतियोंका परिचय
सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Sāraṇa points out the long-tailed Vānara commander Hara and the countless dark-hued bear-warriors massed like storm-clouds upon the shore.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

तांस्तु ते सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि प्रेक्षमाणस्य यूथपान् ।
राघवार्थे पराक्रान्ता ये न रक्षन्ति जीवितम् ॥ १ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

तांस्तु ते सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि प्रेक्षमाणस्य यूथपान् ।
राघवार्थे पराक्रान्ता ये न रक्षन्ति जीवितम् ॥ १ ॥

मूलम् - VR

तांस्तु ते सम्प्रक्ष्यामि प्रेक्षमाणस्य यूथपान् । राघव अर्थे पराक्रान्ता ये न रक्षन्ति जीवितम् ॥ ६-२७-१

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

(सारणने कहा—) ‘राक्षसराज! आप वानर-सेनाका निरीक्षण कर रहे हैं, इसलिये मैं आपको उन यूथपतियोंका परिचय दे रहा हूँ, जो रघुनाथजीके लिये पराक्रम करनेको उद्यत हैं और अपने प्राणोंका मोह नहीं रखते हैं ॥ १ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

सारणनु मुन्दुवरिसुत्तानॆ- महाराज! वानर सैन्यवन्नु गमनिसुत्तिरुव निमगॆ, रामन सलुवागि तम्म पराक्रम तोरुव, जीवद हङ्गन्नु तॊरॆदु बन्दिरुव दळपतिगळ परिचयमाडिकॊडुत्तेनॆ.॥1॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Now I shall declare in full to you, as you look on (prekṣamāṇasya), those troop-commanders (yūthapān) who, valorous for the sake of Rāghava (rāghavārthe), do not spare their own lives.

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

1–3.

Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa) observes that Sāraṇa now introduces principal troop-commanders even more distinguished than Panasa (पनसादिभ्योऽप्युत्कृष्टान् प्रधानयूथपतीन् वक्तुं प्रतिजानीते). Adopting the reading वालाः (`tail-hairs’) in 2a, he defines a vyāma as the span of both outstretched arms (व्यामो नाम प्रसारितबाहुद्वयप्रमाणम्) and likens Hara’s multicolored tail-hairs shining in the sky to solar rays refracted as rainbows across clouds (मेघेषु शक्रचापादिरूपेण प्रसृता रश्मय इव).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

स्निग्धा यस्य बहुव्यामा दीर्घलाङ्गूलमाश्रिताः ।
ताम्राः पीताः सिताः श्वेताः प्रकीर्णा घोरकर्मणः ॥ २ ॥
प्रगृहीताः प्रकाशन्ते सूर्यस्येव मरीचयः ।
पृथिव्यां चानुकृष्यन्ते हरो नामैष वानरः ॥ ३ ॥
यं पृष्ठतोऽनुगच्छन्ति शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।
वृक्षानुद्यम्य सहसा लङ्कारोहणतत्पराः ॥ ४ ॥
यूथपा हरिराजस्य किङ्कराः समुपस्थिताः ।

मूलम् - VR

स्निग्धा यस्य बहु श्यामा बाला लान्गूलम् आश्रिताः । ताम्राः पीताः सिताः श्वेताः प्रकीर्णा घोर कर्मणः ॥ ६-२७-२ प्रगृहीताः प्रकाशन्ते सूर्यस्य इव मरीचयः । पृथिव्याम् च अनुकृष्यन्ते हरो नाम एष यूथपः ॥ ६-२७-३ यम् पृष्ठतो अनुगच्चन्ति शतशो अथ सहस्रशः । वृक्षानुद्यम्य सहसा लङ्का रोहणतत्पराः ॥ ६-२७-४ यूथपा हरिराजस्य किम्कराः समुपस्थिताः ।

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘इधर यह हर नामका वानर है । भयंकर कर्म करनेवाले इस वानरकी लंबी पूँछपर लाल, पीले, भूरे और सफेद रंगके साढ़े तीन-तीन हाथ बड़े-बड़े चिकने रोएँ हैं । ये इधर-उधर फैले हुए रोम उठे होनेके कारण सूर्यकी किरणोंके समान चमक रहे हैं तथा चलते समय भूमिपर लोटते रहते हैं । इसके पीछे वानरराजके किंकररूप सैकड़ों और हजारों यूथपति उपस्थित हो वृक्ष उठाये सहसा लङ्कापर आक्रमण करनेके लिये चले आ रहे हैं ॥ २—४ १/२ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

अल्लि काणुत्तिरुववने हरनॆम्ब वानरनु भयङ्कर कर्ममाडुव इवन उद्दवाद बालदल्लि कॆम्पु-हळदि बूदु बिळि बण्णद अनेक मारुगळष्टु कूदलुगळिवॆ. हरडिकॊण्डु मेलक्कॆद्द इवन कूदलुगळु सूर्यकिरणगळन्तॆ हॊळॆयुत्तिवॆ, नडॆवाग नॆलदल्लि ऎळॆदाडिकॊण्डु होगुत्तानॆ. इवन हिन्दॆ लङ्कॆयन्नु आक्रमिसलु नूरारु साविर वानर किङ्कररु वृक्षगळन्नॆत्तिकॊण्डु सिद्धरागि निन्तिद्दारॆ.॥2-4॥

पदच्छेदः

4. shatashaH = hundreds; atha = and; sahasrashaH = thousands; yuuthapaaH = of troop-leaders; samupasthitaaH = who are resoled; kimkaraaH = to be the servants; hariraajasya = of Sugreeva; pR^iSThateH = behind; yam = whom; anugachchhanti = they are following; udyamya = carrying; vR^ikSaan = trees; laN^kaarohaNatatparaaH = with an intention to mount the city of Lanka; sahasaa = soon.

अनुवादः

“Hundreds and thousands of troop-leaders who are resolved to be the servants of Sugreeva rally behind him, carrying trees with them and with an intention to attack Lanka soon.”

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

2: He of dreadful deeds (ghorakarmaṇaḥ), upon whose long tail (dīrghalāṅgūlam) rest glossy hairs (snigdhāḥ) measuring many fathoms (bahuvyāmāḥ)—coppery, yellow, pale, and bright white, scattered all around—

3: —which, when held aloft (pragṛhītāḥ), shine like the rays of the sun (sūryasyeva marīcayaḥ) and trail along the ground (pṛthivyāṃ cānukṛṣyante)—this Vānara is named Hara.

4: Behind him (pṛṣṭhataḥ) follow by the hundreds and thousands, suddenly brandishing trees (vṛkṣān udyamya) and intent upon scaling Laṅkā (laṅkārohaṇatatparāḥ),—

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

2–4.

Rāma Varmā (Tilaka) cites Kataka confirming that these verses describe Hara (हरवर्णनमिति कतकः), noting that others refer them to Tāra (तारस्येत्यन्ये), and identifies Hara as belonging to the long-tailed golāṅgūla breed (हरो गोलाङ्गूलजातिः). Śivasahāya (Śiromaṇi) construes verses 2–4 as a single syntactic unit (सार्धश्लोकत्रयमेकान्वयि). Govindarāja explains that because Hara is Sugrīva’s servant, his subordinate captains too are rightly called हरिराजस्य किंकराः.

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Sāraṇa describes Dhūmra, overlord of the bears from Mount Ṛkṣavat, and his younger brother Jāmbavān, who aided Indra against the Asuras.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

नीलानिव महामेघांस्तिष्ठतो यांस्तु पश्यसि ॥ ५ ॥
असिताञ्जनसङ्काशान् युद्धे सत्यपराक्रमान् ।
असङ्ख्येयाननिर्देशान् परं पारमिवोदधेः ॥ ६ ॥
पर्वतेषु च ये केचिद् विषयेषु नदीषु च ।
एते त्वामभिवर्तन्ते राजन्नृक्षाः सुदारुणाः ॥ ७ ॥
एषां मध्ये स्थितो राजन् भीमाक्षो भीमदर्शनः ।
पर्जन्य इव जीमूतैः समन्तात् परिवारितः ॥ ८ ॥
ऋक्षवन्तं गिरिश्रेष्ठमध्यास्ते नर्मदां पिबन् ।
सर्वर्क्षाणामधिपतिर्धूम्रो नामैष यूथपः ॥ ९ ॥

असङ्ख्येयान निर्देशान् परं पारमिवोदधेः ॥ पर्वतेषु च ये केचिद् विषयेषु नदीषु च।
एते त्वामभिवर्तन्ते राजन्नक्षाः सुदारणाः ॥ एषां मध्ये स्थितो राजन् भीमाक्षो भीमदर्शनः ।
पर्जन्य इव जीमूतैः समन्तात् परिवारितः ॥ ऋक्षवन्तं गिरिश्रेष्ठ मध्यास्ते नर्मदां पिबन् ।
सर्वर्क्षाणामधिपतिर्धूम्रो नामैष यूथपः ॥

नीलान् इव महामेघांस् तिष्ठतो यांस् तु पश्यसि ॥ ६-२७-५ असितान् जन सम्काशान् युद्धे सत्य पराक्रमान् । असम्ख्येयान् अनिर्देश्यान् परम् पारम् इव उदधेः ॥ ६-२७-६ पर्वतेषु च ये केचिद् विषमेषु नदीषु च । एते त्वाम् अभिवर्तन्ते राजन्न् ऋष्काः सुदारुणाः ॥ ६-२७-७ एषाम् मध्ये स्थितो राजन् भीम अक्षो भीम दर्शनः । पर्जन्य इव जीमूतैः समन्तात् परिवारितः ॥ ६-२७-८ ऋक्षवन्तम् गिरि श्रेष्ठम् अध्यास्ते नर्मदाम् पिबन् । सर्व ऋक्षाणाम् अधिपतिर् धूम्रो नाम एष यूथपः ॥ ६-२७-९

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘उधर नील महामेघ और अञ्जनके समान काले रंगके जिन रीछोंको आप खड़े देख रहे हैं, वे युद्धमें सच्चा पराक्रम प्रकट करनेवाले हैं । समुद्रके दूसरे तटपर स्थित हुए बालुका-कणोंके समान इनकी गणना नहीं की जा सकती, इसीलिये पृथक्-पृथक् नाम लेकर इनके विषयमें कुछ बताना सम्भव नहीं है । ये सब पर्वतों, विभिन्न देशों और नदियोंके तटोंपर रहते हैं । राजन्! ये अत्यन्त भयंकर स्वभाववाले रीछ आपपर चढ़े आ रहे हैं । इनके बीचमें इनका राजा खड़ा है, जिसकी आँखें बड़ी भयानक और जो दूसरोंके देखनेमें भी बड़ा भयंकर जान पड़ता है । वह काले मेघोंसे घिरे हुए इन्द्रकी भाँति चारों ओरसे इन रीछोंद्वारा घिरा हुआ है । इसका नाम धूम्र है । यह समस्त रीछोंका राजा और यूथपति है । यह रीछराज धूम्र पर्वतश्रेष्ठ ऋक्षवान् पर रहता और नर्मदाका जल पीता है ॥ ५—९ ॥

अनुवादः

“Jambavan’s giant bodied troops, resembling demons and devils, having thick hair and endowed with unlimited energy, wander about, climbing mountain-heights and hurl massive rocks as big as huge clouds. They do not have a fear in facing death.”

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

13: Climbing up, they of vast frames (vipulākārāḥ) hurl down from the mountain peaks (parvatāgrebhyaḥ) boulders as huge as great clouds (mahābhravipulāḥ śilāḥ), and do not shrink back from death (na mṛtyor udvijanti).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

13–14.

Both Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa) and Rāma Varmā (Tilaka) take verses 13–14 as describing Jāmbavān’s soldiers (जाम्बवतः सैनिकान् वर्णयति). Govindarāja notes that climbing implies ascending mountain peaks (पर्वताग्राणीत्यर्थात्सिद्धम्) and glosses मुञ्चन्ति as dislodging boulders (च्यावयन्ति). Tilaka explains रोमशाः as thickly hirsute (अधिकरोमवन्तः) and attributes their resemblance to Rākṣasas and Piśācas to their ferocity (रक्षःपिशाचसादृश्यं क्रौर्येण).

“O, king! This Lord of monkeys- the commander of commanders on whom, whether he is in a hurried fury or leaping or standing still and all the monkeys stand looking - he is called Dambha who along with his bulky troops dwell on Sahasraksha mountain.”

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

15: Whom all the Vānaras, standing still, gaze upon—that commander of troop-commanders (yūthapayūthapam), fierce with rage (abhisaṃrabdham), who stands as though leaping (plavamānam avasthitam)—

16: —this lord of monkeys (harīśvaraḥ), O king, attends upon the thousand-eyed Indra (sahasrākṣam) with his host (balena); endowed with might (balasaṃyuktaḥ), this troop-commander is named Dambha.

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

15–16.

While Śivasahāya (Śiromaṇi) attaches verse 15 to Jāmbavān’s troops gazing at Jāmbavān (अर्धषट्कमेकान्वयि), Govindarāja and Tilaka pair 15–16 as introducing Dambha (द्वाभ्यां दम्भं वर्णयति). टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

On Saṃnādana (संनादनं वर्णयति), Govindarāja and Tilaka explain that his body is a full yojana in breadth and a yojana in height (योजनायतशरीरः, योजनोन्नतकायः), so that as he strides, a mountain a yojana distant brushes his side (एकयोजनपरिमितः पर्वतः पार्श्वस्थो भवति).

19.

Concluding the portrait of Saṃnādana, Śivasahāya (Śiromaṇi) construes शक्रस्य युद्धं दत्तम् as meaning that he fought against Indra himself and was never defeated (तेन सह योधितवानित्यर्थः). Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa), however, interprets शक्रस्य रणे as in Indra’s battle against the Asuras, explaining that Saṃnādana gave battle to the Asuras and suffered no defeat from them (येनासुरेभ्यो युद्धं दत्तं तदा पराजयश्चासुरेभ्यो न प्राप्तः).

20–22.

Introducing Krathana (क्रोधनः in Śiromaṇi), all three commentators gloss कृष्णवर्त्मना as by the fire-god Agni (अग्निना, वह्निना), who begot him during the Deva-Asura war to aid the gods (त्रिदिवौकसां साह्यार्थम्). Reading यस्य वैश्रवणो राजा in 21b, Govindarāja construes यस्य with the mountain—Kailāsa (तत्र गिरौ कैलासे in Tilaka)—of which Kubera is king, which possesses the celestial jambū tree (यो गिरिः जम्बूं जम्बूवृक्षम् उपनिषेवते), and which delights Rāvaṇa’s brother Kubera.

23–24.

Rāma Varmā (Tilaka) explains अकत्थनः as free from self-praise, demonstrating valor solely through deeds (आत्मश्लाघारहितः कार्येणैव पराक्रमप्रदर्शकः), while Śivasahāya glosses समवस्थितः as single-mindedly resolved (एकाग्रचित्तः).

45. hariH = this monkey; vikraantaH = is courageous; balavaan = stron; shuuraH = valiant; vyavasthitaH = established; sve = in his; paurSe = manliness; na kurute = and does not make; dayaam = pity for; praaNaanaam = his lives; raamapriyaartham = for the sake of Rama.

अनुवादः

“This Shatabali the monkey is courageous, strong, valiant and is established in his manliness. He does not care for his life, in the cause of Rama.”

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

He alone aspires (eṣaivāśaṃsate) to crush Laṅkā with his own army (svenānīkena)—valorous, mighty, heroic, and firmly established in his own manly prowess (pauruṣe sve vyavasthitaḥ).

Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy

पदच्छेदः

46. ekaikameva = every single one; gajaH = Gaja; gavakshaH = Gavaksha; gavayaH = Gavaya; nalaH = Nala; niilaH vaanarashcha = and a monkey called Nila; (each) vR^itaH = is surrounded; dasha koTibhiH = by ten crores; yodhaanaam = of warriors.

अनुवादः

“Each and every single one, like Gaja, Gavaya, Nala and a monkey called Nila is surrounded by crores of warriors.”

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

For the sake of what is dear to Rāma (rāmapriyārtham), this monkey shows no regard for his own life-breath (prāṇānāṃ dayāṃ na kurute). Gaja, Gavākṣa, Gavaya, Nala, and the Vānara Nīla—

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

46–48.

Śivasahāya observes that since Gaja and the other commanders do not spare their own lives for Rāma’s sake (रामप्रियार्थम्), any attempt to sow dissension among them is impossible (एतेन भेदानवकाशः सूचितः), and adds that the Vindhya monkeys are too numerous to be counted individually (बहुत्वाद्धेतोः संख्यातुं नैव शक्यन्ते).

इदे रीति विन्ध्यपर्वतदल्लि वासिसुव इन्नू अनेक शीघ्रपराक्रमि, श्रेष्ठ असङ्ख्य वानररू इद्दारॆ.॥47॥

पदच्छेदः

47. tathaa = besides; anye = other; vaanara shreSThaH = excellent monkeys; vindhya parvata vaasinaH = residing in vindhya mountain; laghuvikramaaH = and are impossible; samkhyaatum = to be counted; bahutvaat = because of their multitude.

अनुवादः

“Besides, other excellent monkeys residing in Vindhya mountain are quick-paced and are impossible to be counted because of their multitude.”

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

—each one alone is surrounded by ten crores of warriors (koṭibhir daśabhir vṛtaḥ). So too other foremost Vānaras dwelling on the Vindhya mountain (vindhyaparvatavāsinaḥ), swift in valor (laghuvikramāḥ), cannot be numbered on account of their multitude (bahutvāt).

48. mahaaraaja = O; monarch! Sarve = all of them; mahaa prabhavaaH = are highly prominent; sarve = all of them; mahaashailanikaashakaayaaH = are having their stature; equal to great mountains; sarve = all of them; samarthaaH = are capable; kshaNena = in a moment; kartum = to level; pR^ithiviim = the earth; pravidhvasta vikiirNa shailaam = by uprooting and razing to its mountains to the ground.

All of them, O great king, are of mighty power (mahāprabhāvāḥ); all have bodies resembling great mountains (mahāśailanikāśakāyāḥ); all are capable of rendering the earth in an instant (kṣaṇena) strewn with shattered and scattered mountains (pravidhvastavikīrṇaśailām).”
