<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. saH kapiH* = that Hanuma; *viiraH* = the courageous one;
*pavanaatmajaH* = the son of Lord Vayu; *dadarsha* = saw; *mahat vimaanam* = a
great aerial plane; *samsthitam* = standing; *madhye* = in the middle; *tasya
bhavanasya* = of that building; *maNivajrachitritam* = with a surprising hue due to
diamonds and gems; *prataptajaambuunadajaalakR^itrimam* = decorated by series of refined
gold.

**अनुवादः**

That Hanuma the courageous one, the son of Lord Vayu, saw the great aerial plane
standing in the middle of that building with a surprising hue, due to diamonds, and gems decorated
by series of refined gold.
</details>


<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Standing in the middle of that palace (_tasya madhye bhavanasya saṃsthitaḥ_), that wise monkey, the son of the Wind-god, beheld the great aerial car (_mahad vimānaṃ dadarśa_) variegated with gems and jewels (_maṇiratnacitritam_) and artfully wrought with lattices of burnished _jāmbūnada_ gold (_prataptajāmbūnadajālakṛtrimam_).
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

आ पुष्पकविमानवु अप्रमेयवाद सौन्दर्यदिन्द कूडिरुव प्रतिमॆयन्तॆ निर्मितवागित्तु. लोकवे मॆच्चुकॊळ्ळुवन्तह ऊहातीतवाद, साटियिल्लद आ पुष्पकविमानवन्नु स्वतः विश्वकर्मने निर्मिसिद्दनु. अदु आकाशक्कॆ होगुव वायुमार्गदल्लि प्रतिष्ठितवागित्तु. (नॆलबिट्टु अन्तराळदल्लित्तु) सूर्यनमार्गक्कॆ तोरुगल्लिनन्तॆ काणुत्तिद्द आ दिव्यविमानवन्नु हनुमन्तनु नोडिदनु.॥2॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. tat* = that plane; *vyaraajata* = shone; *lakshmavat*
= like symbol; *aadityapathasya* = for solar path; *vaayupathapratishhThatam* =
standing in the aerial path; *divamgatam* = obtaining in the sky; *kR^itam* =
manufactured; *svyam vishvakarmaNaa* = by Vishvakarma himself; *saadhviti* = and
praised by him; *aprameyaapratikaarakR^itrimam* = one without comparison in beauty.

**अनुवादः**

That plane shone like symbol for solar path standing in the aerial path obtaining
the sky. Manufactured by Vishvakarma himself and praised by him as one without comparison in
beauty.
</details>


<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Wrought with immeasurable and peerless counter-images (_aprameyapratikārakṛtrimam_), and fashioned by Viśvakarman himself with the acclaim, “This is truly exquisite!” (_kṛtaṃ svayaṃ sādhv iti viśvakarmaṇā_), that aerial car, stationed in the sky-reaching path of the wind (_divaṃ gate vāyupathe pratiṣṭhitam_), shone forth as the very emblem of the path of the sun (_vyarājatādityapathasya lakṣma tat_).
</details>
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. tatra* = In that plane; *na kimchit na kR^itam* = there is
not even a small thing that is not made; *prayatnataH* = with effort; *tatra* = in
that; *na kimchit* = there is nothing; *na mahaarharatnavat* = without the best
diamonds; *te visheshhaaH* = those characteristics; *naniyataaH* = definitely are
not; *sureshhvapi* = in Devas also; *tatra* = in that; *kimchit na* = there is
nothing; *na mahaavisheshhavat* = that is not great significance.

**अनुवादः**

In that plane there is not even a small thing that is not made with great effort, in
that there is nothing without the best diamonds, those characteristics definitely are not in Devas
also, in that there is nothing that is not of great significance.
</details>


<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

There was nothing therein that was not fashioned with utmost care (_na tatra kiṃcin na kṛtaṃ prayatnataḥ_); there was nothing therein that was not set with priceless gems (_na mahārharatnavat_); those special excellences were not found even among the gods (_na te viśeṣā niyatāḥ sureṣv api_); there was nothing therein that did not possess some supreme distinction (_na mahāviśeṣavat_).
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. tapHsamaadhaanaparaakramaarjitam* = one that has been obtained by
austerities and by prowess; *manaHsamaadhaanavichaarachaariNam* = one that moves about by
the thoughts of concentrated mind; *anekasamsthaanavisheshhanirmitam* = made from various
significant parts; *tulyavisheshhanadarshanam* = with an appearance of parts of equal
significance; *tataH tataH* = (collected from) here and there

**अनुवादः**

One that has been obtained by austerities and by prowess, one that moves about by
thoughts of concentrated mind, made from various significant parts with an appearance of parts of
equal significance, collected from here and there from all over the world.
</details>


<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Won through austerity, meditative concentration, and valor (_tapaḥsamādhānaparākramārjitam_), coursing in accordance with the focused thought of the mind (_manaḥsamādhānavicāracāriṇam_), fashioned with manifold architectural distinctions (_anekasaṃsthānaviśeṣanirmitam_), and constructed throughout with harmonious features (_tatas tatas tulyaviśeṣanirmitam_)—

<details><summary>टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

**5.8.1–2 (Viśvakarman's Masterpiece and the Wind-Path)**

$18

_____________

**5.8.3 (Fourfold Emphatic Litotes)**

Through four double negatives (न तत्र किंचिन्न...), the poet emphasizes that every single part of Puṣpaka is painstakingly crafted, studded with priceless gems, and endowed with artistic excellences (रचनाविशेषाः) unmatched even in the aerial cars of the gods (सुरविमानेष्वपि न).

_____________

**5.8.4 (Won by Austerity and Guided by Thought)**

Construing verses 4–8 as a five-stanza syntactic period (पञ्चकं कुलकम्) governed by ददर्श in verse 8, the commentators note that Puṣpaka was first won by Kubera through asceticism and mental absorption and then by Rāvaṇa through valor (तपस्समाधानेन पराक्रमेण च अर्जितम्). On the masculine accusative मनःसमाधानविचारचारिणम् qualifying विमानम्, **Tilaka** and **Śiromaṇi** explain that विमान belongs to the _ardharcādi_ class of nouns admitting both masculine and neuter gender (विमानशब्दोऽर्धर्चादिर्वा).

_____________

**5.8.5 (Prosodic Shortening vs. Possessive Suffix in Maharddhinām)**

Moving as soon as its master focuses his mind—because its presiding deity is sentient (तदभिमानिदेवतायाश्चित्त्वरूपत्वात्)—Puṣpaka is like the celestial abode of Indra and the blessed (अग्र्यमुदाम् इन्द्रादीनाम्). On the short _i_ in महर्द्धिनाम्, **Tilaka** calls the absence of lengthening Vedic/epic (दीर्घाभावश्छान्दसः), and **Govindarāja** quotes the prosodic maxim permitting short for long to avoid breaking the meter (अपि माषं मषं कुर्याच्छन्दोभङ्गं न कारयेत्); **Śiromaṇi**, however, defends it as regular Pāṇinian grammar by deriving it as an _in_-stem (महर्द्धिनः) rather than an _i_-stem (महर्द्धिरस्ति येषु तेषाम् एतेन दीर्घाभाव आर्ष इति भट्टोक्तिश्चिन्त्या).

_____________

**5.8.6 (Distinguishing the Repeated Vicitrakūṭam)**

On the threefold play on कूट in verse 6, **Tilaka** glosses the first विचित्रकूटं as `a repository of wondrous objects' (आश्चर्यवस्तूनां कूटं समवायभूतम्), बहुकूटमण्डितं as `adorned with many halls' (बहुभिः कूटैः शालाभिर्मण्डितम्), and the second विचित्रकूटं as `having variegated subsidiary crags like a mountain peak' (विचित्रावान्तरशृङ्गवच्छिखरमिव), while **Govindarāja** distinguishes minor peaks (कूटः अवान्तरशृङ्गं) from the main summit (शिखरं महाशृङ्गम्).

_____________

**5.8.7–8 (The Aerial Bhūtagaṇa Bearers and Vernal Beauty)**

Thousands of sky-coursing _bhūtagaṇas_ with rolling, unblinking eyes (विवृत्तानि आघूर्णितानि विध्वस्तानि निमेषरहितानि) bear the Puṣpaka car, which is even lovelier than the month of spring itself (वसन्तमासादपि चारुदर्शनम्).
