Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro

Book I : Bala Kanda - The Youthful Majesties chapter/Sarga 42

Bhageeratha’s effort for bringing Ganga to earth is fulfilled. Amshuman and his son Dileepa could not make any effort to bring the divine river to earth. But Bhageeratha, the son of Dileepa, staunch at heart tries earnestly to get her onto earth. Brahma agreeing for this descent of Ganga designates lord Shiva to bear the burden of the onrush of Ganga, because the earth cannot sustain it.

भागसूचना
  1. अंशुमान् और भगीरथकी तपस्या, ब्रह्माजीका भगीरथको अभीष्ट वर देकर गंगाजीको धारण करनेके लिये भगवान् शङ्करको राजी करनेके निमित्त प्रयत्न करनेकी सलाह देना
सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Upon Sagara’s passing, the ministers and subjects enthrone the righteous Aṃśumān, who later entrusts the kingdom to his son Dilīpa and practices severe austerity on a peak of Himavān, while Dilīpa too is haunted by his grand-uncles’ unredeemed fate.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

कालधर्मं गते राम सगरे प्रकृतीजनाः ।
राजानं रोचयामासुरंशुमन्तं सुधार्मिकम् ॥ १ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

कालधर्मं गते राम सगरे प्रकृतीजनाः ।
राजानं रोचयामासुरंशुमन्तं सुधार्मिकम् ॥ १ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

श्रीराम! सगरकी मृत्यु हो जानेपर प्रजाजनोंने परम धर्मात्मा अंशुमान् को राजा बनानेकी रुचि प्रकट की ॥ १ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

श्रीरामा! सगरन मृत्युवादाग प्रजाजनरु अमात्यरु परमधार्मिक अंशुमन्तनन्नु राजनन्नागिसलु तम्म इच्छॆयन्नु प्रकटिसिदरु.॥1॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

1. raama = oh Rama; sagare kaaladharmam gate = gone - on passing away by Time’s virtue of Sagara; prakR^itiijanaaH = ministers and subjects of that kingdom; sudhaarmikam amshumantam = highly honourable one, Amshuman is; raajaanam rocayaamaasuH = predisposed to - and enthroned as king.

अनुवादः

“When King Sagara passed away owing to the irrefutable virtue of Time, the ministers and subjects of that kingdom are predisposed towards the highly honourable Amshuman to become their king and they enthroned him accordingly.” Thus Vishvamitra continued narration about the predecessors of Rama. [1-42-1]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“When Sagara had succumbed to the law of Time (kāladharmaṃ gate rāma sagare), O Rāma, the ministers and subjects chose the supremely righteous Aṃśumān as their king (prakṛtījanāḥ rājānaṃ rocayām āsur aṃśumantaṃ sudhārmikam).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

42.1–2 (kāladharmam & prakṛtījanāḥ)

Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa) defines kāladharma as death—the inevitable law of Time awaiting every born creature (jātamātrasya kālenāvaśyaṃ prāptavyo dharmaḥ). On the lengthened ī in prakṛtījanāḥ (‘ministers and subjects’, citing Vaijayantī: prakṛtiḥ sahaje yonāv amātye paramātmani), Tilaka and Govindarāja call it an archaic license (dīrghaś chāndasaḥ), whereas Śiromaṇi justifies it by Pāṇini 6.3.65 (kṛdikārād aktinaḥ) because prakṛti functions here as a conventional noun (saṃjñā).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

स राजा सुमहानासीदंशुमान् रघुनन्दन ।
तस्य पुत्रो महानासीद् दिलीप इति विश्रुतः ॥ २ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

स राजा सुमहानासीदंशुमान् रघुनन्दन ।
तस्य पुत्रो महानासीद् दिलीप इति विश्रुतः ॥ २ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

रघुनन्दन! अंशुमान् बड़े प्रतापी राजा हुए । उनके पुत्रका नाम दिलीप था । वह भी एक महान् पुरुष था ॥ २ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

रघुनन्दन! अंशुमन्तनु महा प्रतापि राजनादनु. अवन पुत्र दिलीपनू ऒब्ब महा पुरुषनागिद्दनु.॥2॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

2. raghunandana = oh, Raghu’s delight; saH amshumaan = he, that Amshuman; sumahaan raajaa aasiit = he was a very exceptional king; tasya = to him; diliipa iti vishrutaH = renowned as Dileepa; mahaan putraH aasiit = has become - took birth as marvellous son.

अनुवादः

“He that Amshuman turned out to be a very great king, and oh, Rama of Raghu’s delight, he begot a marvellous son who is renowned as Dileepa. [1-42-2]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“That Aṃśumān was a very great king, O delight of the Raghus (sa rājā sumahān āsīd aṃśumān raghunandana); and he had a great son renowned by the name of Dilīpa (tasya putro mahān āsīd dilīpa iti viśrutaḥ).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

तस्मै राज्यं समादिश्य दिलीपे रघुनन्दन ।
हिमवच्छिखरे रम्ये तपस्तेपे सुदारुणम् ॥ ३ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

तस्मै राज्यं समादिश्य दिलीपे रघुनन्दन ।
हिमवच्छिखरे रम्ये तपस्तेपे सुदारुणम् ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

रघुकुलको आनन्दित करनेवाले वीर! अंशुमान् दिलीपको राज्य देकर हिमालयके रमणीय शिखरपर चले गये और वहाँ अत्यन्त कठोर तपस्या करने लगे ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

रघुकुलनन्दन! अंशुमन्तनु दिलीपनिगॆ राज्यवन्नु ऒप्पिसि, हिमालयद रमणिय शिखरक्कॆ होगि अत्यन्त कठोर तपस्सु माडतॊडगिदनु.॥3॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

3. raghunandana = oh Rama, Raghu’s descendent; tasmai diliipe = in him, to Dileepa; raajyam samaadishya = completely ordering - assigning kingdom ; ramye himavat shikhare = pleasant, peak of Himalayas; sudaaruNam tapaH tepe = [Amshuman] undertook very stern ascesis.

अनुवादः

Assigning the kingdom to Dileepa, oh, Rama of Raghu’s dynasty, Amshuman undertook very stern ascesis on a pleasant peak of Himalayas desiring the descent of River Ganga to earth. [1-42-3]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Having made over the kingdom to that Dilīpa (tasmai rājyaṃ samādiśya dilīpe), O delight of the Raghus, Aṃśumān practiced most severe austerity upon a lovely peak of Himavān (himavacchikhare ramye tapas tepe sudāruṇam).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

42.3–4 (himavacchikhare & svargaṃ lebhe)

Why did Aṃśumān abdicate in favor of Dilīpa (tasmai dilīpe = dilīpāya, Tilaka) and perform severe penance on Himavān? All three commentators supply the purpose: to bring Gaṅgā down to earth for his uncles’ salvation (pitṛgataye gaṅgāyā bhūlokāvatāraṇāya, Tilaka). Yet as Govindarāja and Śiromaṇi emphasize, despite thirty-two thousand years of penance he attained only heaven, not Gaṅgā (svargaṃ lebhe na tu gaṅgām).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

द्वात्रिंशच्छतसाहस्रं वर्षाणि सुमहायशाः ।
तपोवनगतो राजा स्वर्गं लेभे तपोधनः ॥ ४ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

द्वात्रिंशच्छतसाहस्रं वर्षाणि सुमहायशाः ।
तपोवनगतो राजा स्वर्गं लेभे तपोधनः ॥ ४ ॥

मूलम् - VR

द्वात्रिंशच्च सहस्राणि वर्षाणि सुमहायशाः । तपोवनगतो राजा स्वर्गं लेभे तपोधनः ॥ १-४२-४

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

महान् यशस्वी राजा अंशुमान‍्ने उस तपोवनमें जाकर बत्तीस हजार वर्षोंतक तप किया । तपस्याके धनसे सम्पन्न हुए उस नरेशने वहीं शरीर त्यागकर स्वर्गलोक प्राप्त किया ॥ ४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महान् यशस्वी राजा अंशुमन्तनु आ तपोवनदल्लि मूवत्तॆरडु साविर वर्ष तपस्सु माडिदनु. तपोधन सम्पन्ननाद आ नरेशनु अल्ले शरीरवन्नु त्यजिसि स्वर्गलोकवन्नु पडॆदनु.॥4॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

4. sumahaayashaaH = of very great renown; raajaa = king - Amshuman; dvaatrimshat ca sahasraaNi varSaaNi = thirty-two thousand years; tapovanagataH = on going to ascetic woods - practising ascesis; tapodhanaH = ascesis as asset - one whose wealth is practising ascesis, but not its reward; svargam lebhe = achieved heaven.

अनुवादः

On practising ascesis in ascetic-woods for thirty-two thousand years that highly renowned king Amshuman achieved heaven as he acquired only the wealth of practising the ascesis. [1-42-4]

टिप्पनी

Instead of achieving reward of ascesis in the form of descent of River Ganga, he could achieve only his personal merit of his penance, namely an abode in heaven.

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Dwelling in the ascetic forest for thirty-two hundred thousand years (dvātriṃśacchatasāhasraṃ varṣāṇi tapovanagataḥ), that king of supreme renown, rich in austerity, attained heaven (sumahāyaśāḥ rājā svargaṃ lebhe tapodhanaḥ).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

दिलीपस्तु महातेजाः श्रुत्वा पैतामहं वधम् ।
दुःखोपहतया बुद्ध्या निश्चयं नाध्यगच्छत ॥ ५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

दिलीपस्तु महातेजाः श्रुत्वा पैतामहं वधम् ।
दुःखोपहतया बुद्ध्या निश्चयं नाध्यगच्छत ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

अपने पितामहोंके वधका वृत्तान्त सुनकर महातेजस्वी दिलीप भी बहुत दुःखी रहते थे । अपनी बुद्धिसे बहुत सोचने-विचारनेके बाद भी वे किसी निश्चयपर नहीं पहुँच सके ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

तन्न पितामहर वधॆय वृत्तान्तवन्नु केळि महा तेजस्वी दिलीपनू बहळ दुःखितनागिद्दनु. तन्न बुद्धियिन्द बहळ विचार माडिद बळिकवू अवनिन्द यावुदे तीर्मानक्कॆ बरलागलिल्ल.॥5॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

5. mahaatejaaH diliipaH tu = great resplendent, Dileepa, on his part; paitaamaham vadham shrutvaa = on hearing grandfather’s [sixty thousand sons of Sagara] elimination; duhkhopahatayaa buddhyaa = with a mind marred by agony - at the plight of his father Amshuman; nishcayam na adhyagacChata = not arrived at decision.

अनुवादः

The great resplendent Dileepa on hearing the elimination of his grandfathers, the sixty thousand sons of Sagara at the hand of sage Kapila, and with a mind that is marred by the plight of his father Amshuman in absolving the souls of Sagara’s sons, he that Dileepa could not arrive at any decision concerning the descent of Ganga. [1-42-5]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Dilīpa of great splendor, however, having heard of the slaughter of his grand-uncles (dilīpas tu mahātejāḥ śrutvā paitāmahaṃ vadham), could not arrive at any definite means with his mind overwhelmed by sorrow (duḥkhopahatayā buddhyā niścayaṃ nādhyagacchata).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

42.5 (paitāmahaṃ vadham & duḥkhopahatayā)

Tilaka and Govindarāja gloss vadham as untimely death (apamṛtyum). Why was Dilīpa’s intellect paralyzed by grief? Govindarāja explains: he grieved that even after his father Aṃśumān’s grueling austerity, his grand-uncles remained unredeemed (pitrā duścare tapasi kṛte ’pi teṣāṃ gatir nābhūt).

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Ever pondering how to bring down Gaṅgā and redeem his ancestors, the righteous King Dilīpa begets the noble Bhagīratha, offers many sacrifices during a thirty-thousand-year reign, and ascends to Indra’s heaven after consecrating his son.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

कथं गङ्गावतरणं कथं तेषां जलक्रिया ।
तारयेयं कथं चैतानिति चिन्तापरोऽभवत् ॥ ६ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

कथं गङ्गावतरणं कथं तेषां जलक्रिया ।
तारयेयं कथं चैतानिति चिन्तापरोऽभवत् ॥ ६ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

वे सदा इसी चिन्तामें डूबे रहते थे कि किस प्रकार पृथ्वीपर गंगाजीका उतरना सम्भव होगा? कैसे गंगाजलद्वारा उन्हें जलाञ्जलि दी जायेगी और किस प्रकार मैं अपने उन पितरोंका उद्धार कर सकूँगा ॥ ६ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

पृथ्विगॆ गङ्गॆयन्नु हेगॆ तरुवुदु? गङ्गाजलदिन्द अवरिगॆ हेगॆ तर्पण कॊडुवुदु? याव विधदिन्द आ पितृगळन्नु नानु उद्धरिसबल्लॆनु? ऎम्ब चिन्तॆयल्ले सदा अवनु मुळुगुत्तिद्दनु.॥6॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

6. gangaavataraNam katham = how - to make alighting of Ganga possible; teSaam jalakriyaa = water oblation to them [grandfathers, offering] ; katham = how - to offer; etaan katham taarayeyam ca = how to cross over them [them from this mortal bindings of heaps of ashes,] also; iti = this way; cintaaparaH = given to worry; abhavat = he [Dileepa] became.

अनुवादः

Dileepa became worried as to how River Ganga is to be alighted onto earth from heaven, how water-oblations are to be offered for the souls of Sagara’s sons, and how to cross them, the souls, over this mortal world. [1-42-6]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“‘How can the descent of Gaṅgā be brought about (kathaṃ gaṅgāvataraṇam)? How can their obsequial water-rite be performed (kathaṃ teṣāṃ jalakriyā)? And how may I deliver them (tārayeyaṃ kathaṃ caitān)?’—thus he remained ever absorbed in anxious thought (iti cintāparo ’bhavat).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

42.6 (kathaṃ gaṅgāvataraṇam)

Both Tilaka and Govindarāja note that v. 6 dramatizes (abhinayati) Dilīpa’s inner anguish. Śiromaṇi traces the three questions as a strict causal chain: how can Gaṅgā be brought down? Without her descent, how can the funeral libation (jalakriyā) be offered to ancestors who died an untimely death? And since no other water can save them (jalāntareṇa eṣāṃ taraṇaṃ na bhaviṣyati), how can I deliver them?

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

तस्य चिन्तयतो नित्यं धर्मेण विदितात्मनः ।
पुत्रो भगीरथो नाम जज्ञे परमधार्मिकः ॥ ७ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

तस्य चिन्तयतो नित्यं धर्मेण विदितात्मनः ।
पुत्रो भगीरथो नाम जज्ञे परमधार्मिकः ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

प्रतिदिन इन्हीं सब चिन्ताओंमें पड़े हुए राजा दिलीपको, जो अपने धर्माचरणसे बहुत विख्यात थे, भगीरथ नामक एक परम धर्मात्मा पुत्र प्राप्त हुआ ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

प्रतिदिन इदे चिन्तॆयल्लि मुळुगिद्द दिलीपराजनिगॆ तन्न धर्माचरणॆयिन्द विख्यातनाद भगीरथनॆम्ब ऒब्ब परमधार्मिक पुत्रनु हुट्टिदनु.॥7॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

7. viditaatmanaH = knower of soul [self-mortified one]; dharmeNa = righteously; nityam cintayataH = always, who is thinking [about alighting of Ganga to earth]; tasya = to him; bhagiirathaH naama = Bhageeratha, known as; paramadhaarmikaH putraH jaGYe = most virtuous, son, is born.

अनुवादः

To him who is self-mortified and who is always thinking righteously about the alight of Ganga onto earth, to such a Dileepa a most-virtuous son is born who is renowned by his name Bhageeratha. [1-42-7]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“To him who was constantly pondering thus and whose very soul was renowned through dharma (tasya cintayato nityaṃ dharmeṇa viditātmanaḥ), there was born a supremely righteous son named Bhagīratha (putro bhagīratho nāma jajñe paramadhārmikaḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

42.7–8 (dharmeṇa viditātmanaḥ & rājyam akārayat)

Tilaka glosses dharmeṇa viditātmanaḥ as ‘renowned through righteousness’ (dharmeṇa prasiddhaḥ), while Govindarāja construes dharmeṇa as the motivating cause of his constant reflection (dharmeṇa prayojakena cintayataḥ). On rājyam akārayat, all three commentators note the non-causative ṇic (svārthe ṇic, equivalent to akarot).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

दिलीपस्तु महातेजा यज्ञैर्बहुभिरिष्टवान् ।
त्रिंशद्वर्षसहस्राणि राजा राज्यमकारयत् ॥ ८ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

गङ्गायाः पतनं राजन् पृथिवी न सहिष्यते ।
तां वै धारयितुं राजन् नान्यं पश्यामि शूलिनः ॥ २४ ॥

गङ्गायाः पतनं राजन् पृथिवी न सहिष्यती ।
तां वै धारयितुं राजन् नान्यं पश्यामि शूलिनः ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘महाराज! गंगाजीके गिरनेका वेग यह पृथ्वी नहीं सह सकेगी । मैं त्रिशूलधारी भगवान् शङ्करके सिवा और किसीको ऐसा नहीं देखता, जो इन्हें धारण कर सके’ ॥ २४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महाराजा! गङ्गॆयु बीळुव वेग ई पृथ्वियु सहिसलारळु. त्रिशूलधारी भगवान् शङ्करनल्लदॆ बेरॆ यारू ईकॆयन्नु धरिसबल्लवरन्नु नानु नोडिल्ल.॥24॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

24. raajan = oh king; gangaayaaH patanam = Ganga’s, downfall; pR^ithivii na sahiSyate = earth, can not, endure; taam dhaarayitum = to sustain her [Ganga] ; raajan oh king; shuulinaH = Trident wielder - god Shiva; anyam = any other - other than him; na pashyaami vai = not, I behold, indeed.

अनुवादः

‘Oh, king Bhageeratha, the earth cannot endure the downfall of Ganga and to sustain Ganga, oh, king, indeed I do not behold none other than the Trident-wielder, god Shiva.’ Thus Brahma spoke to Bhageeratha. [1-42-24]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“‘The earth will not be able to endure the fall of Gaṅgā, O King (gaṅgāyāḥ patanaṃ rājan pṛthivī na sahiṣyate); to sustain her, O King, I behold no one other than the trident-bearing Śiva (tāṃ vai dhārayituṃ rājan nānyaṃ paśyāmi śūlinaḥ)!’

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

तमेवमुक्त्वा राजानं गङ्गां चाभाष्य लोककृत् ।
जगाम त्रिदिवं देवैः सर्वैः सह मरुद्‍गणैः ॥ २५ ॥

मूलम् - VR

तमेवमुक्त्वा राजानं गङ्गां चाभाष्य लोककृत् । जगाम त्रिदिवं देवैः सह सर्वैर्मरुद्गणैः ॥ १-४२-२५

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

राजासे ऐसा कहकर लोकस्रष्टा ब्रह्माजीने भगवती गंगासे भी भगीरथपर अनुग्रह करनेके लिये कहा । इसके बाद वे सम्पूर्ण देवताओं तथा मरुद्‍गणोंके साथ स्वर्गलोकको चले गये ॥ २५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

राजनल्लि हीगॆ हेळि लोकस्रष्टा ब्रह्मदेवरु भगवती गङ्गॆगू भगीरथन मेलॆ अनुग्रह माडुवन्तॆ तिळिसिदरु. बळिक समस्त देवतॆगळु हागू मरुद्गणरॊन्दिगॆ तम्म लोकक्कॆ तॆरळिदरु.॥25॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

25. lokakR^it = creator of worlds - Brahma; tam raajaanam = to him, to king; evam uktvaa = this way, on speaking; * gangaam ca aabhaaSya* = on speaking - having a little talk, saying hello to Ganga, also ; saha sarvaiH devaiH marut gaNaiH = with, all, gods, with groups of Wind-gods; tridivam jagaama = proceeded to heaven.

अनुवादः

“Speaking this way to the king Bhageeratha and informally greeting Ganga also, that Creator of Worlds, Brahma, left for heaven along with all the groups of gods and Wind-gods.” Thus Vishvamitra continued narration about the arrival of River Ganga to earth. [1-42-25]

समाप्तिः

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे द्विचत्वारिंशः सर्गः ॥ ४२ ॥
इस प्रकार श्रीवाल्मीकिनिर्मित आर्षरामायण आदिकाव्यके बालकाण्डमें बयालीसवाँ सर्ग पूरा हुआ ॥ ४२ ॥

अनुवाद (समाप्तिः) (कन्नड)

वाल्मीकि विरचित आर्ष रामायण आदिकाव्यद बालकाण्डदल्लि नलवत्तॆरडनॆय सर्ग पूर्णवायितु.॥42॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Having spoken thus to that king and having addressed Gaṅgā as well, the Creator of the worlds (tam evam uktvā rājānaṃ gaṅgāṃ cābhāṣya lokakṛt) departed to the third heaven accompanied by all the gods and the hosts of Maruts (jagāma tridivaṃ devaiḥ sarvaiḥ saha marudgaṇaiḥ).”

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

42.25 & Colophon (gaṅgāṃ cābhāṣya)

What did Brahmā say when addressing Gaṅgā (gaṅgāṃ cābhāṣya) before ascending to heaven? Both Tilaka and Govindarāja supply his paternal directive: “My child, bestow your grace upon the king at the proper time!” (vatse rājānaṃ yathākālam anugṛhāṇety uktvā).
