Book I : Bala Kanda - The Youthful Majesties chapter / sarga 38

Sage Vishvamitra narrates the legend of Sagara, the earliest King of Ayodhya and Rama’s forefather, who is childless, but who begets sixty thousand sons of merit and one son of demerit. Emperor Sagara is the one who brought celestial Ganga onto the surface of earth..

भागसूचना
  1. राजा सगरके पुत्रोंकी उत्पत्ति तथा यज्ञकी तैयारी
सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Having concluded the celestial account of Kārttikeya, Viśvāmitra begins the terrestrial history of Gaṅgā’s descent by recounting how childless King Sagara of Ayodhyā and his two queens Keśinī and Sumati performed austere penance on Mount Bhṛguprasravaṇa.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

तां कथां कौशिको रामे निवेद्य मधुराक्षराम् ।
पुनरेवापरं वाक्यं काकुत्स्थमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ १ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

तां कथां कौशिको रामे निवेद्य मधुराक्षराम् ।
पुनरेवापरं वाक्यं काकुत्स्थमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ १ ॥

मूलम् - VR

तां कथां कौशिको रामे निवेद्य मधुराक्षरम् । पुन रेवापरं वाक्यं काकुत्स्थमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ १-३८-१

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

विश्वामित्रजीने मधुर अक्षरोंसे युक्त वह कथा श्रीरामको सुनाकर फिर उनसे दूसरा प्रसंग इस प्रकार कहा— ॥ १ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

विश्वामित्ररु मधुराक्षरगळिन्द कूडिद कार्तिकेय वृत्तान्तद कथॆयन्नु श्रीरामनिगॆ तिळिसि मत्तॆ इन्नॊन्दु प्रसङ्गवन्नु ई प्रकार हेळिदरु.॥1॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

1. kaushikaH = descendent of Kaushika, Vishvamitra; taam kathaam = that, legend of Kaarthikeya; raame nivedya = on submitting - describing to Rama; punaH eva = again, thus - further; idam aparam vaakyam = this, best, sentence; madhuraakSaram = in melodious words; kaakutstham abraviit = spoke to Rama of Kakutstha.

अनुवादः

Describing that legend of Kaartikeya to Rama, the descendent of Kaushi, namely sage Vishvamitra has further spoken this best sentence worded melodiously to Rama, the descendent of Kakutstha. [1-38-1]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Having related to Rāma that narrative of sweet syllables (tāṃ kathāṃ madhurākṣarām), Kauśika (Viśvāmitra) once again spoke this further discourse (punar evāparaṃ vākyam) to Kākutstha:

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

38.1 (aparaṃ vākyam — Resuming the Question of Gaṅgā’s Tripathagā Course)

Rāma Varmā (Tilaka), Śiromaṇi, and Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa) observe that having completed the celestial narrative (divyasaṃbhavaṃ vistaraṃ pratipādya) of Umā, Gaṅgā, and Skanda’s birth, Viśvāmitra now initiates the terrestrial history (mānuṣalokasaṃbhavavistaram, Govindarāja) to answer Rāma’s original question from Sarga 35 (tripatho hetunā kena: how Gaṅgā came to flow through the three worlds, Tilaka).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अयोध्याधिपतिर्वीर पूर्वमासीन्नराधिपः ।
सगरो नाम धर्मात्मा प्रजाकामः स चाप्रजः ॥ २ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

अयोध्याधिपतिर्वीर पूर्वमासीन्नराधिपः ।
सगरो नाम धर्मात्मा प्रजाकामः स चाप्रजः ॥ २ ॥

मूलम् - VR

अयोध्याधिपतिर्वीरः पूर्वमासीन्नराधिपः । सगरो नाम धर्मात्मा प्रजाकामः स चाप्रजः ॥ १-३८-२

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘वीर! पहलेकी बात है, अयोध्यामें सगर नामसे प्रसिद्ध एक धर्मात्मा राजा राज्य करते थे । उन्हें कोई पुत्र नहीं था; अतः वे पुत्र-प्राप्तिके लिये सदा उत्सुक रहा करते थे ॥ २ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

वीरने! बहळ हिन्दिन मातु, अयोध्यॆयल्लि सगर ऎम्ब प्रसिद्ध, धर्मात्मा राजनु राज्यवाळुत्तिद्दनु. अवनिगॆ यावुदे सन्तान इरदॆ, अवनु पुत्रप्राप्तिगागि सदा उत्सुकनागिद्दनु.॥2॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

2. puurvam = once upon a time; ayodhyaadhipatiH = Ayodhya’s king; viiraH dharmaatmaa = valiant one, virtue-souled one; sagaraH naama = renowned as Sagara; naraadhipaH aasiit = people’s king, was there; saH = he was; aprajaH = without offspring; prajaakaamaH ca = longing for offspring, also.

अनुवादः

“Once there was a valiant and virtue-souled king of Ayodhya named Sagara, who is without offspring, as such he was longing for offspring. [1-38-2]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“In days of yore, O hero (vīra), there was a righteous-souled monarch, a lord of men and sovereign of Ayodhyā (ayodhyādhipatiḥ), named Sagara (sagaro nāma dharmātmā); though longing for progeny (prajākāmaḥ), he was childless (sa cāprajaḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

38.2–4 (sagaro nāma & ariṣṭanemer duhitā)

On v. 2, Tilaka and Govindarāja construe aprajaḥ as the cause of prajākāmaḥ (“because he was childless, he longed for offspring”). On v. 4, all three commentators identify Ariṣṭanemi as a title of Prajāpati Kaśyapa (ariṣṭanemiḥ kaśyapaḥ), confirmed by the epithet suparṇabhaginī (“sister of Garuḍa,” since Suparṇa was born to Kaśyapa by Vinatā, Tilaka).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

वैदर्भदुहिता राम केशिनी नाम नामतः ।
ज्येष्ठा सगरपत्नी सा धर्मिष्ठा सत्यवादिनी ॥ ३ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

वैदर्भदुहिता राम केशिनी नाम नामतः ।
ज्येष्ठा सगरपत्नी सा धर्मिष्ठा सत्यवादिनी ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘श्रीराम! विदर्भराजकुमारी केशिनी राजा सगरकी ज्येष्ठ पत्नी थी । वह बड़ी धर्मात्मा और सत्यवादिनी थी ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

श्रीरामा! विदर्भराजकुमारि केशिनी ऎम्बुवळु सगरन ज्येष्ठ पत्नियागिद्दळु. अवळु धर्मात्मळू, सत्यवादिनियू आगिद्दळु.॥3॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

  1. raama = oh Rama; vaidarbhaduhitaa = daughter of Vaidarbha [king of] ; naamataH keshinii naama = by name renowned as Keshini; jyeSThaa sagarapatnii = elder, Sagara’s wife; saa dharmiSThaa satyavaadinii = she is, virtue minded and advocate of truth.

अनुवादः

“The daughter of King of Vidarbha, renowned by name Keshini, is the eldest wife of Sagara, and she was virtue-minded, and an advocate of truth. [1-38-3]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“The daughter of the King of Vidarbha (vaidarbhaduhitā), O Rāma, known by the name of Keśinī (keśinī nāma nāmataḥ), was Sagara’s elder queen (jyeṣṭhā sagarapatnī)—most devoted to dharma and ever truthful in speech (dharmiṣṭhā satyavādinī).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अरिष्टनेमेर्दुहिता सुपर्णभगिनी तु सा ।
द्वितीया सगरस्यासीत् पत्नी सुमतिसञ्ज्ञिता ॥ ४ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

1 अरिष्टनेमेर्दुहिता सुपर्णभगिनी तु सा ।
द्वितीया सगरस्यासीत् पत्नी सुमतिसञ्ज्ञिता ॥ ४ ॥

मूलम् - VR

अरिष्ठनेमिदुहिता सुपर्णभगिनी तु सा । द्वितीया सगरस्यासीत् पत्नी सुमतिसंज्ञिता ॥ १-३८-४

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘सगरकी दूसरी पत्नीका नाम सुमति था । वह अरिष्टनेमि कश्यपकी पुत्री तथा गरुडकी बहिन थी ॥ ४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

सगरन इन्नॊन्दु पत्निय हॆसरु सुमति ऎन्दित्तु. आकॆयु अरिष्टनेमिय पुत्रि हागू गरुडन तङ्गियागिद्दळु.॥4॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

4. ariSThanemiduhitaa = Arishtanemi’s daughter; suparNabhaginii tu = Garuda’s sister, on her part; sumatisam~jnitaa = Sumati, indicated by - designated by that name; saa sagarasya = she is of Sagara; dvitiiyaa patnii aasiit = second, wife, was there.

अनुवादः

“The daughter of Arishtanemi, namely sage Kashyapa, and who is also the sister of Garuda, the Divine-Eagle-vehicle of Vishnu, is the second wife of Sagara, who is known by the name Sumati. [1-38-4]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“And the daughter of Ariṣṭanemi (Kaśyapa), who was the sister of Suparṇa (Garuḍa) (ariṣṭanemer duhitā suparṇabhaginī tu sā), was Sagara’s second queen, known by the name of Sumati (dvitīyā sagarasyāsīt patnī sumatisaṃjñitā).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

ताभ्यां सह महाराजः पत्नीभ्यां तप्तवांस्तपः ।
हिमवन्तं समासाद्य भृगुप्रस्रवणे गिरौ ॥ ५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

ताभ्यां सह महाराजः पत्नीभ्यां तप्तवांस्तपः ।
हिमवन्तं समासाद्य भृगुप्रस्रवणे गिरौ ॥ ५ ॥

मूलम् - VR

ताभ्यां सह महाराजा पत्नीभ्याम् तप्तवांस्तपः । हिमवंतं समासाद्य भृगुप्रस्रवणे गिरौ ॥ १-३८-५

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘महाराज सगर अपनी उन दोनों पत्नियोंके साथ हिमालय पर्वतपर जाकर भृगुप्रस्रवण नामक शिखरपर तपस्या करने लगे ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महाराजा सगरनु तन्न आ इब्बरू पत्नियरॊन्दिगॆ हिमालय पर्वतक्कॆ होगि भृगुप्रस्रवण ऎम्ब शिखरदल्लि तपस्सन्नाचरिसिदनु.॥5॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

5. taabhyaam patniibhyaam saha = along with them two wives ; [saH = he that]; mahaaraajaa = great-king; himavantam samaasaadya = on well-reaching Himalayas; * bhR^iguprasravaNe girau* = on Brhugu-prasavana, mountain; * tapaH taptavaan* = he undertook ascesis; for progeny.

अनुवादः

“Reaching the Himalayan Mountains along with his two wives that great King Sagara practised asceticism on a mountain called Brugu-prsravana for progeny. [1-38-5]

टिप्पनी

The Mt. Brugu-prsravana is said to be among Himalayan range on which the kindness of Sage Bhrigu is ever- flowing.

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Accompanied by those two queens (tābhyāṃ saha patnībhyām), the great king repaired to the Himavān range and practiced austere penance (taptavāṃs tapaḥ) upon the mountain named Bhṛguprasravaṇa (bhṛguprasravaṇe girau).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

38.5 (bhṛguprasravaṇe girau)

Tilaka and Govindarāja explain Bhṛguprasravaṇa as a foothill peak of Himavān (himavatpādaparvate / paryantaparvate) whose mountain cascades (prasravaṇaṃ vāripravāhaḥ) were sanctified by the presence of Sage Bhṛgu (bhṛgvadhiṣṭhitam), while Śiromaṇi adds that the peak was also where Bhṛgu frequently roamed (bhṛgoḥ prasravaṇam atipracāro yasmin).

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Propitiated after a full hundred years of penance, Sage Bhṛgu grants Sagara the boon that one queen shall bear a single son to perpetuate his dynasty while the other shall bear sixty thousand sons.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अथ वर्षशते पूर्णे तपसाऽऽराधितो मुनिः ।
सगराय वरं प्रादाद् भृगुः सत्यवतां वरः ॥ ६ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

अथ वर्षशते पूर्णे तपसाऽऽराधितो मुनिः ।
सगराय वरं प्रादाद् भृगुः सत्यवतां वरः ॥ ६ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘सौ वर्ष पूर्ण होनेपर उनकी तपस्याद्वारा प्रसन्न हुए सत्यवादियोंमें श्रेष्ठ महर्षि भृगुने राजा सगरको वर दिया ॥ ६ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

नूरु वर्षगळु पूर्णवादाग तपस्सिगॆ ऒलिद सत्यवादिगळल्लि श्रेष्ठराद महर्षि भृगुवु राजा सगरनिगॆ वरवन्नु कॊट्टनु.॥6॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

6. atha varSashate puurNe = then, on completion of hundred years ; tapasaa aaraadhitaH = one who is worshipped with asceticism; satyavataam varaH = prominent one among patrons of truth; bhR^iguH muniH = Bhrugu, the sage; sagaraaya varam praadaad = bestowed boon to Sagara.

अनुवादः

“On completion of a hundred years of ascesis, and when the Sage Bhrugu is worshipped with asceticism, that sage who is a prominent one among the patrons of truth bestowed a boon to Sagara. [1-38-6]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Then, when a full hundred years had elapsed (atha varṣaśate pūrṇe), the sage Bhṛgu, foremost of the truthful (satyavatāṃ varaḥ), propitiated by that penance (tapasā ārādhito muniḥ), granted a boon to Sagara (sagarāya varaṃ prādāt):

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

38.6–8 (apatyalābhaḥ sumahān & janayitā)

Govindarāja notes that the greatness (sumahattvam) of Sagara’s promised progeny lies in its extraordinary number (subahvapatyaviṣayatvāt), through which (teneti śeṣaḥ) the king will win matchless fame. On v. 8, Tilaka and Govindarāja explain tāta as a term of paternal endearment (tātety upalālane) and janayitā as the periphrastic future (luṭ, equivalent to janayiṣyati), while Tilaka justifies the apposition of singular ṣaṣṭim with plural sahasrāṇi because ṣaṣṭi is an invariant singular noun (nityaikavacanāntatvāt sāmānādhikaraṇyam).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अपत्यलाभः सुमहान् भविष्यति तवानघ ।
कीर्तिं चाप्रतिमां लोके प्राप्स्यसे पुरुषर्षभ ॥ ७ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

अपत्यलाभः सुमहान् भविष्यति तवानघ ।
कीर्तिं चाप्रतिमां लोके प्राप्स्यसे पुरुषर्षभ ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘निष्पाप नरेश! तुम्हें बहुत-से पुत्रोंकी प्राप्ति होगी । पुरुषप्रवर! तुम इस संसारमें अनुपम कीर्ति प्राप्त करोगे ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

पुण्यात्मनाद राजने! निनगॆ अनेक पुत्ररु प्राप्तरागुवरु. पुरुषश्रेष्ठने! नीनु ई लोकदल्लि अनुपम कीर्तियन्नु पडॆयुवॆ.॥7॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

7. anagha = oh merited one; tava = to you; sumahaan apatyalabhaH bhaviSyati = very admirable, progeny achievement, will be there; puruSarSabha = oh best one among men; loke = in world; apratimaam kiirtim ca = not paralleled, renown, also; praapsyase = you will get.

अनुवादः

” ‘Oh, merited one, you will achieve very admirable progeny, and oh, best one among men, you will also acquire an unparalleled renown in the world. [1-38-7]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“‘You shall obtain a supreme abundance of progeny, O sinless one (apatyalābhaḥ sumahān bhaviṣyati tavānagha), and you shall attain peerless renown in the world, O bull among men (kīrtiṃ cāpratimāṃ loke prāpsyase puruṣarṣabha).

षष्टिं पुत्रसहस्राणि अपरा जनयिष्यति ॥ ८ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

मूलम् - VR

एका जनयिता तात पुत्रं वंशकरं तव । षष्टिं पुत्रसहस्राणि ह्यपरा जनयिष्यति ॥ १-३८-८

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

यज्ञकर्मणि वेदज्ञो यष्टुं समुपचक्रमे ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘यह दृढ़ निश्चय करके वे वेदवेत्ता नरेश अपने उपाध्यायोंके साथ यज्ञ करनेकी तैयारीमें लग गये’ ॥ २४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

हीगॆ दृढ निश्चय माडि आ वेदवेत्त नरेशनु तन्न उपाध्यायरॊन्दिगॆ यज्ञद सिद्धतॆगॆ तॊडगिदनु.॥24॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

24b, c. tadaa = then; veda~jnaH = knower of - expert in Veda-s ; saH raajaa = he, that king Sagara; sopaadhyaayagaNaH = along with assemblages of religious-teachers; yajnakarmaNi = in respect of Vedic-ritual’s proceedings; nishcayam kR^itvaa = on making decision; yaSTum samupacakrame = commenced to perform ritual [well near started] .

अनुवादः

That king Sagara who is an expert in Vedic-procedures has then taken a decision along with the assemblages of his religious-teachers insofar as the procedures of the ritual are concerned, and he commenced to perform the Vedic-ritual. [1-38-24b, c]

समाप्तिः

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डेऽष्टात्रिंशः सर्गः ॥ ३८ ॥
इस प्रकार श्रीवाल्मीकिनिर्मित आर्षरामायण आदिकाव्यके बालकाण्डमें अड़तीसवाँ सर्ग पूरा हुआ ॥ ३८ ॥

अनुवाद (समाप्तिः) (कन्नड)

वाल्मीकि विरचित आर्ष रामायण आदिकाव्यद बालकाण्डदल्लि मूवत्तॆण्टनॆय सर्ग पूर्णवायितु. ॥38॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“—firmly determined, O foremost of men, ‘I shall perform a sacrifice’ (sagarasya naraśreṣṭha yajeyam iti niścitā). Having formed that resolution regarding the sacrificial rite, the king, well-versed in the Vedas and accompanied by his assembly of preceptors (sopādhyāyagaṇo vedajñaḥ), commenced the performance of the sacrifice (yajñakarmaṇi yaṣṭuṃ samupacakrame).”
