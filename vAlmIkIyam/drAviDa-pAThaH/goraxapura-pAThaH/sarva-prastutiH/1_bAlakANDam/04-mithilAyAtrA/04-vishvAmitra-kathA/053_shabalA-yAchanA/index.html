Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro

Chapter [Sarga] 53

Vishvamitra tries to gain Kaamadhenu, but Sage Vashishta persistently refuses Vishvamitra’s bargains and barters. ‘When an ordinary cow alone is held sacred and it is an un-marketable commodity, how then can Shabala, a milker of any wish, be given away or bartered for other riches…’ is the argument of Vashishta. Yet, Vishvamitra goes on his bidding, but in vain.

भागसूचना
  1. कामधेनुकी सहायतासे उत्तम अन्न-पानद्वारा सेनासहित तृप्त हुए विश्वामित्रका वसिष्ठसे उनकी कामधेनुको माँगना और उनका देनेसे अस्वीकार करना
सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

At Vasiṣṭha’s command, the wish-milking cow Śabalā brings forth mountain-high heaps of hot rice, rivers of curds, spiced drinks, and thousands of silver dishes filled with confections, feasting Viśvāmitra’s entire army.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

एवमुक्ता वसिष्ठेन शबला शत्रुसूदन ।
विदधे कामधुक् कामान् यस्य यस्येप्सितं यथा ॥ १ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

एवमुक्ता वसिष्ठेन शबला शत्रुसूदन ।
विदधे कामधुक् कामान् यस्य यस्येप्सितं यथा ॥ १ ॥

एवमुक्त्वा वसिष्ठेन शबला शत्रुसूदन ।
विदधॆ कामधुक्कामान् यस्य यस्येप्सितं यथा ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘शत्रुसूदन! महर्षि वसिष्ठके ऐसा कहनेपर चितकबरे रंगकी उस कामधेनुने जिसकी जैसी इच्छा थी, उसके लिये वैसी ही सामग्री जुटा दी ॥ १ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

शत्रुसूदननॆ! महर्षि वसिष्ठरु हीगॆ हेळिदाग विचित्रवर्णमय आ कामधेनुवु यारिगॆ याव बयकॆ इत्तो अवरिगागि अन्तह भोजन सामग्रियन्नु अणिगॊळिसिदळु.॥1॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

  1. shatrusuudana = oh enemy-chastener, Rama; vasiSThena evam uktaa = this way spoken [she who is] by Vashishta; kaamadhuk = desire milker; shabalaa = cow with amazing coloured skin - Shabala; yasya yasya yathaa iipsitam = whose, whose, as to how, fancied; all those; kaamaan = fancies; of foodstuffs; vidadhe = bestowed.

अनुवादः

“Oh, enemy-chastener Rama, when the Divine Cow Shabala is spoken in this way by Sage Vashishta, then that milker bestowed each and every fancy, fancied by each and every fancier, according to one’s own fancy.” Thus Sage Shataananda continued the legend of Shabala or Kaamadhenu, as a part of the legend of Vishvamitra to Rama and others. [1-53-1]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Thus addressed by Vasiṣṭha, O destroyer of foes (śatrusūdana), the wish-milking cow Śabalā brought forth all desired delicacies for each guest just as he wished (vidadhe kāmadhuk kāmān yasya yasyepsitaṃ yathā):

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

53.1–2 (madhūn, maireyān & varāsavān)

Both Śiromaṇi and Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa) note the epic masculine accusative plural madhūn (‘honeys’). Distinguishing the fermented cordials in v. 2, Śiromaṇi quotes Mādhava: ‘sīdhur ikṣurasaiḥ pakvair apakvair āsavo bhavet / maireyaṃ dhātakīpuṣpaguḍadhānāmlasaṃhitam’ (āsava is distilled from unboiled sugarcane juice, while maireya is infused with dhātakī blossoms, jaggery, and grain), and Govindarāja cites Vaijayantī (‘maireyam āsavo dhātrīdhātakīguḍavāribhiḥ’; cf. Rāma Varmā (Tilaka): dhātrīdhātakīguḍavāribhiḥ kṛtaṃ madyam).

पानानि च महार्हाणि भक्ष्यांश्चोच्चावचानपि ॥ २ ॥

मूलम्

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘ईख, मधु, लावा, मैरेय, श्रेष्ठ आसव, पानक रस आदि नाना प्रकारके बहुमूल्य भक्ष्य-पदार्थ प्रस्तुत कर दिये ॥ २ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

कब्बिनरस, जेनु, अरळु, मैरॆय, श्रेष्ठ आसव, पानक, लड्डुक, अपूप, फेणिका मॊदलाद अनेक प्रकारद अमूल्य भक्ष्य पदार्थगळन्नु सिद्धगॊळिसिदळु.॥2॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

2. ikSuun madhuun tathaa laajaan = sugar-cane [and its dishes,] honey [and its dishes,] like that, cornflakes, [rice-flakes etc.]; varaasavaan [aasanaan] = best liquors [or, with best wineglasses]; maireyaan ca = arracks [Arabic ‘arak ‘sweat’, ‘arak al-tamr ‘alcoholic spirit from dates in Arabia, and from sugar-cane and rice in India,] also; mahaarhaaNi paanaani ca = highly [very] apposite to [royalties and army-men,] drinks, further; tathaa = like that; uccaavacaan = verily diverse; bhakSyaan ca = foodstuffs, also; [shabalaa vidadhe = Shabala, bestowed.]

अनुवादः

Shabala bestowed dishes of sugar cane and honey, and flakes of all sorts, like cornflakes, rice-flakes, also best arracks and liquors in best wineglasses, further, the drinks and foodstuffs that are very diverse and verily apposite to royalties and army-men. [1-53-2]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

sugarcanes, honeys, and parched grains (ikṣūn madhūṃs tathā lājān), spiced maireya cordials and choice āsava spirits (maireyāṃś ca varāsavān), costly beverages (pānāni ca mahārhāṇi), and manifold dishes of every kind (bhakṣyāṃś coccāvacān api);

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

उष्णाढ्यस्यौदनस्यात्र राशयः पर्वतोपमाः ।
मृष्टान्यन्नानि सूपांश्च दधिकुल्यास्तथैव च ॥ ३ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

उष्णाढ्यस्यौदनस्यात्र राशयः पर्वतोपमाः ।
मृष्टान्यन्नानि सूपांश्च दधिकुल्यास्तथैव च ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘गरम-गरम भातके पर्वतके सदृश ढेर लग गये । मिष्टान्न (खीर) और दाल भी तैयार हो गयी । दूध, दही और घीकी तो नहरें बह चलीं ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

बिसि-बिसि अन्नद राशि-राशिगळे सिद्धवादुवु, मृष्टान्न (पायस) तॊव्वॆ, सारु, सिद्धवायितु. हालु, मॊसरु, तुप्प, मॊदलादवुगळ कालुवॆगळे हरिदवु.॥3॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

mountain-like heaps of piping-hot boiled rice (uṣṇāḍhyasyaudanasyātra rāśayaḥ parvatopamāḥ), sweet milk-rice puddings, seasoned broths, and veritable rivers of curds (mṛṣṭāny annāni sūpāṃś ca dadhikulyās tathaiva ca);

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

53.3 (mṛṣṭāny annāni & dadhikulyāḥ)

Tilaka, Śiromaṇi, and Govindarāja gloss uṣṇāḍhyasya as rice rich in warmth (uṣṇatvaguṇayuktasya), mṛṣṭāny annāni as sweet milk-rice puddings (pāyasabhedāḥ), and sūpān as seasoned split-pea and pulse broths (paripakvāḍhakīdvidalādayaḥ, Śiromaṇi). By metonymy (upalakṣaṇam), dadhikulyāḥ (‘canals of curds’) includes streams of milk and clarified butter as well (Tilaka, Śiromaṇi).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

नानास्वादुरसानां च खाण्डवानां तथैव च ।
भोजनानि सुपूर्णानि गौडानि च सहस्रशः ॥ ४ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

नानास्वादुरसानां च खाण्डवानां तथैव च ।
भोजनानि सुपूर्णानि गौडानि च सहस्रशः ॥ ४ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘भाँति-भाँतिके सुस्वादु रस, खाण्डव तथा नाना प्रकारके भोजनोंसे भरी हुई चाँदीकी सहस्रों थालियाँ सज गयीं ॥ ४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

बगॆ बगॆय रुचिकर रस, खाण्डव (श्रीखण्ड) हागू नाना बगॆय भोजनगळिन्द तुम्बिद साविरारु बॆळ्ळिय तट्टॆगळु अणिगॊण्डवु.॥4॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

3.* [atra* = there]; uSNaaDhyasya odanasya api = foods rich in heat [steamy], even; parvatopamaaH raashayaH = mountain similar, stacks - have come up; mR^iSTaannaani ca = palatable, foodstuffs [side dishes, desserts of rice,] also; suupaaH ca = cooked pulse’s dumplings, also; tathaa eva = like that; dadhikulyaaH ca = cascades of curds, also; naanaasvaadurasaanaam ca = varied daintily soft-drinks, also; khaaNDavanaam = sugar-candies, [or, SaaDabaanaam = juices with six kinds of tastes]; tathaa eva ca = like that, also; supuurNaani = fully replete with; bhaajanaani = dishes, [silver hollowware; or, bhojanaani = delectable foodstuffs;] gauDaani ca = [foodstuffs made from the] treacly dumplings of cane sugar, also; sahasrashaH = in thousands; in no time they have come up.

अनुवादः

There emerged mountainous stacks of steamy foodstuffs, palatable side-dishes and desserts of rice etc., also the dumplings of cooked pulses, like that the cascades of curds and the other milk products like butter, ghee, cheese etc. Thousands of silver dishes and hollowware fully replete with daintily soft drinks, like that full with sugar-candy preparations, and with preparations that comprise all the six tastes have come up together with delectable foodstuffs made out of treacly dumplings of cane sugar. [1-53-3, 4]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

and by the thousands, silver banquet vessels brimful of confections and candied sweets of manifold delicious flavors (nānāsvādurasānāṃ ca khāṇḍavānāṃ tathaiva ca bhojanāni supūrṇāni gauḍāni ca sahasraśaḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

53.4–5 (khāṇḍavānām & gauḍāni bhojanāni)

Citing Kataka (‘rajate guḍaḥ’ ity abhidhānam), Tilaka and Śiromaṇi gloss gauḍāni bhojanāni as thousands of silver banquet vessels (rājatāni bhojanapātrāṇi) filled with khāṇḍavānām (sugar-candies), whereas Govindarāja reads ṣāḍavānāṃ … bhājanāni … gauḍāni (vessels filled with six-taste confections and jaggery sweets, guḍavikārāḥ).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

सर्वमासीत् सुसन्तुष्टं हृष्टपुष्टजनायुतम् ।
विश्वामित्रबलं राम वसिष्ठेन सुतर्पितम् ॥ ५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

सर्वमासीत् सुसन्तुष्टं हृष्टपुष्टजनायुतम् ।
विश्वामित्रबलं राम वसिष्ठेन सुतर्पितम् ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘श्रीराम! महर्षि वसिष्ठने विश्वामित्रजीकी सारी सेनाके लोगोंको भलीभाँति तृप्त किया । उस सेनामें बहुत-से हृष्ट-पुष्ट सैनिक थे । उन सबको वह दिव्य भोजन पाकर बड़ा संतोष हुआ ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

श्रीरामा! महर्षि वसिष्ठरु विश्वामित्रर ऎल्ल सैन्यवन्नु चॆन्नागि तृप्तिपडिसिदरु. आ सैन्यदल्लि दष्ट - पुष्टराद अनेक योधरिद्दरु. अवरॆल्लरिगॆ आ दिव्य भॊजन उण्डु बहळ सन्तोषवायितु.॥5॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

5.* raama* = oh Rama; vishvaamitrabalam = Vishvamitra’s forces; sarvam eva = all, like that; vasiSThena sutarpitam = well feasted by Vashishta; susantuSTam = well gladdened ; hR^iSTapuSTajanaayutam aasiit = consisting rejoiced and regaled men, it became.

अनुवादः

Oh, Rama, all of the men in the forces of Vishvamitra, who are already rejoiced and regaled by the regular feasts accorded by their king Vishvamitra, are now comparatively well gladdened when feasted by Sage Vashishta, in which they rejoiced and regaled, for a good measure. [1-53-5]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Thoroughly feasted and gratified by Vasiṣṭha, O Rāma (vasiṣṭhena sutarpitam), the entire army of Viśvāmitra was supremely content, its men joyous and well-nourished (sarvam āsīt susaṃtuṣṭaṃ hṛṣṭapuṣṭajanāyutam).

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Honored together with his queens, priests, and ministers, King Viśvāmitra offers a hundred thousand cows for Śabalā, claiming that she is a jewel and that all jewels rightfully belong to the king.

‘राजर्षि विश्वामित्र भी उस समय अन्तःपुरकी रानियों, ब्राह्मणों और पुरोहितोंके साथ बहुत ही हृष्ट-पुष्ट हो गये ॥ ६ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

राजर्षि विश्वामित्ररू कूड आग अन्तःपुरद राणियरॊन्दिगॆ, ब्राह्मण, पुरोहितरॊन्दिगॆ भोजन माडि बहळ हृष्ट-पुष्टनादनु.॥6॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

6. tadaa = then; saantaHpuravaraH = with best inmates [royal ladies] of palace chambers ; sabraahmaNapurohitaH = with court-scholars and royal-priests; raajarSiH = kingly sage; vishvaamitraH raajaa api = Vishvamitra, king, even; hR^iSTapuSTaH abhavat = became exultant, energetic.

अनुवादः

Partaking that feast together with the best royal ladies of palace chambers, and with the court-scholars and the court-priests even the king Vishvamitra, who by his nature is a kingly sage, became exultant and energetic. [1-53-6]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

The royal sage Viśvāmitra himself became joyous and well-satisfied then (viśvāmitro hi rājarṣir hṛṣṭapuṣṭas tadābhavat), along with the chief ladies of his inner apartments and his brāhmaṇas and royal chaplain (sāntaḥpuravaro rājā sabrāhmaṇapurohitaḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

53.6–7 (hṛṣṭapuṣṭaḥ & amātya vs. mantrin)

Govindarāja explains that Viśvāmitra was hṛṣṭaḥ (delighted) by Vasiṣṭha’s courteous regard (ādareṇa) and puṣṭaḥ (nourished) by the banquet (bhojanādinā). How do amātya and mantrin differ in v. 7? Tilaka, Govindarāja, and Śiromaṇi explain that amātyas are executive ministers assisting in state affairs (karmasacivāḥ), whereas mantrins are privy counselors deliberating on secret policy (mantrasya sacivāḥ).

सामात्यो मन्त्रिसहितः सभृत्यः पूजितस्तदा ।
युक्तः परमहर्षेण वसिष्ठमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ ७ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

सामात्यो मन्त्रिसहितः सभृत्यः पूजितस्तदा ।
युक्तः परमहर्षेण वसिष्ठमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘अमात्य, मन्त्री और भृत्योंसहित पूजित हो वे बहुत प्रसन्न हुए और वसिष्ठजीसे इस प्रकार बोले—

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

अमात्यरु, मन्त्रिगळु, भृत्यर सहित पूजितनागि राजनु बहळ सन्तोषगॊण्डु, वसिष्ठरल्लि हीगॆ हेळिदनु.॥7॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

‘बुद्धिमान् विश्वामित्रके ऐसा कहनेपर भगवान् वसिष्ठ बोले—‘राजन्! मैं यह चितकबरी गाय तुम्हें किसी तरह भी नहीं दूँगा ॥ २२ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

बुद्धिवन्त विश्वामित्रनु हीगॆ हेळिदाग, भगवान् वसिष्ठरु हेळिदरु-राजने! नानु ई वर्णमय गोवन्नु याव विधदिन्दलू कॊडलारॆ.॥22॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

22. dhiimataa vishvaamitreNa evam uktaH = thus who is spoken by shrewd [king] Vishvamitra; bhagavaan = reverential sage Vashishta; raajan = oh king; kathamcana = whatever

  • may happen; shabalaam na daasyaami = I do not surrender Shabala; iti praaha = thus, clearly said.

अनुवादः

When the shrewd king Vishvamitra spoke to the reverential sage Vashishta, that sage has clearly said, ‘whatever it is oh, king, I do not surrender Shabala, the cow.’ [1-53-22]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Thus addressed by the wise Viśvāmitra, the venerable Vasiṣṭha replied: “Under no circumstances, O King, will I give away Śabalā (na dāsyāmīti śabalāṃ prāha rājan kathaṃcana)!

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

एतदेव हि मे रत्नमेतदेव हि मे धनम् ।
एतदेव हि सर्वस्वमेतदेव हि जीवितम् ॥ २३ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

एतदेव हि मे रत्नमेतदेव हि मे धनम् ।
एतदेव हि सर्वस्वमेतदेव हि जीवितम् ॥ २३ ॥

मूलम् - VR

एतदेव हि मे रत्नं एतदेव हि मे धनम् । एतदेव हि सर्वस्वमेतदेव हि जीवितम् ॥ १-५३-२३

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘यही मेरा रत्न है, यही मेरा धन है, यही मेरा सर्वस्व है और यही मेरा जीवन है ॥ २३ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

इदे नन्न रत्नवागिदॆ, इदे नन्न धनवागिदॆ, इदे नन्न सर्वस्ववागिदॆ, इदे नन्न जीवनवागिदॆ.॥23॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

23. etat eva = this, alone; me ratnam hi = for me, gem, indeed; etat eva hi me dhanam = this, alone, truly, wealth [treasure] for me; etat eva sarvasvam hi = this, alone, really everything for me; etat eva jiivitam hi = this, alone, life [alter ego,] actually.

अनुवादः

Really this alone is my gemmy cow, thus I do not require your jewels or gems… truly, this alone is my treasure, thus I do not require your gilded chariots, horses or elephants… really, this alone is my everything, thus I need nothing from you… and actually, this alone is my alter-ego, thus you cannot separate me from myself. [1-53-23]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“For she alone is my jewel, and she alone is my wealth (etad eva hi me ratnam etad eva hi me dhanam); she alone is my entire possession, and she alone is my very life (etad eva hi sarvasvam etad eva hi jīvitam)!

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

दर्शश्च पौर्णमासश्च यज्ञाश्चैवाप्तदक्षिणाः ।
एतदेव हि मे राजन् विविधाश्च क्रियास्तथा ॥ २४ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

दर्शश्च पौर्णमासश्च यज्ञाश्चैवाप्तदक्षिणाः ।
एतदेव हि मे राजन् विविधाश्च क्रियास्तथा ॥ २४ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘राजन्! मेरे दर्श, पौर्णमास, प्रचुर दक्षिणावाले यज्ञ तथा भाँति-भाँतिके पुण्यकर्म—यह गौ ही है । इसीपर ही मेरा सब कुछ निर्भर है ॥ २४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

राजने नन्न दर्श, पौर्णमास्य, धाराळ दक्षिणॆयुळ्ळ यज्ञ, बगॆ-बगॆय पुण्य कर्मगळु ई गोवे आगिदॆ. इदर मेलॆये नन्नदॆल्लवू अवलम्बिसिदॆ.॥24॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

24. raajan = oh king; me = my; darshaH ca = darsha, also - Vedic-rituals; paurNamaasaH ca = poorna maasa Vedic-rituals, also; aaptadakSiNaaH = worthwhile donations; yaGYaaH ca eva = Vedic-rituals, like that, also; tathaa = like that; * vividhaaH kriyaaH ca* = divers, activities, also; etat eva hi = this, alone, indeed.

अनुवादः

This alone is my darsha-puurNamaasa rituals, and like that this alone is all my Vedic-rituals with worthwhile donations and oh, king, this essentially is of service in diverse activities of mine. [1-53-24]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“My new-moon and full-moon sacrifices, my yajñas complete with abundant priestly gifts, and my manifold sacred rites as well—she alone is all these to me, O King (darśaś ca paurṇamāsaś ca yajñāś caivāptadakṣiṇāḥ etad eva hi me rājan vividhāś ca kriyās tathā).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

53.24 (darśapaurṇamāsa & āptadakṣiṇāḥ)

Why does Vasiṣṭha specify yajñāś caivāptadakṣiṇāḥ (sacrifices with abundant priestly gifts)? Tilaka explains that just as the havya and kavya oblations come from Śabalā, even the sacrificial gifts (dakṣiṇā) required to complete his Vedic yajñas depend on her wish-fulfilling power (havyādivad dakṣiṇāpy etadadhīneti sūcitam).

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

अतोमूलाः क्रियाः सर्वा मम राजन् न संशयः ।
बहुना किं प्रलापेन न दास्ये कामदोहिनीम् ॥ २५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

अतोमूलाः क्रियाः सर्वा मम राजन् न संशयः ।
बहुना किं प्रलापेन न दास्ये कामदोहिनीम् ॥ २५ ॥

अतोमूलाः क्रियाः सर्वा मम राजन्न संशयः ।
बहुना किं प्रलापेन न दास्ये कामदोहीनीम् ॥

मूलम् - VR

अतो मूलाः क्रियाः सर्वा मम राजन्न संशयः । बहूना किं प्रलापेन न दास्ये कामदोहिनीम् ॥ १-५३-२५

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘नरेश्वर! मेरे सारे शुभ कर्मोंका मूल यही है, इसमें संशय नहीं है । बहुत व्यर्थ बात करनेसे क्या लाभ । मैं इस कामधेनुको कदापि नहीं दूँगा’ ॥ २५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

नरेशने! नन्न ऎल्ल शुभ कर्मगळ मूल इदे आगिदॆ. इदरल्लि संशयविल्ल. व्यर्थवागि हॆच्चु मातनाडुवुदरिन्द एनु लाभ. नानु ई कामधेनुवन्नु ऎन्दिगू कॊडलारॆ.॥25॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

25. raajan = oh king; mama sarvaaH kriyaaH = for all my activities; ataH muulaaH = in it [in cow,] instated; samshayaH na = there is no doubt; bahuunaa pralaapena kim [prayojanam] = by a lot of, palavering, what is [the use]; kaamadohiniim = wishes milker; na daasye = I do not give away.

अनुवादः

" Oh, king, all of my activities are instated in this cow, then wherefore a lot of palavering a bargain or barter? I do not give away this milker any wish." So said Vashishta to Vishvamitra, and thus Sage Shataananda continued his narration of the legend of Sacred Cow. [1-53-25]

समाप्तिः

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे त्रिपञ्चाशः सर्गः ॥ ५३ ॥
इस प्रकार श्रीवाल्मीकिनिर्मित आर्षरामायण आदिकाव्यके बालकाण्डमें तिरपनवाँ सर्ग पूरा हुआ ॥ ५३ ॥

अनुवाद (समाप्तिः) (कन्नड)

वाल्मीकि विरचित आर्ष रामायण आदिकाव्यद बालकाण्डदल्लि ऐवत्तमूरनॆय सर्ग पूर्णवायितु.॥53॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“All my sacred rites without doubt have their root in her, O King (atomūlāḥ kriyāḥ sarvā mama rājan na saṃśayaḥ). What use is much talk? I will never give away this wish-milking cow (bahunā kiṃ pralāpena na dāsye kāmadohinīm)!”

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

53.25 (atomūlāḥ kriyāḥ sarvāḥ & kāmadohinīm)

Tilaka and Śiromaṇi note that atomūlāḥ (‘having their root in her’) justifies why v. 24 identified Śabalā with the sacrifices themselves. Govindarāja derives kāmadohinīm by Pāṇini 3.2.78 (‘supy ajātau—’ iti ṇiniḥ), while Śiromaṇi notes that bahunā kiṃ pralāpena warns the king to abandon all hope of obtaining her.
