Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro

Book I : Bala Kanda - The Youthful Majesties

On Vishvamitra’s performing severe ascesis Brahma accords the sageship of ‘Great-sage’ to him, whereas Vishvamitra’s ambition is to acquire the sageship of a ‘Brahma-sage’ Absolute-sageship. In the meanwhile, the celestial wench Menaka arrives at that place and Vishvamitra is enticed by her beauty and spends some years with her. Later, on realising the he should have won over lust, he further undertakes severe ascesis, by which gods are perturbed.

भागसूचना
  1. विश्वामित्रको ऋषि एवं महर्षिपदकी प्राप्ति, मेनकाद्वारा उनका तपोभंग तथा ब्रह्मर्षिपदकी प्राप्तिके लिये उनकी घोर तपस्या
सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

After a thousand years of penance at Puṣkara, Brahmā confers the rank of Ṛṣi upon Viśvāmitra; as the sage resumes his austerities, he beholds the peerless celestial nymph Menakā bathing in the sacred lake like lightning in a cloud.

अभ्यगच्छन् सुराः सर्वे तपः फलचिकीर्षवः ॥ १ ॥

मूलम् - VR

पूर्णे वर्षसहस्रे तु व्रतस्नातं महामुनिम् । अभ्यागच्छन् सुराः सर्वे तपःफलचिकीर्षवः ॥ १-६३-१

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

[शतानन्दजी कहते हैं—श्रीराम!] जब एक हजार वर्ष पूरे हो गये, तब उन्होंने व्रतकी समाप्तिका स्नान किया । स्नान कर लेनेपर महामुनि विश्वामित्रके पास सम्पूर्ण देवता उन्हें तपस्याका फल देनेकी इच्छासे आये ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

(शतानन्दरु हेळुत्तिद्दारॆ - श्री रामा!) ऒन्दु साविर वर्ष पूर्णवादाग विश्वामित्ररु व्रत स्नान माडिदरु. व्रत समाप्तियादाग ब्रह्मादि समस्त देवतॆगळु तपस्सिन फलवन्नु कॊडलु अल्लिगॆ बन्दरु.॥1॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

1. varSasahasre puurNe = while being completed thousand years; sarve suraaH = all, gods; tapaHphalacikiirSavaH = wishing [to accord to Vishvamitra] for ascetic fruit; vratasnaatam mahaamunim = great saint took bath of solemnity ; abhyaagacChan = came towards.

अनुवादः

“When thousand years are completed in ascesis and when that great saint Vishvamitra took a bath of solemnity, all the gods have come forward wishing to accord the fruit of that ascesis to Vishvamitra.” Thus Sage Shataananda continued the legend of Vishvamitra. [1-63-1]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

When a full thousand years had elapsed (pūrṇe varṣasahasre tu), all the gods, desiring to bestow the fruit of his austerity (tapaḥphalacikīrṣavaḥ), approached the great sage who had taken his ritual bath upon completing his vow (vratasnātaṃ mahāmunim abhyagacchan).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.1–2 (From Rājarṣi to Ṛṣi)

Why does Viśvāmitra receive only the rank of Ṛṣi after a thousand years of penance? Govindarāja (Bhūṣaṇa) explains that because he had cursed his sons in Sarga 62 and had not yet conquered anger, he could not yet attain Brahmarṣi status (putraviṣayakrūraśāpadānena krodhanirjayābhāvān na brahmarṣitvasiddhiḥ). Yet, as Rāma Varmā (Tilaka), Śiromaṇi, and Govindarāja all point out, Brahmā’s declaration ṛṣis tvam asi—deliberately omitting the prefix rājan—signifies that his Kṣatriya nature has now begun to dissolve (rājapadaprayogeṇa kṣatriyatvaviśleṣaḥ sūcitaḥ).

ऋषिस्त्वमसि भद्रं ते स्वार्जितैः कर्मभिः शुभैः ॥ २ ॥

मूलम्

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

उस समय महातेजस्वी ब्रह्माजीने मधुर वाणीमें कहा— ‘मुने! तुम्हारा कल्याण हो । अब तुम अपने द्वारा उपार्जित शुभकर्मोंके प्रभावसे ऋषि हो गये’ ॥ २ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

आग महातेजस्वी ब्रह्मदेवरु मधुरवाणियिन्द - मुनिये! निनगॆ मङ्गळवागलि. ईग नीनु गळिसिद शुभ कर्मगळ प्रभावदिन्द ऋषियादॆ.॥2॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

  1. sumahaatejaaH brahmaa = very highly resplendent, Brahma; * suruciram vacaH abraviit* = spoke highly palatable words; tvam = you; svaarjitaiH = acquired - undertaken on your own; shubhaiH karmabhiH = with auspicious, deeds; R^iSiH asi = you are - you have become sage; te bhadram = let there be safety to you.

अनुवादः

The very highly resplendent Brahma spoke to him with highly palatable words, ‘you have now become a kingly sage by virtue of auspicious deeds you have personally undertaken, let safeness betide you.’ [1-63-2]

टिप्पनी

Vividly: ‘so far, you are a king and by your auspicious deeds now you have become a ‘kingly sage’ raajaSi and to attain a still higher brahmarSi ‘Absolute sageship’ you are not yet merited… as the Absolute sageship will be endowed to those who are above the negative traits of humans, like greed, anger, lust, jealousy etc.’

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Brahmā of supreme splendour spoke these most pleasing words (abravīt sumahātejā brahmā suruciraṃ vacaḥ): “You have now become a Ṛṣi—may auspiciousness attend you!—through the righteous deeds earned by your own exertion (ṛṣis tvam asi bhadraṃ te svārjitaiḥ karmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ).”

तमेवमुक्त्वा देवेशस्त्रिदिवं पुनरभ्यगात् ।
विश्वामित्रो महातेजा भूयस्तेपे महत् तपः ॥ ३ ॥

मूलम्

तमेवमुक्त्वा देवेशस्त्रिदिवं पुनरभ्यगात् ।
विश्वामित्रो महातेजा भूयस्तेपे महत् तपः ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

उनसे ऐसा कहकर देवेश्वर ब्रह्माजी पुनः स्वर्गको चले गये । इधर महातेजस्वी विश्वामित्र पुनः बड़ी भारी तपस्यामें लग गये ॥ ३ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

विश्वामित्ररल्लि हीगॆ हेळि देवेश्वर ब्रह्मदेवरु तम्म लोकक्कॆ तॆरळिदरु. इत्त महातेजस्वी मुनिगळु पुनः भारी तपस्सिगॆ तॊडगिदरु.॥3॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

3. deveshaH = god of gods [Brahma]; tam evam uktvaa = on speaking to him in that way; punaH tridivam abhyagaat [abhi-agaat] = again came towards heaven [came back]; mahaatejaaH vishvaamitraH = a highly vigorous one, Vishvamitra; bhuuyaH mahat tapaH tepe = again performed great ascesis.

अनुवादः

On speaking to Vishvamitra in that way Brahma returned to heaven, and that highly vigorous Vishvamitra again performed a great ascesis. [1-63-3]

टिप्पनी

The word vi in the name of Vishvamitra is taken as the third syllable in Gayatri Hymn.

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Having spoken thus to him, the lord of the gods returned again to heaven (tam evam uktvā deveśas tridivaṃ punar abhyagāt); and the resplendent Viśvāmitra once again practiced mighty austerity (bhūyas tepe mahat tapaḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.3 (The Third Syllable of the Rāmāyaṇa-Gāyatrī)

Govindarāja records the traditional metrical landmark at 63.3cd: the syllable vi in viśvāmitro mahātejā bhūyas tepe mahat tapaḥ marks the third syllable of the twenty-four-syllable Rāmāyaṇa-Gāyatrī (ity atra gāyatrītṛtīyākṣaram), occurring two thousand verses into the poem.

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

नरश्रेष्ठ! तदनन्तर बहुत समय व्यतीत होनेपर परम सुन्दरी अप्सरा मेनका पुष्करमें आयी और वहाँ स्नानकी तैयारी करने लगी ॥ ४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

नरश्रेष्ठने! अनन्तर बहळ समय कळॆदाग परमसुन्दरी अप्सरॆ मेनकॆयु पुष्करक्कॆ बन्दु अल्लि स्नानमाडलु सिद्धळादळु.॥4॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

4. narashreSTha = oh the best man, Rama; tataH = then; * mahataa kaalena* = after a great time - at a long lost time; *menakaa paramaapsaraaH

  • = Menaka, high [prominent, apsara -celestial wench; puSkareSu snaatum samupacakrame = started to take bath in holy lake - started a swimming for sport.

अनुवादः

Then at a long lost time Menaka, the prominent apsara, celestial wench, has arrived and started to swim for sport in that holy lake. [1-63-4]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Then, after a long time (tataḥ kālena mahatā), the supreme celestial nymph Menakā (menakā paramāpsarāḥ), O best of men, came to bathe in the sacred lakes of Puṣkara (puṣkareṣu naraśreṣṭha snātuṃ samupacakrame).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.4–5 (Menakā Like Lightning in a Cloud)

Dispatched by the gods (devapreṣiteti śeṣaḥ, Govindarāja), Menakā bathes in the dark waters of Lake Puṣkara (tatra sarasi snāntī), gleaming like a streak of lightning amid a bank of rainclouds (jaladamaṇḍalamadhyavartinī vidyud iva).

तां ददर्श महातेजा मेनकां कुशिकात्मजः ।
रूपेणाप्रतिमां तत्र विद्युतं जलदे यथा ॥ ५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

तां ददर्श महातेजा मेनकां कुशिकात्मजः ।
रूपेणाप्रतिमां तत्र विद्युतं जलदे यथा ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

महातेजस्वी कुशिकनन्दन विश्वामित्रने वहाँ उस मेनकाको देखा । उसके रूप और लावण्यकी कहीं तुलना नहीं थी । जैसे बादलमें बिजली चमकती हो, उसी प्रकार वह पुष्करके जलमें शोभा पा रही थी ॥ ५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महातेजस्वी कुशिकनन्दन विश्वामित्ररु अल्लि आ मेनकॆयन्नु नोडिदरु. आकॆयु अतुलवाद रूप लावण्यदिन्द मोडगळल्लिन विद्युल्लतॆयन्तॆ आ पुष्कर जलदल्लि शोभिसुत्तिद्दळु.॥5॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

5. mahaatejaaH kushikaatmajaH = great resplendent, Kushi’s son - Vishvamitra; tatra = there - in holy lake; jalade vidyutam yathaa = electric-flash as with in black-cloud; ruupeNa apratimaam = not matchabe by mein - an incomparable one; taam menakaam dadarsha = he has seen at her, Menaka.

अनुवादः

That great resplendent sage Vishvamitra has seen Menaka who is incomparable in her mien and comparable to an electric-flash in a black-cloud, while she is swimming in the holy lake. [1-63-5]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

The resplendent son of Kuśika beheld that Menakā there (tāṃ dadarśa mahātejā menakāṃ kuśikātmajaḥ), matchless in beauty, shining like a flash of lightning in a dark cloud (rūpeṇāpratimāṃ tatra vidyutaṃ jalade yathā).

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Pierced by Kāma’s infatuation, Viśvāmitra invites Menakā to dwell in his hermitage; ten years pass like a single day and night before the sage awakens in shame and indignation at the gods’ stratagem to steal his penance.

उसे देखकर विश्वामित्र मुनि कामके अधीन हो गये और उससे इस प्रकार बोले—‘अप्सरा! तेरा स्वागत है, तू मेरे इस आश्रममें निवास कर ॥ ६ ॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

6, 7a. dR^iSTvaa muniH = having seen, the sage; kandarpavashagaH = on going into Love-god’s control; taam idam abraviit = said this to her ; apsaraH te svaagatam astu = oh apsara, welcome is there to you; mama iha aashrame vasa ca = you stay here at my hermitage, also; madanena sumohitam = bewitched [for you] by Love-god; [maam = me]; anugR^ihNiiSva = oblige me; te bhadram = you be safe.

अनुवादः

On seeing her the sage having gone under the sway the Love-god said this to her, ‘oh, apsara, you are welcome, I invite you to bide in my hermitage. Oblige me who am bewitched by Love-god for you. You be safe.’ [1-63-6, 7a]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Falling under the sway of Kāma’s intoxicating pride (kandarpadarpavaśagaḥ), the sage spoke these words to her: “Welcome to you, O celestial nymph! Abide here in my hermitage (apsaraḥ svāgataṃ te ’stu vasa ceha mamāśrame).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.6–7 (Subjugation to Kāma)

Tilaka and Śiromaṇi gloss kandarpadarpavaśagaḥ as fallen under the sway of the intoxicating passion aroused by Kāma (kandarpakṛto darpo madaḥ, Tilaka), and anugṛhṇīṣva as ‘assuage my torment of passion by granting your embrace’ (matkāmatāpaṃ śamaya, Govindarāja; bhogadānena, Tilaka).

इत्युक्ता सा वरारोहा तत्र वासमथाकरोत् ॥ ७ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

अनुगृह्णीष्व भद्रं ते मदनेन विमोहितम् ।
इत्युक्ता सा वरारोहा तत्र वासमथाकरोत् ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘तेरा भला हो । मैं कामसे मोहित हो रहा हूँ । मुझपर कृपा कर ।’ उनके ऐसा कहनेपर सुन्दर कटिप्रदेशवाली मेनका वहाँ निवास करने लगी ॥ ७ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

निनगॆ ऒळ्ळॆयदागलि नानु कामदिन्द मोहितनागिरुवॆनु. नन्न मेलॆ दयॆतोरु. अवरु हीगॆ हेळिदाग सुन्दरियाद मेनकॆयु अल्ले इरतॊडगिदळु.॥7॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Show favour—may auspiciousness attend you!—to me who am utterly bewildered by love (anugṛhṇīṣva bhadraṃ te madanena vimohitam)!” Thus addressed, that fair-hipped nymph took up her abode there (sā varārohā tatra vāsam athākarot).

23b, 24. tapodhanaH = ascetically wealthy one; dharme /gharme = in righteousness / in summer; pancatapaaH bhuutvaa = on becoming - among them five ascetic-fires; varSaasu aakaashasamshrayaH = in rain [rainy season,] taking shelter [as rooftop] by [open] sky; shishire = in winter; raatryahaani salile shaayii = reclining night and day in water; evam varSasahasram = this way, thousand years; ghoram tapaH upaagamat hi = he obtained - he undertook severe ascesis, indeed.

अनुवादः

In summer he became the Five-Fire Ascetic, in rainy season open sky is his rooftop, and in winter water is his reclining bed even by day or by night, and thus that ascetically wealthy Vishvamitra indeed undertook a severe ascesis in this way, for another thousand years. [1-63-23b, 24]

टिप्पनी

Five fires are four Fires in four directions and the fifth one is the scorching sun, overhead. An ascetic staying among these five fires will become an ascetic fire. The first word in first foot dharme, will also used as gharme, as above, in some mms.

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

23: With arms upraised, without any support, subsisting on air alone as he practiced austerity (ūrdhvabāhur nirālambo vāyubhakṣas tapaś caran), surrounded by five fires in the summer heat, and having only the open sky for shelter during the rains (gharme pañcatapā bhūtvā varṣāsv ākāśasaṃśrayaḥ),

24: And lying in water day and night during the dewy winter season, rich in austerity (śiśire salileśāyī rātryahāni tapodhanaḥ)—thus for a thousand years he underwent terrible penance (evaṃ varṣasahasraṃ hi tapo ghoram upāgamat).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.23–24 (The Three Seasonal Austerities)

Tilaka, Śiromaṇi, and Govindarāja gloss nirālambaḥ as without any physical prop or shelter (gṛhādyālambanarahitaḥ), gharme pañcatapāḥ as enduring four blazing fires around him with the summer sun overhead as the fifth, ākāśasaṃśrayaḥ as standing in the open without roof or tree-cover during the monsoon rains (varṣāvaraṇarahitadeśasthaḥ), and salileśāyī (var. salilasthāyī) as immersed in icy water day and night during winter.

सन्तापः सुमहानासीत् सुराणां वासवस्य च ॥ २५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

तस्मिन् सन्तप्यमाने तु विश्वामित्रे महामुनौ ।
सन्तापः सुमहानासीत् सुराणां वासवस्य च ॥ २५ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

महामुनि विश्वामित्रके इस प्रकार तपस्या करते समय देवताओं और इन्द्रके मनमें बड़ा भारी संताप हुआ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महामुनि विश्वामित्ररु इन्तह घोर तपस्सु माडुत्तिरुवाग देवतॆगळल्लि मत्तु इन्द्रन मनस्सिनल्लि भारी सन्ताप उण्टायितु.॥25॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

25. mahaamunau = of great saint; tasmin vishvaamitre = that, Vishvamitra; samtapyamaane tu = while undertaking ascesis, but; suraaNaam vaasavasya ca = for gods, to Indra, even; sumahaan santaapaH = a very high, burning [problem]; aasiit = it became.

अनुवादः

While that great saint is undertaking fervent ascesis it has become a very high burning problem to gods and even to Indra. [1-63-25]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

While that great sage Viśvāmitra was thus performing fiery austerity (tasmin saṃtapyamāne tu viśvāmitre mahāmunau), a great torment of anxiety arose among the gods and in Vāsava (Indra) (saṃtāpaḥ sumahān āsīt surāṇāṃ vāsavasya ca).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.25 (Indra’s Groundless Panic)

Reading saṃbhramaḥ (or saṃtāpaḥ), Govindarāja remarks that Indra’s alarm was misplaced fear (asthāne bhayam): seeing Viśvāmitra’s Kṣatriya birth and fierce penance, Indra needlessly dreaded ‘Does he covet my throne of heaven?’ (kim asmatpadaṃ kāṅkṣata iti).

उवाचात्महितं वाक्यमहितं कौशिकस्य च ॥ २६ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

मूलम् - VR

रंभामप्सरसं शक्रः सह सर्वैर्मरुद्गणैः । उवाचात्महितं वाक्यमहितं कौशिकस्य च ॥ १-६३-२६

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

समस्त मरुद्‍गणोंसहित इन्द्रने उस समय रम्भा अप्सरासे ऐसी बात कही, जो अपने लिये हितकर और विश्वामित्रके लिये अहितकर थी ॥ २६ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

समस्त मरुद्गणगळ सहित इन्द्रनु आग रम्भा अप्सरॆयल्लि तमगॆ हितकर हागू विश्वामित्ररिगॆ अहितकरवाद मातन्नु हेळिदनु.॥26॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

26. shakraH = Indra; sarvaiH marut gaNaiH saha = along with all Air-gods’ assemblages; aatmahitam = advantageous for himself; kaushikasya = to Kaushika’s son Vishvamitra; ahitam ca = disadvantageous, also; vaakyam = words; rambhaam apsarasam uvaaca = spoke to Rambha the apsara [celestial wench].

अनुवादः

“Then Indra along with all the assemblages of the Air-gods spoke to Rambha, the celestial wench, words that are advantageous to himself, but disadvantageous to the son of sage Kushi, namely Vishvamitra.” Thus Sage Shataananda continued his narration. [1-63-26]

समाप्तिः

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे त्रिषष्टितमः सर्गः ॥ ६३ ॥
इस प्रकार श्रीवाल्मीकिनिर्मित आर्षरामायण आदिकाव्यके बालकाण्डमें तिरसठवाँ सर्ग पूरा हुआ ॥ ६३ ॥

अनुवाद (समाप्तिः) (कन्नड)

वाल्मीकि विरचित आर्ष रामायण आदिकाव्यद बालकाण्डदल्लि अरवत्त मूरनॆय सर्ग पूर्णवायितु.॥63॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Surrounded by all the hosts of Maruts, Śakra (Indra) spoke to the celestial nymph Rambhā (rambhām apsarasaṃ śakraḥ sarvaiḥ saha marudgaṇaiḥ) a word beneficial to himself and detrimental to Kauśika (uvācātmahitaṃ vākyam ahitaṃ kauśikasya ca).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

63.26 (Summoning Rambhā)

Tilaka identifies ātmahitam as beneficial to Indra himself (ātmā indraḥ), while Govindarāja notes that ahitaṃ kauśikasya ca also hints at Indra’s lingering hostility toward Viśvāmitra over Triśaṅku and Ambarīṣa’s sacrifice (tadviṣayavairaniryātanārthaṃ ca rambhāpreraṇam).
