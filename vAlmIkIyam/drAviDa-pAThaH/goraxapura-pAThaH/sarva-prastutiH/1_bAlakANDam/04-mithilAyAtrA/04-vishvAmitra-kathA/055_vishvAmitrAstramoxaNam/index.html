Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro

Book I : Bala Kanda - The Youthful Majesties

Vishvamitra ruins Vashishta’s hermitage on acquiring missiles at the grace of God Shiva by practising a paramount ascesis. When the forces generated by Wish-Milker Sacred Cow, namely Kaamadhenu, have eliminated Vishvamitra’s army and his hundred sons, he felt indignant and pray Shiva for bestowal of unusual missiles. On getting them, he again comes to Sage Vashishta’s hermitage and ruins it completely. Then extremely infuriated Sage Vashishta resorts to his Brahma-baton to confront Vishvamitra.

भागसूचना
  1. अपने सौ पुत्रों और सारी सेनाके नष्ट हो जानेपर विश्वामित्रका तपस्या करके महादेवजीसे दिव्यास्त्र पाना तथा उनका वसिष्ठके आश्रमपर प्रयोग करना एवं वसिष्ठजीका ब्रह्मदण्ड लेकर उनके सामने खड़ा होना
सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Urged by Vasiṣṭha to create by her yogic potency, Śabalā brings forth hosts of warriors from every limb who annihilate Viśvāmitra’s army, and Vasiṣṭha burns the king’s hundred sons to ashes with a single huṃkāra.

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

विश्वामित्रन अस्त्रगळिन्द घासिगॊण्डु व्याकुलरादुदन्नु कण्डु वसिष्ठरु पुनः - ‘कामधेनुवे! ईग योगबलदिन्द बेरॆ सैनिकरन्नु सृष्टिमाडु’ ऎन्दु अप्पणॆ माडिदरु.॥1॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

1. tataH = then; vasiSThaH = Vashishta; vishvaamitraastramohitaan = those who are perplexed by missiles of Vashishta; aakulaan = those flurrying; taan dR^iSTvaa = them - on seeing army; kaamadhuk = oh wish milker [Shabala]; yogataH sR^ija = you generate [more army] by yogic [capacity] ; codayaamaasa = started to motivate - in this way.

अनुवादः

Then on seeing the flurrying warriors earlier created by the Sacred Cow, Shabala, who are perplexed by the missiles of Vishvamitra, Vashishta started to motivate her, ‘oh, wish-milker, generate more forces by your yogic capacity.’ Thus Sage Shataananda continued his narration about the legend of Vishvamitra to Rama. [1-55-1]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Seeing those warriors thrown into confusion and bewildered by Viśvāmitra’s missiles (viśvāmitrāstramohitān), Vasiṣṭha urged the wish-granting cow: “O Kāmadhuk, create by your yogic power (kāmadhuk sṛja yogataḥ)!”

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.1 (kāmadhuk sṛja yogataḥ)

Seeing her first warriors routed and bewildered by Viśvāmitra’s missiles (ākulān palāyamānān, Tilaka), Vasiṣṭha commands the wish-milking cow: “O Kāmadhuk, create through your yogic potency!” (yogato yogamahimnā, Tilaka, Govindarāja). Govindarāja notes that although bovine (tiryaktve ’pi), she possesses yogic power through Brahmā’s grace (brahmaprasādād yogaśaktir asti).

From her roaring huṃkāra were born Kāmbojas radiant as the sun (kāmbojā ravisaṃnibhāḥ), and from her udder sprang forth Barbaras wielding weapons in their hands (ūdhasaś cātha sambhūtā barbarāḥ śastrapāṇayaḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.2–4 (kāmbojāḥ, barbarāḥ, yavanāḥ, śakāḥ, mlecchāḥ, hārītāḥ & kirātakāḥ)

From Śabalā’s huṃkāra (var. humbhārava) spring sun-bright Kāmbojas; from her udder (ūdhasaḥ stanāt, Govindarāja) armed Barbaras (var. Pahlavas/Paplavas); from her reproductive tract Yavanas; from her dung-passage Śakas; and from her hair-pores (romakūpeṣu romakūpebhyaḥ, Tilaka) Mlecchas, Hārītas, and Kirātas, who instantly annihilate Viśvāmitra’s fourfold army.

“From the ‘hums’ of her mooing Kaamboja-s similar to sunshine are born, from her udder Pahlava-s wielding weaponry are born, from the area of her privates Yavana-s, likewise from her rectal area Shaka-s, and from her hair-roots Mleccha-s, Haariitaa-s along with Kirataka-s are issued forth. [1-55-2, 3]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

From her reproductive tract sprang Yavanas (yonideśāc ca yavanāḥ), from her dung-passage Śakas (śakṛddeśāc chakāḥ smṛtāḥ), and from her hair-pores Mlecchas, Hārītas, and Kirātas (romakūpeṣu mlecchāś ca hārītāḥ sakirātakāḥ).

‘रघुनन्दन! उन सब वीरोंने पैदल, हाथी, घोड़े और रथसहित विश्वामित्रकी सारी सेनाका तत्काल संहार कर डाला ॥ ४ ॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

4. raghunandana = oh Raghu’s legatee, Rama; taiH = by them [Yavana-s etc.]; tat kSaNaat = that, very moment; vishvamitrasya = of Vishvamitra; sapadaatigajam = with foot-soldiers, elephants; saashvam saratham = with horses, with chariots; tat sainyam = that, army; niSuuditam = utterly slaughtered.

अनुवादः

At that very moment, oh, Rama the legatee of Raghu, Vishvamitra’s army comprising foot-soldiers, elephants, chariots and horses is utterly slaughtered by the Yavana, Mleccha etc., forces generated by the Sacred Cow. [1-55-4]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

By them that entire host of Viśvāmitra was annihilated on the spot (tais tan niṣūditaṃ sarvaṃ viśvāmitrasya tatkṣaṇāt), together with its foot-soldiers, elephants, horses, and chariots (sapadātigajaṃ sāśvaṃ saratham), O delight of the Raghus!

‘महात्मा वसिष्ठद्वारा अपनी सेनाका संहार हुआ देख विश्वामित्रके सौ पुत्र अत्यन्त क्रोधमें भर गये और नाना प्रकारके अस्त्र-शस्त्र लेकर जप करनेवालोंमें श्रेष्ठ वसिष्ठमुनिपर टूट पड़े । तब उन महर्षिने हुंकारमात्रसे उन सबको जलाकर भस्म कर डाला ॥ ५-६ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महात्मा वसिष्ठरिन्द तम्म सैन्यद संहारवन्नु नोडि विश्वामित्रन नूरु पुत्ररु अत्यन्त क्रोधगॊण्डु, नाना प्रकारद अस्त्र-शस्त्रगळन्नॆत्तिकॊण्डु, जप माडुववरल्लि श्रेष्ठराद वसिष्ठर मेलॆ ऎरगिदरु. आग आ महर्षियु हुङ्कारमात्रदिन्द अवरॆल्लरन्नु सुट्टुबिट्टरु.॥5-6॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

5. mahaatmanaa vasiSThena = by great souled one, by Vashishta; niSuuditam sainyam dR^iSTvaa = on seeing utterly slaughtered army; vishvaamitrasutaanaam = Vishvamitra’s sons; shatam = [a band of] hundred; naanaavidhaayudham = diverse sorts of weapons wielding; * susamkruddham* = very highly angering; japataam varam vasiSTham = supreme among meditators , towards Vashishta; abhyadhaavat = [that band of hundred sons] sprinted forth; mahaan R^iSiH = [that] great, sage; taan sarvaan = them, all; humkaareNa eva = by ‘hum’ [blasting,] only; nirdadaaha = completely burnt down.

अनुवादः

On seeing their army utterly slaughtered by the vital spirit of Vashishta, a band of hundred sons of Vishvamitra wielding divers weapons exasperatedly sprinted forth towards that supreme meditator among all meditators. But that great sage Vashishta completely burnt all of them down just by blasting ‘hum’ sounds. [1-55-5, 6]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

5: Beholding their army annihilated (dṛṣṭvā niṣūditaṃ sainyam) by the high-souled Vasiṣṭha, Viśvāmitra’s hundred sons, armed with weapons of every kind (viśvāmitrasutānāṃ tu śataṃ nānāvidhāyudham)—

6: —rushed in furious wrath (abhyadhāvat susaṃkruddham) against Vasiṣṭha, the foremost of japa-reciters (japatāṃ varam); with a mere huṃkāra syllable (huṃkāreṇaiva), the great seer burned them all to ashes (tān sarvān nirdadāha mahān ṛṣiḥ).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.5–6 (viśvāmitrasutānāṃ śatam & huṃkāreṇaiva nirdadāha)

Realizing that Vasiṣṭha himself is the source of the cow’s might (ṛṣir eva dhenor balam, Tilaka), Viśvāmitra’s hundred sons charge the sage. Why did Vasiṣṭha incinerate them with a single huṃkāra after sparing the king in 54.11? Tilaka explains that unlike an anointed sovereign (pṛthivīpatitvābhāvāt), these princes were murderous aggressors (ātatāyitvāc ca) whom the foremost of japa-reciters (japatāṃ varam, Govindarāja) could lawfully consume with ascetic fire (tapobalasaṃbhṛtena huṃkāreṇa).

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Bereft of his sons and army, Viśvāmitra sinks into despondency like a waveless ocean, a fangless serpent, an eclipsed sun, and a wing-clipped bird; installing one surviving son on the throne, he retires to Himavat to propitiate Śiva.

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

7. tadaa = then; saashvarathapaadaataaH = with horses, chariots, foot-soldiers; te vishvaamitrasutaaH = those, Vishvamitra’s sons; mahaatmanaa vasiSThena = by the one who has vital spirit, by Vashishta; muhuurtena = in a wink; bhasmiikR^itaa = rendered to ashes.

अनुवादः

That sage with vital spirit, Vashishta, then in a wink rendered those sons of Vishvamitra, along with their horses, chariots, and foot-soldiers to ashes. [1-55-7]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Together with their horses, chariots, and foot-soldiers (sāśvarathapādātāḥ), those sons of Viśvāmitra were reduced to ashes in a single moment (bhasmīkṛtā muhūrtena) by the high-souled Vasiṣṭha.

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.7–8 (muhūrtena bhasmīkṛtāḥ)

Both Tilaka and Govindarāja cite Pāṇini 2.3.6 (apavarge tṛtīyā, illustrated by māsenānuvāko ’dhītaḥ) to explain the instrumental muhūrtena: within a single instant the action of reducing the princes and their fourfold retinue to ashes achieved complete fulfillment.

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.16 (sāṅgopāṅgopaniṣadaḥ sarahasyaḥ dhanurvedaḥ)

All three commentators distinguish the fourfold apparatus of Dhanurveda: aṅgas are direct auxiliaries (sākṣādupakārakāṇi / sannipatyopakārakam); upāṅgas are indirect auxiliaries (ārādupakārakāṇi); upaniṣads are esoteric mantras (mantrā upaniṣadaḥ, Tilaka; rahasyamantraḥ, Govindarāja); and rahasya is the secret doctrine accessible solely from a preceptor’s lips (ācāryamukhaikagamyaṃ vidyājātam, Tilaka). While Govindarāja views the -a stem in -upaniṣadaḥ as archaic (ārṣam), Tilaka and Śiromaṇi justify it via the samāsānta suffix -ṭac in a collective dvandva (indrāc chud-gṛhāntāt samāhāre).

वसिष्ठस्याश्रमपदं शून्यमासीन्महात्मनः ।
मुहूर्तमिव निःशब्दमासीदीरिणसन्निभम् ॥ २४ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

वसिष्ठस्याश्रमपदं शून्यमासीन्महात्मनः ।
मुहूर्तमिव निःशब्दमासीदीरिणसन्निभम् ॥ २४ ॥

मूलम् - VR

वसिष्ठस्याश्रमपदं शून्यमासीन्महात्मनः । मुहूर्तमिव निःशब्दमासीदिरिणसंनिभम् ॥ १-५५-२४

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘महात्मा वसिष्ठका वह आश्रम सूना हो गया । दो ही घड़ीमें ऊसर भूमिके समान उस स्थानपर सन्नाटा छा गया ॥ २४ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

महात्मा वसिष्ठर आ आश्रमवु शून्यवायितु. मुहूर्त मात्रदल्लि बञ्जरु भूमियन्तागि, निश्शब्दवायितु.॥24॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

24. mahaatmanaH vasiSThasya aashramapadam = of broad-minded sage, Vashishta ’s, hermitage threshold; shuunyam aasiit = it has become void; muhuurtam iva = just in a wink ; iriNasannibham = alike arid land - burial ground; niHshabdam aasiit = become silent .

अनुवादः

The threshold of that broad-minded Vashishta’s hermitage fell void, and in a wink it has become silent like a burial ground as every shrub, herb and tree is cindery, and every bird, animal and stock is an evacuee. [1-55-24]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

The hermitage of the high-souled Vasiṣṭha became utterly deserted (śūnyam āsīn mahātmanaḥ); for an hour it stood devoid of all sound, resembling a barren, saline desert (muhūrtam iva niḥśabdam āsīd īriṇasaṃnibham).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.24 (īriṇasaṃnibham)

Emptied of every living creature (sarvaprāṇipalāyanāt, Tilaka), the great sage’s hermitage becomes silent and resembles an arid, saline desert devoid of trees or men (īriṇam ūṣaram, nirvṛkṣanirmanuṣyakāntārasadṛśam, Tilaka).

सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका
English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Though Vasiṣṭha repeatedly calls out to his fleeing disciples that he will dispel Gādhi’s son like morning mist before the sun, he turns in righteous fury upon Viśvāmitra and raises his ascetic staff like a second rod of Yama.

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

वदतो वै वसिष्ठस्य मा भैरिति मुहुर्मुहुः ।
नाशयाम्यद्य गाधेयं नीहारमिव भास्करः ॥ २५ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

वदतो वै वसिष्ठस्य मा भैरिति मुहुर्मुहुः ।
नाशयाम्यद्य गाधेयं नीहारमिव भास्करः ॥ २५ ॥

मूलम् - VR

वदतो वै वसिष्ठस्य मा भैष्टेति मुहुर्मुहुः । नाशयाम्यद्य गाधेयं नीहारमिव भास्करः ॥ १-५५-२५

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘वसिष्ठजी बार-बार कहने लगे—‘डरो मत, मैं अभी इस गाधिपुत्रको नष्ट किये देता हूँ । ठीक उसी तरह, जैसे सूर्य कुहासेको मिटा देता है’ ॥ २५ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

वसिष्ठरु पदे पदे हेळतॊडगिदरु - हॆदरबेडि. सूर्यनु मञ्जन्नु इल्लवागिसुवन्तॆ, ईगले गाधि पुत्रनन्नु नाशमाडिबिडुवॆनु.॥25॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

25. bhaaskaraH niihaaram iva = Sun, as with mist - like sun demisting the mist; gaadheyam = Gadhi’s son - Vishvamitra will be; adya naashayaami = now, I will destroy; maa bhaiSTa = do not, fear; iti = thus; vasiSThasya = of Vashishta; muhuH muhuH = again, again; vadataH = [although] saying [shouting at the fleeing inmates of hermitage, they have; vipradrutaa = very speedily fled].

अनुवादः

Although Vashishta shouted at them saying time and again, ‘do not fear, do not fear… now I will dissipate that son of Gaadhi, Vishvamitra, like Sun demisting the mist,’ all the inmates of hermitage have hastily fled. [1-55-25]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

—even while Vasiṣṭha was repeatedly calling out (vadato vai vasiṣṭhasya muhur muhuḥ): “Fear not (mā bhaiḥ)! Today I shall destroy the son of Gādhi as the sun dispels the morning mist (nāśayāmy adya gādheyaṃ nīhāram iva bhāskaraḥ)!”

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.25 (vadato vai vasiṣṭhasya & nīhāram iva bhāskaraḥ)

Why did the disciples flee in v. 23 when their protector Vasiṣṭha was present? All three commentators construe vadato vai vasiṣṭhasya with vidravanti as a genitive absolute of disregard (ṣaṣṭhī cānādare, Pāṇini 2.3.38): in their panic, they fled in spite of Vasiṣṭha repeatedly crying, “Fear not (mā bhaiḥ)! Today I shall dispel Gādhi’s son as the sun dispels the mist!”

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

एवमुक्त्वा महातेजा वसिष्ठो जपतां वरः ।
विश्वामित्रं तदा वाक्यं सरोषमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ २६ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

एवमुक्त्वा महातेजा वसिष्ठो जपतां वरः ।
विश्वामित्रं तदा वाक्यं सरोषमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ २६ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘जपनेवालोंमें श्रेष्ठ महातेजस्वी वसिष्ठ ऐसा कहकर उस समय विश्वामित्रजीसे रोषपूर्वक बोले—

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

जप माडुववरल्लि श्रेष्ठराद महातपस्वी वसिष्ठरु हीगॆ हेळि, विश्वामित्रनल्लि रोषदिन्द इन्तु नुडिदरु.॥26॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

26. mahaatejaaH japataam varaH vasiSThaH = great-resplendent, the best one among meditators , Vashishta; evam uktvaa = on saying [to evacuees] that way; tadaa = then; * vishvaamitram* = to Vishvamitra; saroSam = with rancour; idam vaakyam abraviit = said this sentence.

अनुवादः

On saying that way to the evacuees, Vashishta, the great-resplendent sage and a best one among best meditators rancorously said this word to Vishvamitra. [1-55-26]

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Having spoken thus to his fleeing disciples, Vasiṣṭha of mighty splendor, the foremost of japa-reciters (japatāṃ varaḥ), then spoke these wrathful words to Viśvāmitra (viśvāmitraṃ tadā vākyaṃ saroṣam idam abravīt):

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.26–27 (cirasaṃvṛddham āśramam & tvaṃ na bhaviṣyasi)

Addressing his fleeing followers first (palāyamānaprāṇijātaṃ prati, Tilaka), Vasiṣṭha turns in righteous anger upon Viśvāmitra: “Because (yad yasmāt) you have destroyed this ancient hermitage and are a deluded transgressor (durācāro mūḍhaḥ), therefore you shall perish (na bhaviṣyasi naṅkṣyasi, Tilaka, Śiromaṇi).”

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

आश्रमं चिरसंवृद्धं यद् विनाशितवानसि ।
दुराचारो हि यन्मूढस्तस्मात् त्वं न भविष्यसि ॥ २७ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

आश्रमं चिरसंवृद्धं यद् विनाशितवानसि ।
दुराचारो हि यन्मूढस्तस्मात् त्वं न भविष्यसि ॥ २७ ॥

मूलम् - VR

आश्रमं चिरसंवृद्धं यद्विनाशितवानसि । दुराचारो हि यन्मूढ तस्मात्त्वं न भविष्यसि ॥ १-५५-२७

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘‘अरे! तूने चिरकालसे पाले-पोसे तथा हरे-भरे किये हुए इस आश्रमको नष्ट कर दिया—उजाड़ डाला, इसलिये तू दुराचारी और विवेकशून्य है और इस पापके कारण तू कुशलसे नहीं रह सकता’ ॥ २७ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

ऎलवो! दीर्घ कालदिन्द साकिद, नळनळिसुत्तिरुव ई आश्रमवन्नु नाशमाडिदॆ, हाळुगॆडहिदॆ, आद्दरिन्द नीनु दुराचारी, विवेकशून्यनागिरुवॆ. ई पापदिन्दागि नीनु क्षेमवागि इरलारॆ.॥27॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

27. muuDha = oh senseless one; cirasamvR^iddham aashramam = the hermitage that is well nourished for a long time; yat = by which reason; vinaashitavaan asi = you have havocked ; yat = by which; duraacaaraH hi = bad conduct [reprehensible one,] indeed you are; tasmaat = thereby; tvam na bhaviSyasi = you, will not, survive.

अनुवादः

You senseless king, by which reason you have havocked this hermitage that is well nourished for a long time by me, for that reason you have become a reprehensible one, and thereby you will not survive any longer. [1-55-27]

टिप्पनी

Vividly: ‘when I have sensibly nourished the trees, birds and animals or disciples, saints and sages, in indoctrinating them the true dharma ‘righteous duty…’ observable by each of the birthed being, what necessitated you to harm the harmless ‘fly anywhere’ birds, ’new to world’ calves and ’take what you need’ trees and animals… you should have confronted me headlong, instead… but you on seeing such a beatitude of this hermitage, you have become envious in your self-aggrandisement… and as said in an old saying like, ‘a senseless reprehensible being, may it be a human, animal, bird, or a reptile is unfit to survive for long…’ you are now transposable with such a senseless being by this act of yours, which you have undertaken with the notion of ‘survival of the fittest…’ thereby I will now show you who is fit enough to survive, an instructor or an intruder, in effacing your survival…’

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

“Since you have destroyed this hermitage nurtured for so long (āśramaṃ cirasaṃvṛddhaṃ yad vināśitavān asi), and since you are a wicked-minded fool (durācāro hi yan mūḍhaḥ), therefore you shall cease to live (tasmāt tvaṃ na bhaviṣyasi)!”

विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः

इत्युक्त्वा परमक्रुद्धो दण्डमुद्यम्य सत्वरः ।
विधूम इव कालाग्निर्यमदण्डमिवापरम् ॥ २८ ॥

सर्वाष् टीकाः
मूलम्

इत्युक्त्वा परमक्रुद्धो दण्डमुद्यम्य सत्वरः ।
विधूम इव कालाग्निर्यमदण्डमिवापरम् ॥ २८ ॥

अनुवाद (हिन्दी)

‘ऐसा कहकर वे अत्यन्त क्रुद्ध हो धूमरहित कालाग्निके समान उद्दीप्त हो उठे और दूसरे यमदण्डके समान भयंकर डंडा हाथमें उठाकर तुरंत उनका सामना करनेके लिये तैयार हो गये’ ॥ २८ ॥

अनुवाद (कन्नड)

हीगॆ हेळि अवरु अत्यन्त कृद्धरागि धूमरहित कालाग्नियन्तॆ उरिदॆद्दु, इन्नॊन्दु यमदण्डदन्तॆ भयङ्कर योगदण्डवन्नु कैयल्लॆत्तिकॊण्डु अवनन्नु ऎदुरिसलु सिद्धवादरु.॥28॥

Desiraju Hanumanta Rao

पदच्छेदः

28. iti uktvaa = thus, on saying; satvaraH = with haste [post-hastily]; paramakruddhaH = extremely infuriated; vidhuumakaalaagniH iva = as with fumeless ultimate fire ; aparam yamadaNDam iva = which is like another Yama’s baton; dandam = a baton; udyamya = Vashishta up heaved.

अनुवादः

“On saying thus that extremely infuriated Sage Vashishta post-hastily up-heaved his baton, which is alike the fumeless Ultimate Fire of End Time, and the other baton of Yama, the Terminator, and affronted Vishvamitra.” Thus, Sage Shataananda continued his narration of Vishvamitra’s legend. [1-55-28]

समाप्तिः

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये बालकाण्डे पञ्चपञ्चाशः सर्गः ॥ ५५ ॥
इस प्रकार श्रीवाल्मीकिनिर्मित आर्षरामायण आदिकाव्यके बालकाण्डमें पचपनवाँ सर्ग पूरा हुआ ॥ ५५ ॥

अनुवाद (समाप्तिः) (कन्नड)

वाल्मीकि विरचित आर्ष रामायण आदिकाव्यद बालकाण्डदल्लि ऐवत्तैदनॆय सर्ग पूर्णवायितु. ॥55॥

English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

Speaking thus in supreme wrath (ity uktvā paramakruddhaḥ), blazing like the smokeless fire of Doomsday (vidhūma iva kālāgniḥ), he swiftly raised his ascetic staff like a second rod of Yama (daṇḍam udyamya satvaraḥ yamadaṇḍam ivāparam).

टीका-notes (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)

55.28 (vidhūma iva kālāgnir yamadaṇḍam ivāparam)

Blazing like the smokeless fire of cosmic dissolution (vidhūmaḥ kālāgnir iva), Vasiṣṭha swiftly raises his ascetic staff and stands ready for battle (daṇḍam udyamya tasthāv iti śeṣaḥ, Tilaka, Govindarāja) like a second rod of Yama.
