109. परमधाम जानेके लिये निकले हुए श्रीरामके साथ समस्त अयोध्यावासियोंका प्रस्थान

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका</summary>

<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

At daybreak Rāma directs Vasiṣṭha to send the blazing Agnihotra fire and Vājapeya parasol ahead and perform the entire Mahāprasthāna rite; clad in fine linen, reciting the supreme Brahman with kuśa grass in hand, Rāma emerges radiant as the sun, flanked by Śrī, Hrī, and steadfast Resolve.
</details>
</details>

फिर भगवान् श्रीराम सूक्ष्म वस्त्र धारण किये दोनों हाथोंमें कुश लेकर परब्रह्मके प्रतिपादक वेद-मन्त्रोंका उच्चारण करते हुए सरयूनदीके तटपर चले ॥ ४ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

</details>


<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Uttering not a word anywhere (_avyāharan kvacit kiñcit_), motionless in his senses and indifferent to comfort on the path (_niśceṣṭo niḥsukhaḥ pathi_), he issued forth from that palace blazing like the sun (_dīpyamāna ivāṃśumān_).
</details>
</details>

At Rāma’s right side waited Śrī, holding her lotus (_dakṣiṇe pārśve sapadmā śrīr apāśritā_); at his left walked the great goddess Hrī (_savye tu hrīr mahādevī_), and before him went Vyavasāya [Cosmic Resolve] (_vyavasāyas tathāgrataḥ_).
</details>
</details>
