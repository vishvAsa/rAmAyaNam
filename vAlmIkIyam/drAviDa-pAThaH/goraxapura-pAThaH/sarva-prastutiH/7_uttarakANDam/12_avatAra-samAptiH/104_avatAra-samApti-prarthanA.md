104. कालका श्रीरामचन्द्रजीको ब्रह्माजीका संदेश सुनाना और श्रीरामका उसे स्वीकार करना

<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका</summary>

<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Revealing himself as Time, Rāma’s own son born of māyā in his primordial state, the envoy delivers Pitāmaha’s message, recalling how the Lord once withdrew the worlds, reclined upon the waters, and generated Brahmā, Ananta, and Madhu-Kaiṭabha.
</details>
</details>

</details>

**6:** “‘—both Madhu and Kaiṭabha (_madhuṃ ca kaiṭabhaṃ caiva_), with whose heaps of bones covered over (_yayor asthi-cayair vṛtā_), this earth crowded with mountains became _Medinī_ (_iyaṃ parvata-sambādhā medinī cābhavan mahī_)—
