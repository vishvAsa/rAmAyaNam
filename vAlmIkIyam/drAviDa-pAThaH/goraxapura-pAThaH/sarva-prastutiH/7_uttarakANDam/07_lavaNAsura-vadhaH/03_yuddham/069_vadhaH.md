<details><summary>सर्वाष् टीकाः - अवतारिका</summary>

<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Enraged by Śatrughna’s defiance, Lavaṇa slaps his palms, grinds his teeth, and challenges him repeatedly; Śatrughna retorts that he is not cut from the mould of Lavaṇa’s earlier victims and vows that sages and citizens will soon behold the demon slain like Rāvaṇa.
</details>
</details>

क्रोधमाहारयत् तीव्रं तिष्ठ तिष्ठेति चाब्रवीत् ॥ १ ॥

महामना शत्रुघ्नका वह भाषण सुनकर लवणासुरको बड़ा क्रोध हुआ और बोला—‘अरे! खड़ा रह, खड़ा रह’ ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

हीगॆ कथॆ हेळुत्ता, शुभ विजयद आकाङ्क्षॆ माडुत्तिरुव आ मुनिगळ मातन्नु केळुत्ता - केळुत्ता महात्मा शत्रुघ्नन आ रात्रॆ कळॆदु होयितु.॥1॥
</details>


<details><summary>English (Gemini 4, itihasa.io)</summary>

Hearing that speech of the great-souled Śatrughna (_tac chrutvā bhāṣitaṃ tasya śatrughnasya mahātmanaḥ_), he summoned up fierce fury (_krodham āhārayat tīvram_) and cried out, “Stand! Stand!” (_tiṣṭha tiṣṭheti cābravīt_).
</details>
</details>

Clapping palm fiercely against palm (_pāṇau pāṇiṃ viniṣpiṣya_) and gnashing his teeth (_dantān kaṭakaṭāyya ca_), Lavaṇa challenged that tiger among the Raghus again and again (_lavaṇo raghu-śārdūlam āhvayām āsa cāsakṛt_).
</details>
</details>

</details>
</details>

</details>
