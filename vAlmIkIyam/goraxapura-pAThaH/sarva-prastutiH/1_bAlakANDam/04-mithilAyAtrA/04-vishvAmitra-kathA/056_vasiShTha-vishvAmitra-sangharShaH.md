<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro</summary>

**Chapter [Sarga] 56**

Vashishta nullifies Vishvamitra's missiles just by his Brahma's baton. All missiles
are defused when Vashishta consumed them. Vishvamitra launches the highest missile,
namely Brahma-missile, which too is consumed by Vashishta, whereby the body of Vashishta
becomes a Brahma missile and starts to emit radiation. Then, upon the prayer of
gods and saints, Vashishta withdraws the effect of that missile. Vishvamitra on
seeing this concludes that mere missiles are of no use and thus he embarks on a
sublime ascesis for Brahma-hood.

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. mahaabalaH vishvaamitraH* = great-mighty, Vishvamitra; *vasiSThena
evam uktaH* = Vashishta spoken in this way; *aagneyam astram utkSipya*
= brandishing fiery missile; *tiSTha tiSTha* = withstand, withstand [this
missile]; *iti abraviit ca* = thus, said [challenged,] also.

**अनुवादः**

When Vashishta spoke in this way, the great-mighty Vishvamitra brandishing Fiery-missile
challenged Vashishta saying, "withstand this, withstand this..." Thus, sage Shataananda
continued his narration of Vishvamitra's legend. [1-56-1]

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. kShatrabandhaH* = oh meritless Kshatriya; *eSaH* = this me;
*sthitaH asmi* = I am staying; *yat balam* = which, prowess - you
have; *tat vidarshaya* = that, you display entirely; *gaadhija*
= oh one born to Gaadhi - Vishvamitra; *adya te darpam* = now, your,
vanity; *tava shastrasya [darpam]* = your, missile's, [vanity]; *naashayaami*
= I will ruin.

**अनुवादः**

I am staying here only, you meritless Kshatriya, you may clearly display whatever
prowess you have, oh, Gaadhi's son Vishvamitra, I will now ruin whatever vanity
you or your missiles have. [1-56-3]

**पदच्छेदः**

*5. tasya gaadhiputrasya* = his, Gaadhi son's; *uttamam ghoram tat aagneyam
astram* = matchless, deadly, that, Fire, missile; *[udyatam* = projected,
bolted]; *ambhasaa agneH vega iva* = by water, fire's, speed [rage,] as [silenced]

With his baton of Brahma Vashishta silenced that matchless and deadly Fiery-missile
of Gaadhi's son, namely Vishvamitra, as a deluge of water silencing a rage of fire.

**पदच्छेदः**

*6. ruSitaH gaadhinandanaH* = rancorously, Gaadhi's son; *vaaruNam ca eva*
= Varuna missile, also thus; *raudram ca* = Rudra missile, also; *aindram*
= Indra missile; *tathaa* = like that; *paashupatam* = Paashupata
missile; *aiSiikam ca api* = Ishiika, also, even; *cikSepa* = fusilladed.

**अनुवादः**

Vishvamitra, the son of Gaadhi then rancorously fusilladed the missiles regulated
by gods like Varuna, Rudra, Indra, Paashupata, and even a missile which is projectile
through grass blade called Ishiika. [1-56-6]

**टिप्पनी**

The accounts of these missiles are narrated in chapters 27, 28 of this canto Bala,
where Vishvamitra accords these very missiles to Rama. Hence, their details are
not reiterated here but just catalogued.
