<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro</summary>

**Book I : Bala Kanda - The Youthful Majesties**

Sage Vishvamitra along with others reach the banks of River Ganga and
 they make their sojourn on that riverbank. There when Rama inquisitively
 enquires about River Ganga Vishvamitra narrates the legend of Ganga,
 as to how she is taken to heavens by gods from her father Himalayas.

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. vishvaamitraH* = Viswamitra; *raatrisheSam* = remaining night
 ; *maharSibhiH shoNaakuule upaasya* = on sojourning with great-sages
 on the bank of Sona [the river] ; *nishaayaam suprabhaataayaam*
 = night elapsing, sunny daybreak; *abhyabhaaSata* = spoke.

**अनुवादः**

On sojourning the remaining night on the bank of river Sona along with
 great-sages, Viswamitra spoke when that night is elapsing into a sunny
 daybreak. [1-35-1]

 *te bhadram* = to you, let safe be; *gamanaaya abhirocaya*

 in, hence awake and arise, you be safe, ready yourself for further travel."

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. brahman* = oh Brahman; *shubhajalaH* = one with
 auspicious waters; *pulinamaNDitaH* = decorated with - overspread with sand-dunes,
 ; *ayam shoNaH* = this, [river] Sona; *agaadhaH*
 = so deep; *vayam katareNa pathaa* = we, in which
 of the two, ways; *samtariSyaamahe* = very, easily cross it
 over - rather, without a boat

**अनुवादः**

"This River Sona is with auspicious waters and even adorned with dunes,
 where it is so deep. Oh, Brahman, in which of the two ways, namely
 by fordless waters or by fordable water with dunes, we have to cross
 this river?" [1-35-4]

**टिप्पनी**

The three-way coursing of the river is firstly as Ganga in the lap of
 Himalayas. Then when she cruised heavenward with divinities as Deva

 all moving things etc., and the next is her entry to heaven, and nextly
 her coming to earth. And some say that gods have not left her in middle
