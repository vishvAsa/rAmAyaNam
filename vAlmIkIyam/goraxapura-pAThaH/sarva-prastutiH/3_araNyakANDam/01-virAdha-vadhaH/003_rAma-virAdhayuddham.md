**Book III : Aranya Kanda - Forest
Treck**

Rama and Lakshmana enter into a dialogue with Viradha, the demon and shower arrows on him. Later, Viradha, the demon carries
Rama and Lakshmana away from Seetha.

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. atha* = then afterwards; *viraadhaH vanam puurayan* = Viradha, forest, filling; *punaH vaakyam uvaacha*
= in turn, sentence said; *pR^icChataH mama bruu tam* = while I question, to me, you reply; *kau yuvaam* =
who, you two are; *kva gamiSyathaH* = whereto, wish to go; *hi* = really.

**अनुवादः**

Then in his turn that demon Viradha said this sentence filling the forest with his gruesome voice, "I am asking you, really...
tell me who you are and whereto you wish to go? " [3-3-1]

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

3. *nau* us two; *vR^itta sampannau* = bearing, ennobled behaviour; *kshatriyau* = as Kshatriya-s; *vanagocharau*
= as forest, trekkers; *viddhi* = you know; *tvaam tu veditum icChaavaH* = about you, but, to know, we wish
to; *dandakaan charasi tvam kaH* = in Dandaka, you move about, you, who.

**अनुवादः**

"Know us as Kshatriya-s with ennobled bearing, and we are trekking the forest...but we wish to know about you, who are you
that move about in Dandaka forest? [3-3-3]

**पदच्छेदः**

*6. tapasaa* = by ascesis; *brahmaNaH hi prasaada ja* = Brahma's, only, from beneficence, derived of; *
abhi sampraaptaa* = I have obtained [a boon]; *loke shashtreNa a vadhyataa ca* = in world, by weapon, not, to
be killed, and even; *a cChedya a bhedya ca eva* = not, be cut, not, to be slit, also, thus.

**अनुवादः**

"On my undertaking ascesis I derived a boon from the beneficence of Brahma, by which I will neither be cut, nor slit, nor
killed with a weapon in this world..."[3-3-6]
