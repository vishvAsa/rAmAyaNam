**Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War**

On Rama's enquiry, Vibhishana explains the prowess of Prahasta who entered the
battle-field. The demons with their arrows and the monkeys with their stones combat each other.
Both the monkeys and demons were killed in the battle. Prahasta the demon and Nila the monkey come
face to face to fight. Prahasta hurls a volley of darts over Nila. Nila kills Prahasta's horses by
hurling a huge tree and breaks his bow. Finally a huge rock hurled by Nila breaks Prahasta's head
into myriad pieces and dies. All the other demons abandon the battle-field and return to Lanka.

3; *4. shrutvaa* = hearing; *raaghavasya* = Rama's vachaH =
words; *vibhiiSaNaH* = Vibhishana; *pratyuvaacha* = replied (as follows);
*eSaH* = He; *tribhaaga samvR^itaH* = who is guarded by two thirds of the army;
*laN^kaayaam* = in Lanka; *viiryavaan* = a demon of prowess; *astravit* =

**अनुवादः**

Hearing Rama's words, Vibhishana replied as follows "He is guarded by two-thirds of
the army of Lanka. He is a demon of prowess, skilled in the use of mystic weapons and valiant. He
is the demon named Prahasta, the Army general of Ravana."

समेत्य रामेण सलक्ष्मणेन ।
प्रहृष्ट रूपस् तु बभूव यूथपः ॥ ६-५८-६०
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

सेनानायकके मारे जानेसे वे सारे राक्षस अपना युद्धविषयक उत्साह खो बैठे और राक्षसराज रावणके भवनमें जाकर चिन्ताके कारण चुपचाप खड़े हो गये । तीव्र शोक-समुद्रमें डूब जानेके कारण वे सब-के-सब अचेत-से हो गये थे ॥ ५९-६० ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

सेनानायकनु मरण हॊन्दिदाग राक्षसरॆल्लरू युद्धोत्साहवन्नु कळॆदुकॊण्डु, राक्षसेन्द्र रावणन भवनक्कॆ होगि चिन्तॆयिन्दागि सुम्मनॆ निन्तुकॊण्डिद्दरु. तीव्र शोकसमुद्रदल्लि मुळुगिद्दरिन्द अवरॆल्लरू सत्तन्ते आगिद्दरु.॥59-60॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*60. vijayii* = the triumphant; *yuuthapaH* = warrior;
*niilaH* = Nila; *tataH* = thereafter; *prashasya maanaH* = was honoured;
*raameNa* = by Rama; *sametya* = joined; *salakSmaNena* = with Lakshmana;
*karmaNaa* = for his task; *sukR^itena* = accomplished well; *babhuuva* = and
experienced; *prahR^iSTa ruupaH* = supreme joy.

**अनुवादः**

The triumphant Nila, however, was honoured by Rama and Lakshmana for his task well
accomplished and experienced supreme joy.

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*59. tasmin* = that Prahaste; *chamuumukhye* = the Chief of
Army; *hate* = having been slain; *te raakSasaaH* = those demons; *dhyaana
muukatvam* = dull dumb; *nirudyamaah* = and inactive; *gatvaa* = regained;

*visamjN^aaH iva* = unconscious as it were; *praaptaaH* = plunged; *shokaarNavam

inactive, regained the abode of their king. They became unconscious as it were plunged in an ocean
of burning grief.
