<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War**

Hanuma recounts to Bharata, broad details relating to the soujourn of Rama, Seetha
and Lakshmana in the forest from the day Bharata takes wooden sandals of Rama, till the day Ravana
is killed.

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. iyam* = this; *laukikii* = common; *gathaa* = saying;
*pratibhaati* = sounds; *maam* = to me; *kalyaaNii* = good; *aanandaH*
= that bliss; *eti* = comes; *jiivantam naram* = to a surviving man;
*varShashataadapi* = even if it be after a hundred years!.

**अनुवादः**

"This common saying sounds to me good, that bliss comes to a surviving man, even if
it be after a hundred years!"

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. katham* = how; *kim aashritya* = and being supported by
what; *samaagamaH* = an alliance; *aasiit* = came to be concluded; *raaghavasya
acha kapiinaamcha* = between Rama and he monkeys; *kashmin deshe* = and at what place?;
*aakhyaahi tattvam* = tell the truth to me; *pR^ichchhataH* = enquiring as I am.

**अनुवादः**

"How and on what ground, an alliance has been concluded between Rama and the monkeys
and at what place? Tell the truth to me, enquiring as I am."
