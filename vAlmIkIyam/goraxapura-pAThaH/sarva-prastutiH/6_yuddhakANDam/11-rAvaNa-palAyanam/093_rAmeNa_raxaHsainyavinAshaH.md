**VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of
Chapter [Sarga] 93**

As per Ravana's command, his army marches forward to the battle-field, so as to
fight fiercely with Rama and his army with their weapon. When the demons attack the monkeys with
their various weapons fiercely, they seek refuge in Rama. Rama destroys the troops of demons in the
battle-field swiftly.

*parama duHkhitaH* = and very much distressed; *sabhaam pravishya* = having entered
the assembly-hall; *niShasaada* = sat; *mukhya aasane* = on his pre-eminent seat;

**अनुवादः**

That king, who was dejected and very much distressed, having entered the
ssembly-hall, sat on his pre-eminent seat, snorting like a lion.

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. pariShipya* = encircling; *raamam* = Rama; *ekam* =
alone; *arhatha* = you ought; *hantum* = to kill (him); *varShantaH* = by
showering; *shara varShaaNi* = streams of arrows; *ambudaaH iva* = even as clouds;
*(pour showers) praavR^iTkaale* = during the rainy season.

**अनुवादः**

"Encircling Rama alone, you have to kill him, by showering streams of arrows upon
him, even as clouds pour showers during the rainy season."
