**VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of
Chapter [Sarga] 97**

Ravana sends Mahodara to the battle-field. Mahodara creates havoc, by cutting off
the hands, feet and thighs of monkeys. Some monkeys seek refuge with Sugreeva and some ran away for
life. Sugreeva attacks Mahodara and initially kills the horses of the latter. Both of them fight
till the weapons in their hands get broken. Then, they start wrestling with each other, till they
get exhausted. Finally, Sugreeva cuts off Mahodara's head with a huge sword.

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. bale* = those two armies; *hanyamaane* = being killed;
*anyonyam* = by each another; *mahaamR^idhe* = in that major battle;
*babhuuvatuH* = became; *suupakShiiNe* = diminished very much; *tuurNam* =
quickly; *sarasiiva* = like two lakes; (diminishing); *mahaagharme* = in a severe
summer.

**अनुवादः**

Being killed by each other in that major battle, those two armies were very much
diminished soon, like two lakes in a severe summer.

and to see the reversal of his fate.
