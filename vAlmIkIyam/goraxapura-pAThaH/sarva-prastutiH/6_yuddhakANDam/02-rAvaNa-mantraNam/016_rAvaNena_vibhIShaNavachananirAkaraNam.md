**Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War**

Ravana refuses to hear the words of Vibhishana and rebukes him with harsh words.
Vibhishana gets angry and sets off, to leave Ravana.

*2. vaset* = one can stay; *sapatnena saha* = with an enemy;
*ashiivishheNa* = or with a snake; *kruddhena* = which is enraged; *tu* = but;

3. O; *demon! GYaami* = I know shiilam = the conduct;
*gyaatiinaam* = of kinsmen; *sadaa* = always; *hR^ishhyanti* = are rejoiced;

**अनुवादः**

"O, demon! I know the conduct of kinsmen in all the worlds. These kinsmen always are
rejoiced at the calamities of their kinsmen"

*4. raakshhasa* = O;demon; *n^yaatayaH* = kinsmen;
*avamanyante hi* = indeed insult; *paribhavanticha* = and even humiliate;

**पदच्छेदः**

*5. ghoraaH* = the horrible; *G^yaataH* = kinsmen;
*prachchhanna hR^idayaaH* = with their concealed thoughts; *aatataayinaH* = and
treacherous minds; *bhayaavahaaH* = terrific ones as they are; *nityam* = always;
*anyonyasamhR^ishhTaaH* = rejoice each other; *vyasaneshhu* = in adversities."

**अनुवादः**

"The horrible kinsmen with their concealed thoughts and treacherous minds, terrific
ones as they are, always rejoice each other in adversities."
