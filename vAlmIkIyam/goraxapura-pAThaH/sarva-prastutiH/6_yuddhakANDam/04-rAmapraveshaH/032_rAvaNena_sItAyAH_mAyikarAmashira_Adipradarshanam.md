<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War**

Seetha was shocked, fell unconscious at the sight of Rama's head and bow. She regained
consciousness and asked Ravana to kill her also, so that she would join her husband in heaven. Meanwhile,
Ravana is abruptly called away by an emissary and after consultation with his ministers, prepares his
army for action against the forces of Rama.

1; 2; *3. dR^ishhTva* = seeing; *tat* = that; *shiraH* = head;
*tat* = that; *uttamam* = excellent; *kaarmukamcha* = bow; *sugriivapratisamsargam*

= by Hanuman; *nayane* = the eyes; *mukhavarNamcha* = facial complexion; *sadR^isham*
= resembling; *bhartuH* = (those of) her husband; *tat* = that; *mukham* = face;
*kesham* = the hair; *keshaanta deshamcha* = the expanse of his fore-head; *tam*
= that; *shubham* = beautiful; *chuuDaa maNimcha* = jewel worn on the top of his head;
*abhijN^aaya* = and recognizing; *sarvaiH* = all; *etaiH* = these; *abhijN^aanaiH*
= marks of proof; *saa siitaa* = that Sita; *suduHkhitaa* = was very much afflicted with

**अनुवादः**

Seetha saw the illusory head and bow. She heard Ravana narrating about Rama's friendly
relationship with Sugreeva, as earlier apprised by Hanuman. Recognizing that head as that of Rama, with
a proof resembling her husband's eyes, facial complexion, hair, expanse of his forehead and the beautiful
jewel worn on the top of his head, she was very much afflicted with sorrow, cried like an osprey and
abused Kaikeyi who was the originator of the present calamity (as follows):
