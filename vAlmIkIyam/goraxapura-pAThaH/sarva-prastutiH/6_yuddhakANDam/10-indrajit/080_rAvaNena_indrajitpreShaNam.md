<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War**

Ravana instructs Indrajit to proceed to the battle field. Before proceeding t the
battle, Indrajit performs ceremorial oblations into a sacred fire and obtains a capacity of going
out of sight while fighting. He proceeds to the battle-field and releases a flood of arrows towards
Rama and Lakshmana, while himself remaining invisible in the sky. With a network of arrows,
Indrajit creates a darkness in the sky and showers a multitude of steel arrows towards Rama and
Lakshmana. Sharp golden shafts arrows are then released by Rama and Lakshmana. Struck by the arrows
discharged by Indrajit, monkeys in hundreds fall down dead. Then, Rama and Lakshmana reflect on the
various ways and means to destroy Indrajit.

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/yuddha/sarga80/yuddhasans80.htm)

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. viira* = O hero!; *adR^ishyaH* = either being invisible;
*dR^ishyamaanevaa* = or visible; (yourself); *jahi* = kill; *raamalakShmaNau*
= Rama and Lakshmana; *bhraatarau* = the brothers; *mahaaviiryau* = of great prowes;
*tvam* = you; *balaadhikaH* = are superior in strength; *sarvathaa* = by all
means.

**अनुवादः**

"O hero! Either remaining yourself invisible or visible, kill Rama and Lakshmana,
the brothers of great prowess. You are superior in strength by all means."

Indra; *apratimakarmaaNam* = of incomparable deeds; *samyuge* = in battle; *kim

poured oblations into the sacred fire, with due ceremony, on the sacrificial ground.

weapons along with lances, the demon seized hold of the neck of a live goat, entirely black and
consigned it to the fire.

राक्षसं निहनिष्यन्ति दृष्ट्वा वानरयूथपाः ॥ ४१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘यह मायावी राक्षस बड़ा नीच है । इसने अन्तर्धान-शक्तिसे अपने रथको छिपा लिया है । यदि यह दीख जाय तो वानरयूथपति इस राक्षसको अवश्य मार डालेंगे ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

ई मायावी राक्षसनु बहळ नीचनागिद्दानॆ. इवनु अन्तर्धान शक्तियिन्द तन्न रथवन्नु अडगिसिरुवनु. इवनु कण्डुबन्दरॆ वानर दळपतिगळु ई राक्षसनन्नु खण्डितवागि कॊन्दु हाकुवरु.॥41॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*41. dR^iShTvaa* = on seeing; *enam* = this; *raakShasam*
= demon; tam as such; *vaanarayuuthapaaH* = the leaders of monkey-troops; *balaat* =
will forcibly nihaniShyati = kill; *kShudram* = this petty demon; *maayinam* = who is
skilled in conjuring tricks; *antarhita ratham* = and whose chariot remains invisible.

**अनुवादः**

"On seeing this demons, the leaders of mokey-troops will forcibly kill this petty
demon, who is skilled in conjuring tricks and whose chariot remains invisible."

</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘यदि यह पृथ्वीमें समा जाय, स्वर्गको चला जाय, रसातलमें प्रवेश करे अथवा आकाशमें ही स्थित रहे तथापि इस तरह छिपे होनेपर भी मेरे अस्त्रोंसे दग्ध होकर प्राणशून्य हो भूतलपर अवश्य गिरेगा’ ॥ ४२ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

इवनु भूमियॊळगॆ सेरिकॊण्डरू, स्वर्गक्कॆ होदरू, रसातळवन्नु प्रवेशिसिदरू, अथवा आकाशदल्ले स्थितनागि हीगे अडगिद्दरू नन्न अस्त्रगळिन्द दग्धनागि प्राणशून्यनागि भूमिगॆ अवश्यवागि बीळुवनु.॥42॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*42. mama astradagdhaH* = scorched by my mystic missiles;
*patiShyati gataasuH* = he will fall dead; *bhuumitale* = on the ground; *eShaH
vishate yadi* = even if he enters; *bhuumim* = the earth; *divam vaa* = or into
heaven; *rasaatalam vaa* = or into the subterranean world; *nabhasthalam vaa* = or
penetrates the vault of heaven; *niguuDho.api* = and remains completely hidden;
*evam* = in this manner.

**अनुवादः**

"Scorched by my mystic missiles, he will fall dead on the ground, even if he enters
the earth or into heaven or into the subterrranean world or penetrates the vault of heaven and
remains completely hidden in this manner."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

इस प्रकार महान् अभिप्रायसे युक्त वचन कहकर वानर शिरोमणियोंसे घिरे हुए रघुकुलके प्रमुख वीर महात्मा श्रीरामचन्द्रजी उस क्रूरकर्मा भयानक राक्षसका वध करनेके लिये तत्काल ही इधर-उधर दृष्टिपात करने लगे ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

हीगॆ महार्थयुक्तवाद मातुगळन्नु हेळि वानर श्रेष्ठरिन्द परिवृतनाद रघुकुलद प्रमुख वीर महात्मा श्रीरामनु आ क्रूरकर्म भयानक राक्षसनन्नु वधिसलु अत्त इत्त नोडतॊडगिदनु.॥43॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*43. uktvaa* = speaking; *mahaartham* = highly meaningful;
*vachanam* = words; *ityevam* = in the aforesaid manner; *mahaatmaa* = the
great-souled; *raghupraviiraH* = Rama; *vR^itaH* = surrounded;
*plavagarShabhaiH* = by the foremost of monkeys; *tadaa* = then; *nirikShate*
= started to reflect; *tvaritam* = on the speedy means; *vadhaaya* = of killing;
*raudrasya* = the furious; *nR^ishamsa karmaNaH* = Indrajit; the demon of cruel
deeds.

**अनुवादः**

Speaking highly meaningful words in the aforesaid manner, the great-souled Rama,
surrounded by the foremost of monkeys, started to reflect on the speedy means of killing the
furious Indrajit, the demons of cruel deeds.
