<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro</summary>

**Book IV
: Kishkindha Kanda - The Empire of Holy Monkeys**

Vanara-s deliverance from black hole is effectuated by Sainted Lady Swayamprabha. On listening to Hanuma about
the plight of monkeys by which they entered this cavity in searching for Seetha, Sainted Lady Swayamprabha becomes
sympathetic and offers guestship. When entreated by Hanuma for an exit from that incarceration, and as the time
frame set by Sugreeva is lapsed in this very cavity, Swayamprabha uses her ascetic powers to transport the monkeys
from that cave, which exit is otherwise impossible for any other intruder, in his aliveness.

</details>

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. atha* = then; *dharma caariNii* = she who in probity, pursuer of; *eka agraa* = single-mindedly
concentrative; *taapasii* = sainted lady; *vishraantaan* = when relaxed; *taan sarvaan hari yuuthapaan*

**अनुवादः**

When those monkey commanders are relaxed after refreshments, then that sainted lady, who concentrates single-mindedly
and who is a pursuer of probity, spoke this word to all of them. [4-52-1]

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. vaanaraaH* = oh, Vanara-s; *phala bhakSaNaat* = fruits, by devouring; *vaH khedaH* =
your, enervation; *pranaSTaH yadi* = eased off, if; *etat* = all that [episode of yours]; *mayaa
shraavyam yadi* = by me, listenable, if; *[taam* = that]; *kathataam* = episode; *shrotum

"Oh, vanara-s, if your enervation is eased off by devouring fruits, and if I may listen to that episode of yours,
I wish to listen to it." Thus Swayamprabha addressed the monkeys. [4-52-2]

*4, 5. sarvasya lokasya raajaa* = of all, world, king; *mahendra varuNa upamaH* = Mahendra, Rain-god,
who is similar to; *daasharathiH* = son of Dasharatha; *shriimaan raamaH* = illustrious one, Rama;

**अनुवादः**

Dandaka forest with his bother Lakshmana, even along with his wife Vaidehi, but Ravana coercively abducted his
wife from Janasthaana. [4-52-4, 5]

इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये किष्किन्धाकाण्डे द्विपञ्चाशः सर्गः ॥ ५२ ॥  
इस प्रकार श्रीवाल्मीकिनिर्मित आर्षरामायण आदिकाव्यके किष्किन्धाकाण्डमें बावनवाँ सर्ग पूरा हुआ ॥ ५२ ॥
</details>
