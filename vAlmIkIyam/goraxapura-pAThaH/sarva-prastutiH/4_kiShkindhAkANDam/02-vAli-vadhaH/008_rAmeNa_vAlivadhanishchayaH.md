**Book
IV : Kishkindha Kanda - The Empire of Holy Monkeys**

Sugreeva gives a report to Rama about his plight for fear of Vali, who made his own
brother an enemy and abducted Sugreeva's wife. Rama asks Sugreeva to inform him about the real
cause of enmity, so that he can decide correctly. Sugreeva narrates his woeful condition.

**अनुवादः**

Lakshmana's elder brother, Rama the valiant one. [4-8-1]

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

महात्मा श्रीरामचन्द्रनु हीगॆ हेळिदाग सुग्रीवनिगॆ तन्न नाल्कु मन्दि वानररॊन्दिगॆ अपार हर्ष उण्टायितु.॥45॥
</details>

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*45. mahaatmanaa kaakutsthena evam uktaH tu* = by great souled, Rama,
thus, when said; *sugriivaH* = he that Sugreeva; *caturbhiH vaanaraiH saha* = with
four, vanara-s, along with; *praharSam atulam lebhe* = rejoice, very much, obtained.

**अनुवादः**

Thus said by the great souled Rama, Sugreeva is very much rejoiced along with four
of vanara-s available in his attendance. [4-8-45]

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

तदनन्तर सुग्रीवके मुखपर प्रसन्नता छा गयी और उन्होंने श्रीरामको वालीके साथ वैर होनेका यथार्थ कारण बताना आरम्भ किया ॥ ४६ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

अनन्तर सुग्रीवन मुखदल्लि प्रसन्नतॆ आवरिसितु मत्तु अवनु वालियॊन्दिगॆ वैर उण्टाद यथार्थ कारणवन्नु श्रीरामनल्लि हेळलु प्रारम्भिसिदनु.॥46॥
</details>

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*46. tata* = then; *prahR^iSTa vadanaH* = with pleased,
countenance; Sugreeva; *Lakshmana agraje* = to Lakshmana's elder, to Rama; *vairasya
kaaraNam* = enmity's, cause; *tattvam* = truthfully; *aakhyaatum* = to tell;
*upacakrame* = started.

**अनुवादः**

Then with a pleased countenance Sugreeva started to tell Rama about the cause of the
enmity, it all it truthfulness. [4-8-46]
