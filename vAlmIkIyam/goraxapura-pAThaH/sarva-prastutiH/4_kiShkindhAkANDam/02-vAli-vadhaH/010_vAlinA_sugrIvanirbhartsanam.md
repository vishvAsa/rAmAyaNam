**Book
IV : Kishkindha Kanda - The Empire of Holy Monkeys**

Sugreeva informs Rama that Vali could not be appeased in spite of repeated requests
as he is resolved to accept Sugreeva as an enemy. Vali banishes Sugreeva from the kingdom with the
same thinking.

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4, 5, 6a. nR^ipa* = oh, king; *aartaH* = saddened [I was];
*samvatsaram* = for one year; *bila dvaarii sthitaH* = at that, cave's entrance, I
stayed; *atha bilaat samutthitam shoNitam dvaari dR^iSTvaa* = then, from cave, started to
flow, blood, at entrance, having seen; *shoka samvigna hR^idayaH* = sadness, engulfed, with
a heart; *bhR^isham vyaakulita indriyaH* = much, perturbed, senses; *tadaa tat bila
dvaaram* = then, that, cave's, entrance; *shaila shringeNa apidhaaya* = with a hill,
top, having covered; *tasmaat deshaat apaakramya* = from that, place, moving away; *punaH
kiSkindhaam praavisham* = again, Kishkindha, I entered.

**अनुवादः**

"Saddened I was after staying at the entrance of the cave for one year, oh, king,
and on seeing the blood that started to flow from out the cave in the entrance my heart is engulfed
in sadness and my senses perturbed very much, and then covering that cave's entrance with a
hilltop, and departing from that country, I re-entered Kishkindha. [4-10-4, 5, 6a]
