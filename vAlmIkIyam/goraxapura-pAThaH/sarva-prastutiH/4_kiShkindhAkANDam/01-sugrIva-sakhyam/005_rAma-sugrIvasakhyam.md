<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro</summary>

**Book IV : Kishkindha Kanda -
The Kingdom of Holy Monkeys**

Hanuma fetches Rama and Lakshmana to Sugreeva, and advises him to befriend Rama. Accordingly Rama and
Sugreeva take the oath of friendship before an altar of fire and Rama assures to eliminate Vali the
vice, from the face of earth.

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*7. tau bhraatarau* = those two, brothers; *raama lakSmaNau* = Rama, Lakshmana; *bhavataa
sakhya kaamau* = with you, friendship, interested; *puujaniiyatamau* = most venerable are;
*imau* = them; *ubhau* = two; *pragR^ihya* = you receive them; *arcayasva*
= worship.

**अनुवादः**

"These two brothers Rama and Lakshmana, are interested to make friendship with you... thus you receive
these two and worship them, for they are most venerable ones..." Hanuma thus advised Sugreeva. [4-5-7]

**टिप्पनी**

Here Hanuma's role as teacher is depicted. God comes to the rescue of the needy, and communicates through
a competent teacher. Only the teacher can visualize the god and his intent, and thus narrate it to the
taught. That is what Hanuma's advise to Sugreeva.

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

31. Sugreeva; Rama; *praNaya* = friendly; *prasa~Nge* = conversation; *siita* =
Seetha's; *kapi indra* = monkeys, lord's [Vali's]; *kshaNadaa charaanaam* = night, walker's
[Ravana's]; *raajiiva* = lotus; *hema* = golden; *jvalana* = fireball; *upamaanaani*
= in similitude; *vaamaani* = left side; *netraaNi* = eyes; *samam* = equally;
*sphuranti* = fluttered.

**अनुवादः**

During the friendly conversation of Rama and Sugreeva, the left eyes of Seetha, Vali and Ravana that
bear similitude with lotuses, golden orbs, and fireballs respectively have fluttered equally. [4-5-31]

**टिप्पनी**

This verse is in* krama alankaara*, an equated metaphoric expression, by juxtaposing words equally to
compare Seetha's eyes with lotuses, Vali's eyes with golden-balls for his complexion is golden, and
Ravana's eyes with fireballs. But all are left eyes only that have fluttered. The flutter of left eye
to the male is a bad omen while to the female it is a good omen. Hence the friendship of Rama and Sugreeva
is the seedling point for eradication of vice, namely Vali and Ravana, from face of the earth.
