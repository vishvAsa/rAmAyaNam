<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao - Intro</summary>

**Book
IV : Kishkindha Kanda - The Empire of Holy Monkeys**

The war cries of monkey soldiers in their enthusiasm to conquer Ravana are uttered
in the audience of Sugreeva, when all of them started to their designated directions. This suggests
the *sugriiva aaj~na* 'the inviolable order of Sugreeva.'

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1, 2a. plavaga rSabhaH* = of fly-jumper, the best one; *raajaa
sugriivaH* = king [of monkeys,] Sugreeva; *sarvaaH plavagaan aahuuya* = all of them,
fly-jumpers, calling forth; *raama kaarya artha siddhaye* = Rama's, task, purpose, to
achieve; *samastaan* = to all of them; *abraviit* = spoke to; *vaanara
uttamaiH* = vanara-s, best ones; *bhavadbhiH* = by you; *evam etat* = in this way
[as I detailed,] all this [earth]; *vicetavyam* = shall be searched.

**अनुवादः**

On calling forth all the fly-jumpers for achieving the purpose of Rama's task the
best fly-jumper and the king of monkeys Sugreeva spoke to all of them, "oh, best vanara-s, you
shall search all of this earth as I have detailed." Sugreeva said so to all monkeys. [4-45-1,
2a]

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

इस तरह वहाँ वानरराज सुग्रीवके समीप बलके घमंडमें भरे हुए वानर उस समय एक-एक करके आते और उनके सामने उपर्युक्त बातें कहते थे ॥ १७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

ई प्रकार अल्लि वानरराज सुग्रीवन बळिगॆ बलदर्पितराद वानररु ऒब्बॊब्बरागि बन्दु मेलिनन्तॆ मातुगळन्नु आडुत्तिद्दरु.॥17॥
</details>

<details><summary>Desiraju Hanumanta Rao</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*17. tadaa* = then; *vaanaraa* = vanara-s; *bala
darpitaaH* = by might, in proper pride; *tatra* = there; *tasya hari raajasya
sannidhau* = that, monkey, king's, in audience; *eka ekaH* = one by one; *iti* =
in this way; *vacanam uucuH* = words [war whoops,] uttered - and departed.

**अनुवादः**

Thus each individual vanara gave war-whoops with the proper proud of his might in
the audience of monkeys king Sugreeva and departed. [4-45-17]

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/kish/sarga45/kishkindhasans45.htm)
</details>
