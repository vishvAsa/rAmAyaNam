<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. nishaa* = the night; *abhavat* = became; *kiMchichheshaa* = a little
remaning; *tathaa viprekshamaaNasya* = while (Hanuma) searched like that; *vaidehiim* = Seetha;
*vanam* = in the garden; *pushhpitapaadapam* = with flowers in blossom;

**अनुवादः**

A little of the night was left while Hanuma was searching like that for Seetha in the garden with
flowers in blossom.

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. saH* = That Hanuma; *shushraava* = heard; *brahma ghoshhaan* = Vedic
sounds; *viraatre* = early in the morning; *brahmaraakshasaam* = of Brahma Rakshasas;shhadaN^ga
veda vidushhaam = well versed in six parts of Vedas; *kratu pravara yaajinaam* = who performed excellent
sacrifices;

**अनुवादः**

That Hanuma heard Vedic sounds early in the morning of Brahma Rakshasas well versed in six parts
of Vedas and those who performed excellent sacrifices.

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*5. tasyaam* = In that Seetha's matter; *niyuktaH* = directed; *bHR^isham*
= a lot; *madanena* = by the god of love; *madotkaTaH* = excited by passion; *saH raakshasaH*
= that Rakshasa; *na shasaaka* = was not capable; *guuhitum* = to suppress; *aatmani* = in
self; *tam kaamam* = that desire.

**अनुवादः**

In that Seetha's matter, directed a lot by the god of love and excited by passion, that Rakshasa
was not capable to suppress in self that desire.

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*31. tathaa* = thus; *hanumaan* = Hanuma; *ugra tejaaH sannapi* = (although)
of terrible energy; *nirdhuutaH* = was overshadowed; *tasya tejasaa* = by Ravana's brilliance;
*abhavat* = (and) became; *patra guhyaantare saktaH* = rooted behind a group of leaves; *samvR^itaH*
= and was concealed;

**अनुवादः**

Thus Hanuma although of terrible energy was overshadowed by Ravana's brilliance and became rooted
behind a group of leaves and was concealed.
