**Book V : Sundara Kanda - Book Of Beauty**

Seetha calls out Rama and others and bursts into a wail not being able to endure the threats of
ogre women.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

जब वे क्रूर राक्षसियाँ इस प्रकारकी बहुत-सी कठोर एवं क्रूरतापूर्ण बातें कह रही थीं, उस समय जनकनन्दिनी सीता अधीर हो-होकर रो रही थीं ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

क्रूरस्वभावद आ राक्षसियरु हॆच्चागि भयपडिसुव कठोरवू, दारुणवू आद मातुगळन्नु केळि जानकियु अळतॊडगिदळु.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

1; *atha* = thereafter; *taasaam* = (as) those; *raakshasiinaam* = ogre
women; *asaumyaanaam* = with a cruel nature;vadantiinaam = were speaking; *bahu* = a lot; *parushham*
= harshly; *daaruNam* = (and) horribly;janakaatmajaa = Seetha; *ruroda* = cried.

**अनुवादः**

As those ogre woman with a cruel nature were speaking a lot harshly and horribly, Seetha cried.

उन राक्षसियोंके इस प्रकार कहनेपर अत्यन्त भयभीत हुई मनस्विनी विदेहराजकुमारी सीता नेत्रोंसे आँसू बहाती गद‍्गद वाणीमें बोलीं— ॥ २ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

भयग्रस्तळागिद्दरू पातिव्रत्यदल्लि दृढचित्तळागिद्द सीतादेवियु कण्णीरिडुत्ता गद्गदवाद स्वरदिन्द कूडि तडवरिसुत्ता हीगॆ हेळिदळु.॥2॥

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. evam* = thus; *uktaa* = being spoken to; *raakshasiibhiH* = by ogre
women; *vaidehii* = Seetha; *manasvinii* = with a good mind; *paramatrastaa* = being frightened
a lot; *baashhpa gadgadayaa giraa* = with a voice indistinct due to tears; *uvaacha* = spoke (as
follows).

**अनुवादः**

Thus being spoken to by ogre women, Seetha with a good mind being frightened a lot spoke as follows
with a voice indistinct due to tears.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘राक्षसियो! मनुष्यकी कन्या कभी राक्षसकी भार्या नहीं हो सकती । तुम्हारा जी चाहे तो तुम सब लोग मिलकर मुझे खा जाओ, परंतु मैं तुम्हारी बात नहीं मानूँगी’ ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

राक्षसस्त्रीयरे! मनुष्य स्त्रीयु राक्षसन भार्यॆयागलु खण्डितवागि अर्हळल्ल. नीवु बयसुविरादरॆ ऎल्लरू नन्नन्नु तिन्दु बिडि. नानु मात्र निम्म मातिनन्तॆ ऎन्दिगू नडॆदुकॊळ्ळुवुदिल्ल.॥3॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. maanushhii* = a human woman; *na arhati* = is not suited; *bhavitum*
= to become; *bhaaryaa* = a wife; *raakshasasya* = of an ogre; *sarvaaH* = all of you;
*khaadata* = eat; *maam* = me; *kaamam* = according to your desire;na karishhyaami = I will
not do; *vaH* = (according to) your; *vachaH* = words.

**अनुवादः**

"A human woman is not suited to become a wife of an ogre. All of you eat me according to your desire.
I will not do according to your words."

राक्षसियोंके बीचमें बैठी हुई देवकन्याके समान सुन्दरी सीता रावणके द्वारा धमकायी जानेके कारण शोकसे आर्त-सी होकर चैन नहीं पा रही थीं ॥ ४ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

रावणनिन्द भयपडिसल्पट्ट देवकन्यॆयन्तिद्द आ सीतादेवियु राक्षसस्त्रीयर मध्यदल्लि बिक्कि-बिक्किअळुत्ता दुःखार्तॆयागि शान्तियन्नु कळकॊण्डिद्दळु.॥4॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. raakshsaiimadhyagataa* = being in the middle of ogre women; *tarjitaa*
= being frightened; *raavaNena cha* = by Ravana also; *surasutopamaa* = like a daughter of gods;
*saa siitaa* = that Seetha; *na lebhe* = did not get; *sharma* = comfort; *duHkhaartaa*
= being tormented by grief.

**अनुवादः**

Being in the middle of ogre women, being frightened by Ravana also, like a daughter of gods, that
Seetha did not get comfort being tormented by grief.

जैसे वनमें अपने यूथसे बिछुड़ी हुई मृगी भेड़ियोंसे पीड़ित होकर भयके मारे काँप रही हो, उसी प्रकार सीता जोर-जोरसे काँप रही थीं और इस तरह सिकुड़ी जा रही थीं, मानो अपने अंगोंमें ही समा जायँगी ॥ ५ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

अरण्यदल्लि गुम्पिनिन्द तप्पिसिकॊण्डु तोळगळ मध्यदल्लि सिक्कि पीडिसल्पडुव जिङ्कॆयन्तॆ सीतादेवियु तन्न शरीरवन्नु मुदुडिसिकॊण्डु गड-गडनॆ नडुगुत्तिद्दळु.॥5॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*5. mR^igiiva* = like a fawn; *vane* = in a forest; *yuudhaparibhrashhTaa*
= lost from its herd; *arditaa* = tormented; *kokaiH* = by wolves; *vishantiiva* = (Seetha
was) as though sinking (into); *aatmanaH* = own; *aN^gam* = body; *vepate sma* = (and) shook;
*adhikam* = a lot.

**अनुवादः**

Like a fawn in a forest lost from its herd and tormented by wolves, Seetha was as though sinking
into own body and shook a lot.

= which was wide; *ashokasya* = of Ashoka tree; *chintayaamaasa* = thought; *bhartaaram*

**अनुवादः**
