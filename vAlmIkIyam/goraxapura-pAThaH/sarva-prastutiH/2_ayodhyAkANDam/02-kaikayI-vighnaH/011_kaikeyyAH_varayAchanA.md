<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

आग राजनु मन्मथन बाणक्कॆ ईडागिद्दनु. कामवेगक्कॆ कट्टुबिद्दिद्दनु. अदन्नरित कैकॆयियु अत्यन्त कर्णकठोरवाद मातन्नु हेळिदळु .॥1॥

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. kaikeyii* = Kaikeyi; *uvacha* = spoke ; *daaruNam* =
harsh; *vachaH* = words; *tam pR^ithiviipaalam* = to that king; *viddham* =
struck; *manmadhasharaH* = by cupid's arrows; *kaamavega vashaanugan* = yielded to
hasty lust.

**अनुवादः**

Kaikeyi spoke harsh words to that king, who was struck by cupid's arrows and yielded
to hasty lust.

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. deva* = Oh; King!; *naasmi* = I am not;
*viprakR^itaa* = insulted; *kenachit* = by anybody; *na avamaanitaa* = nor
despired; *tu* = but; *me* = my; *kashchit* = certain; *abhipraayaH* =
wish ; *ichchhaami* = I desire; *tam* = it; *kR^itam* = to be accomplished;
*tvayaa* = by you.

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, King! no body has insulted me, nor despised me. But I have a certain, desire
that needs to be fulfilled by you."

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. tvam ichchhasiyadi* = If you wish; *kartum* = to do;
*pratijJNaam pratijaaniishhva* = you promise; *atha* = Thereafter;
*vyaaharishhyaami* = I shall tell; *at* = that; *yat* = which;
*abhipraarthitam* = desired.

**अनुवादः**

"If you wish to fulfil my desire, you promise to fulfil it. After your promise I
shall inform you that which is desired by me."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

महाराज दशरथनु कामक्कॆ अधीननागिद्दनु. अवनु कैकॆयिय मातन्नु केळि कॊञ्च मुगुळ्नक्कु, नॆलदल्लि बिद्दिद्द आ देविय कूदलन्नु हिडिदॆत्ति तलॆयन्नु तन्न तॊडॆय मेलॆ इरिसिकॊण्डु आकॆयल्लि इन्तॆन्दनु.॥4॥

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. mahaatejaH* = The most majestic; *kaamii* = lustful
Dasaratha; *utsmitaH* = out smiled; *iishaat* = a little; *samgR^ihya* =
seized; *hastena* = with hand; *muuritaneshhu* = into hair; *taam kaikeyiim* =
that Kaikeyi; *shuchismitaam* = having white smile; *uvaacha* = and spoke

**अनुवादः**

The most majestic and lustful Dasaratha smiled a little and caressingly kept his
hand into her hair and spoke these words to Kaikeyi who was having white smile in her face.

me than you except Rama, the best among men."
