<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book II : Ayodhya Kanda - Book Of Ayodhya**

In this chapter Rama arrives at the king's presence. Seeing King disturbed, Rama
becomes grieved and wants to know the cause of king's perturbance. Queen Kaikeyi describes to Rama
about the boons that Dasaratha had given earlier and also her wishes of coronating Bharatha instead
of Rama and sending Rama to Dandaka forest. On listening to these harsh words of Kaikeyi, Rama did
not become sorrowful, but Dasaratha was very much disturbed.

**पदच्छेदः**

*5;6. tam mahaaraajam* = that king Dasaratha; *niH shvasantam* =
was sighing; *indriyaiH* = with senses; *aprahR^ishhTaiH* = sans pleasure; *shoka
santaapa karishitam* = emaciated with grief and anguish; *vyathitaakulachetasam* = with
distressed and agitated mind; *saagaram iva* = like ocean; *akshhobhyantam* =
becoming perturbed; *uurmimaalinam* = with now of waves; *aadityam iva* = like sun;
*upaplutam* = which is eclipsed; *R^ishhimyathaa* = as a sage; *uktaanR^itam*
= who spoke untruth.

**अनुवादः**

That king Dasaratha was sighing heavily with senses sans pleasure, emaciated with
grief and anguish, with distressed and agitated mind, like ocean which cannot be perturbed,
becoming perturbed with row of waves, like sun which is eclipsed and who lost lustre like by a sage
who spoke untruth.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*40. raghunandana* = oh; *Rama! kuru* = do; *etat
vachanam* = these words; *narendrasya* = of the king; *taarayasya* = liberate;
*narenshvaram* = the king.

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, Rama! Do as per the words of the king and liberate him by giving your father an
opportunity to maintain the truth."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

कैकेयीके इस प्रकार कठोर वचन कहनेपर भी श्रीरामके हृदयमें शोक नहीं हुआ, परंतु महानुभाव राजा दशरथ पुत्रके भावी वियोगजनित दुःखसे संतप्त एवं व्यथित हो उठे ॥ ४१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

कैकॆयिय इन्तह कठोर मातन्नु केळियू श्रीरामन हृदयदल्लि शोकवागलिल्ल. आदरॆ महानुभाव राजा दशरथनु पुत्रन भावी वियोग जनित दुःखदिन्द सन्तप्तनागि व्यथितनादनु.॥41॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*41. tasyaam vadantyaam* = while she was speaking; *parushham* =
harsh words; *itiiva* = in this way; *raamaH* = Rama; *nachaiva pravivesha* =
did not even enter; *shokam* = the sorrowfulness; *raajaatu* = but the king;
*mahaanubhaavaH* = the mighty; *pravivyathe* = become much disturbed
putravyasanaabhitaptaH = having been afflicted by calamity occured to hi son.

**अनुवादः**

Though she was speaking such harsh words, Rama was not affected with sorrow. But the
mighty Dasaratha became highly disturbed, having been afflicted by the calamity occurred to his
son.

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga18/ayodhyasans18.htm)
</details>
