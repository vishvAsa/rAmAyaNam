<details><summary>भागसूचना</summary>

41. श्रीरामके वनगमनसे रनवासकी स्त्रियोंका विलाप तथा नगरनिवासियोंकी शोकाकुल अवस्था
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तस्मिंस्तु पुरुषव्याघ्रे निष्क्रामति कृताञ्जलौ ।  
आर्तशब्दो हि सञ्जज्ञे स्त्रीणामन्तःपुरे महान् ॥१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तस्मिंस्तु पुरुषव्याघ्रे निष्क्रामति कृताञ्जलौ ।  
आर्तशब्दो हि सञ्जज्ञे स्त्रीणामन्तःपुरे महान् ॥१ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - VR</summary>

तस्मिंस्तु पुरुषव्याघ्रे विनिर्याति कृताञ्जलौ ।
आर्तशब्दोऽथ सम्जज्ञे स्त्रीणामन्तह्पुते तदा ॥ २-४१-१
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

पुरुषसिंह श्रीरामने माताओंसहित पिताके लिये दूरसे ही हाथ जोड़ रखे थे, उसी अवस्थामें जब वे रथद्वारा नगरसे बाहर निकलने लगे, उस समय रनवासकी रानियोंमें बड़ा हाहाकार मच गया ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

पुरुषसिंह श्रीरामनु मातॆयर सहित पितनिगॆ दूरदिन्दले कैमुगियुत्तिद्दनु. अदे स्थितियल्लि रथदिन्द नगरदिन्द हॊरगॆ हॊरटाग राणीवासद राणियरल्लि दॊड्ड हाहाकारवॆद्दितु.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. tadaa* = then; *tasmin* = that Rama;
*purushhavyaaghre* = the tiger among men; *kR^itaaNjalou* = having paid obeisance
with joined palms; *viniryaati* = departed; *atha* = moreover ; *aartha
shabdaH* = a great cry of distress ;samjaG^ye = arose ;striiNaam = among women ;
*antaHpure* = of inner apartments.

**अनुवादः**

As Rama the tiger among men, having paid obeisance with joined palms departed, a
great cry of distress arose among women of inner apartments.

</details>

यो गतिः शरणं चासीत् स नाथः क्व नु गच्छति ॥ २ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

वे रोती हुई कहने लगीं—‘हाय! जो हम अनाथ, दुर्बल और शोचनीय जनोंकी गति (सब सुखोंकी प्राप्ति करानेवाले) और शरण (समस्त आपत्तियोंसे रक्षा करनेवाले) थे, वे हमारे नाथ (मनोरथ पूर्ण करनेवाले) श्रीराम कहाँ चले जा रहे हैं? ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

अवरु अळुत्ता हेळतॊडगिदरु - अय्यो! अनाथरू, दुर्बलरू, शोचनीयरू आद नमगॆ गति (ऎल्ल सुखगळन्नु कॊडुव) मत्तु शरण्य (समस्त आपत्तुगळिन्द रक्षिसुव)नागिद्दु नम्म नाथनाद (मनोरथ पूर्णगॊळिसुव) श्रीरामनु ऎल्लिगॆ होगुत्तिरुवनु.॥2॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. kvanu* = Where; *saH naathaH* = is he the Lord;
*gachchhati* = going; *yaH* = who; *gataH* = was the path ; *aasiit* =
who became; *sharaNamcha* = the protector ;asya Janasya = of these people;
*anaathasya* = who are defenseless ; *durbalasya* = weak; *tapasvinaH* = and
unhappy."

**अनुवादः**

Where is he going, who was the path, who became the protector of the people, who are
now defenseless weak and unhappy?"

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga41/ayodhyasans41.htm)

</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘जो किसीके द्वारा झूठा कलंक लगाये जानेपर भी क्रोध नहीं करते थे, क्रोध दिलानेवाली बातें नहीं कहते थे और रूठे हुए सभी लोगोंको मनाकर प्रसन्न कर लेते थे, वे दूसरोंके दुःखमें समवेदना प्रकट करनेवाले राम कहाँ जा रहे हैं? ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

यारु यारादरू सुळ्ळु कळङ्क हच्चिदरू सिट्टुगॊळ्ळुत्तिरलिल्लवो, क्रोधदिन्द मातुगळन्नु आडुत्तिरल्लिवो, मुनिद ऎल्ल जनरन्नु ऒलिसि सन्तोषपडिसुत्तिद्दनो, अन्तह बेरॆयवर दुःखदल्लि सम वेदनॆ प्रकटगॊळिसुत्तिद्द श्रीरामनु ऎल्लिगॆ होगुत्तिद्दानॆ.॥3॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. kvachit* = where; *gataH* = has he gone; *kR^idhyati*
= who was not angry; *abhishapto. api* = even if accused ; *varjayan* = who resolved;
*krodhaniiyaani* = every misunderstanding; *prasaadayan* = who propitiated;
*sarvaan* = all those; *kruddhaan* = who were angry; *samaduHkaH* = by sharing
their suffering?"

**अनुवादः**

"Where has he gone, who was not angry even when provoked, who resolved every
misunderstanding and who propitiated all those who were angry by sharing their suffering?

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘जो महातेजस्वी महात्मा श्रीराम अपनी माता कौसल्याके साथ जैसा बर्ताव करते थे, वैसा ही बर्ताव हमारे साथ भी करते थे, वे कहाँ चले जा रहे हैं? ॥ ४ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

महातेजस्वी महात्मा श्रीरामनु तन्न मातॆ कौसल्यॆयॊन्दिगॆ वर्तिसिदन्तॆये नम्मॊन्दिगू वर्तिसुत्तिद्दनु. अवनु ऎल्लिगॆ होगुत्तिरुवनु.॥4॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. yaH* = which Rama; *mahaatejaH* = having great splendor;
*yathaa* = in which manner; *vartate* = was treating; *kausalyaayaam* =
Kausalya; *maatari* = his mother; *tathaa* = in the same manner; *vartate* =
was treating; *Asmaasu* = us; *kvanu* = where; *mahaatmaa* = that great-souled
Rama; *gachchhati* = has gone?"

**अनुवादः**

That illustrious and great-souled Rama, who treated us with the same reverence he
paid to his own mother, where has he gone?"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*5. kvanu* = Where; *gachchhati* = has he gone;
*paritraataa* = (he) the protector; *asya* = of these; *janasya* = people;
*jagataH* = and the world; *samachoditaH* = driven; *vanam* = to the forest;
*raaG^yaa* = by the king; *klishyamaanena* = tormented; *kaikeyyaa* = by
Kaikeyi"

**अनुवादः**

"Where has he gone, he the protector of the people and the whole world, driven to
the forest by the king, goaded on by Kaikeyi? ''

‘अहो! ये राजा बड़े बुद्धिहीन हैं, जो कि जीव-जगत् के आश्रयभूत, धर्मपरायण, सत्यव्रती श्रीरामको वनवासके लिये देश निकाला दे रहे हैं’ ॥ ६ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*6. raajaa* = the king; *nishchetanaH* = is a senseless man. Aho
= Alas! pravastyati = he has sent away from home; *vanavaase* = to dwell in forest;
*raamam* = Rama; *sampriyam* = the very beloved man; *dharmyam* = the pious
minded; *satyavratam* = strictly truthful .

**अनुवादः**

"Alas! In his folly, the king has banished the virtuous the beloved and the strictly
truthful Rama to the forest!"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

इस प्रकार वे सब-की-सब रानियाँ बछड़ोंसे बिछुड़ी हुई गौओंकी तरह दुःखसे आर्त होकर रोने और उच्चस्वरसे क्रन्दन करने लगीं ॥ ७ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

हीगॆ अवरॆल्ल राणियरु करुवन्नु अगलिद हसुविनन्तॆ दुःखदिन्द आर्तरागि गट्टियागि आक्रन्दन माडतॊडगिदरु.॥7॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*8. iti* = thus; *sarvaaH* = all; *taaH mahishhyaH* =
those queens; *rurudushchaiva* = shed their tears; *duhkhaartaaH* = in grief;
*dhenavaH iva* = like unto cows; *vivatsaaH* = deprived of their calves;
*vichukrushuH* = cried out; *sasvaram* = in loud noise.

**अनुवादः**

Thus did all the queens lament, like unto cows deprived of their calves and in there
affection cried out loudly.
