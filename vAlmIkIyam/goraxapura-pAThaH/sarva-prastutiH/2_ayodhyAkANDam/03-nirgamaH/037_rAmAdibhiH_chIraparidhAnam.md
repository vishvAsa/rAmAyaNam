37. श्रीराम आदिका वल्कल-वस्त्र-धारण, सीताके वल्कल-धारणसे रनिवासकी स्त्रियोंको खेदतथा गुरु वसिष्ठका कैकेयीको फटकारते हुए सीताके वल्कल-धारणका अनौचित्य बताना

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘राजन्! मैं भोगोंका परित्याग कर चुका हूँ । मुझे जंगलके फल-मूलोंसे जीवन-निर्वाह करना है । जब मैं सब ओरसे आसक्ति छोड़ चुका हूँ, तब मुझे सेनासे क्या प्रयोजन है? ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

महाराजरे! नानु सर्वसङ्ग परित्याग माडिरुवॆनु. ननगॆ काडिन कन्द-मूलगळिन्द जीवन निर्वाह माडुवुदिदॆ. नानु ऎल्ल आसक्तियन्नु बिट्टिरुवाग ननगॆ सैन्यद प्रयोजन एनिदॆ.॥2॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. raajam* = "Oh; *king! kim kaaryam* = of what use;
*me* = for me; *tyakta bhogasya* = who have forsaken enjoyments; *tyakta
sangasya* = who have cast away attachment; *sarvataH* = for everything;
*jiivitaH* = and am going to live; *vane* = in the forest; *vanyena* = on
nomadic food; *anuyaatreNa* = with an army following me?"

**अनुवादः**

"Of what use for me, who have forsaken enjoyments, cast away attachment for
everything and am going to live in the forest on nomadic food, Oh king, with an army accompanying
me?"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘जो श्रेष्ठ गजराजका दान करके उसके रस्सेमें मन लगाता है—लोभवश रस्सेको रख लेना चाहता है, वह अच्छा नहीं करता; क्योंकि उत्तम हाथीका त्याग करनेवाले पुरुषको उसके रस्सेमें आसक्ति रखनेकी क्या आवश्यकता है? ॥ ३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

श्रेष्ठ गजराजनन्नु दानमाडि, अदर हग्गवन्नु लोभवश इरिसिकॊळ्ळलु बयसुवुदु सरियल्ल; एकॆन्दरॆ उत्तम आनॆयन्ने त्याग माडुववनिगॆ अदर हग्गदल्लि आसक्ति इरिसुव आवश्यकतॆ एनिदॆ.॥3॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. yaH* = who; *dattvaa* = after giving away; *dvipa
shreshhTam* = a first-class elephant; *kurute* = will work out; *manaH* = his
mind; *kakshhaayaam* = on a rope with which it is tied? kim = what use; *tasya* = for
him; *rajju snehena* = by having attachment

**अनुवादः**

"Who, after giving away a first-class elephant, will work out his mind on a rope
with which it is tied? What use is there for him through attachment to the rope after abandaning
the excellent elephant?"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘सत्पुरुषोंमें श्रेष्ठ महाराज! इसी तरह मुझे सेना लेकर क्या करना है? मैं ये सारी वस्तुएँ भरतको अर्पित करनेकी अनुमति देता हूँ । मेरे लिये तो (माता कैकेयीकी दासियाँ) चीर (चिथड़े या वल्कल-वस्त्र) ला दें ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

सत्पुरुषरल्लि श्रेष्ठ महाराजरे! हीगॆये नानु सैन्यवन्नु पडॆदु एनु माडुवुदु? नानु ई ऎल्ल वस्तुगळन्नु भरतनिगॆ अर्पिसलु अनुमति कॊडुत्तेनॆ. ननगॆ (तायि कौसल्यॆय दासियरु) नारुमडियन्नु तन्दु कॊडलि.॥4॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. shreshhTa* = Oh the best; *sataam* = among good men!
jagatpate = Oh; *King! tathaa* = so also; *kim* = what use is there; *mama* =
for me; *dhvajinyaa* = by the army? anujaanaami = I am giving away; *sarvaaNyeva* =
all absolutely; *chiiraNyeva* = let the dresses of a mendicant; *anayantu* = be
brought; *me* = to me.

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, the best among good men! So also, what use is there for me by the army Oh,
King, when I am giving away absolutely all. Let the dresses of a mendicant be brought to me.'

vaasam* = in the abde of forest; *chaturdasha* = for fourteen; *varshhaaNi* =
years; *samaanayata* = bring; *khanitra pitake* = a showel and a basket;
*ubhe* = both; *mama* = for me.

**अनुवादः**

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

*7. sah* = that Rama; *purushha vyaaghrah* = tiger among men;
*pratigR^ihya* = taking; *te* = those; *chiire* = two pieces of bark;
*kaikeyyaaH* = from Kaikeyi; *avakshhipya* = discarding; *suukshhma vastram* =

**अनुवादः**

Taking those two pieces of bark (as loin cloth and cover) from kaikeyi and
discarding his clothing of fine yarn, Rama put on the garb of ascetics.
