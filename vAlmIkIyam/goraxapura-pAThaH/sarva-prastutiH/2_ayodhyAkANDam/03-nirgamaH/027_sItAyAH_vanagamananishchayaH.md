<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

श्रीरामके ऐसा कहनेपर प्रियवादिनी विदेहकुमारी सीताजी, जो सब प्रकारसे अपने स्वामीका प्यार पाने योग्य थीं, प्रेमसे ही कुछ कुपित होकर पतिसे इस प्रकार बोलीं— ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

श्रीरामनु हीगॆ हेळिदाग विदेहकुमारि, ऎल्ल रीतियिन्दलू तन्न पतिय प्रेमवन्नु पडॆयलु योग्यळाद सीतॆयु प्रणयकोपदिन्द ई प्रकार नुडिदळु.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. vaidehi* = Sita; *priya vaadinii* = who speaks kindly;
*priyaarhaa* = deserves kind utterances; *evam* = thus; *uktaa* = spoken;
*samkruddhaa* = became angry; *praNayaadeva* = only because of love;
*abraviit* = spoke; *idam* = these words; *bhartaaram* = to husband.

**अनुवादः**

Sita, who speaks kindly and deserves kind utterances, after hearing Rama's words,
became angry out of love alone and spoke thus to her husband.

**पदच्छेदः**

*22. ananya bhaavaam* = without anyother thought; *anurakta
chetanam* = my mind attached to you; *viyuktaam* = if separated; *tvayaa* = from
you; *nischitaam* = having decided; *maraNaaya* = to die; *nayasva* = usher;

**अनुवादः**

there will be no burden to you from me."
