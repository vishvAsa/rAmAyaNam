<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book II : Ayodhya Kanda - Book Of Ayodhya**

In this chapter Rama describes various troubles of staying in forest and beseeches
seetha not to go to forest

<details><summary>भागसूचना</summary>

28. श्रीरामका वनवासके कष्टका वर्णन करते हुए सीताको वहाँ चलनेसे मना करना
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स एवं ब्रुवतीं सीतां धर्मज्ञां धर्मवत्सलः ।  
न नेतुं कुरुते बुद्धिं वने दुःखानि चिन्तयन् ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स एवं ब्रुवतीं सीतां धर्मज्ञां धर्मवत्सलः ।  
न नेतुं कुरुते बुद्धिं वने दुःखानि चिन्तयन् ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम् - VR</summary>

सएवम् ब्रुवतीम् सीताम् धर्मज्ञो धर्म वत्सलः ।
निवर्तन अर्थे धर्म आत्मा वाक्यम् एतत् उवाच ह ॥ २-२८-१
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

धर्मको जाननेवाली सीताके इस प्रकार कहनेपर भी धर्मवत्सल श्रीरामने वनमें होनेवाले दुःखोंको सोचकर उन्हें साथ ले जानेका विचार नहीं किया ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

धर्मवन्नु तिळिद सीतॆयु हीगॆ हेळिदरू धर्मवत्सल श्रीरामनु वनदल्लि आगुव दुःखगळन्नु नॆनॆदु अवळन्नु जॊतॆगॆ करॆदुकॊण्डु होगुव विचार माडलिल्ल.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. saH* = Rama; *dharmaJNjaH* = the knower of righteousness;
*dharmavatsalaH* = who was fond of righteousness; *nakurute* = did not form;
*buddhim* = the idea; *netum* = of taking; *siitaam* = Sita;
*bruvatiim* = who was speaking; *evam* = thus; *chintayan* = thinking;
*duHkhaani* = of difficulties; *vane* = in the forest.

**अनुवादः**

Thinking of difficulties one will be called upon to bear in the forest, Rama who was
fond of righteousness did not form the idea of taking Sita who was speaking as aforesaid.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

सीताके नेत्रोंमें आँसू भरे हुए थे । धर्मात्मा श्रीराम उन्हें वनवासके विचारसे निवृत्त करनेके लिये सान्त्वना देते हुए इस प्रकार बोले— ॥ २ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

सीतॆय कण्णुगळल्लि नीरु तुम्बित्तु. धर्मात्मा श्रीरामनु अवळन्नु वनवासद विचारदिन्द निवृत्तगॊळिसलु सान्त्वन पडिसुत्ता इन्तॆन्दनु.॥2॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. saantayitvaa* = soothening with kind words; *taam* = to
Sita; *bhaashhpa duushhita lochanaam* = when eyes were blemished with tears;
*dharmaatmaa* = Rama; the virtuous minded; *uvaachaha* = spoke; *etat* =
these; *vaakyam* = words; *punaH* = again; *nivartanaarthe* = for the purpose
of making her turn back.

**अनुवादः**

Soothening with kind words to Sita, when eyes were blemished with tears, the
virtuous Rama spoke again as follows, for the purpose of waking her turn back.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘सीते! तुम अत्यन्त उत्तम कुलमें उत्पन्न हुई हो और सदा धर्मके आचरणमें ही लगी रहती हो; अतःयहीं रहकर धर्मका पालन करो, जिससे मेरे मनको संतोष हो ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

सीते! नीनु अत्यन्त उत्तम कुलदल्लि हुट्टिरुवॆ हागू सदा धर्माचरणॆयल्लि तॊडगिरुवॆ. आद्दरिन्द इल्लॆ इद्दु धर्मपालनॆ माडु, अदरिन्द नन्न मनस्सिगॆ सन्तोषवागुवुदु.॥3॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. siite* = Oh; *Sita! asi* = you are; *mahaakuliinaa* =
born in a high lineage; *sadaa* = ever; *nirataa* = devoted to; *dharme* =
righteousness; *tvam* = you; *aachara* = perform; *svadharmama* = your own
virtue; *iha* = here; *yathaa* = how; *sukham* = agreeable; *me
manasaH* = to my mind.

**अनुवादः**

"Sita! Your are born in a high lineage, ever devoted to righteousness. Perform your
own virtue here, which is agreable to my mind."

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

<details><summary>मूलम् - VR</summary>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘सीते! मैं तुमसे जैसा कहूँ, वैसा ही करना तुम्हारा कर्तव्य है । तुम अबला हो, वनमें निवास करनेवाले मनुष्यको बहुत-से दोष प्राप्त होते हैं; उन्हें बता रहा हूँ, मुझसे सुनो ॥ ४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

सीते! नानु हेळिदन्तॆ माडुवुदु निन्न कर्तव्यवागिदॆ. नीनु अबलॆयागिरुवॆ, वनदल्लि बहळ तॊन्दरॆगळिवॆ. अवन्नु निनगॆ हेळुत्तेनॆ केळु.॥4॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. siite* = Oh;Sita; *abale* = the delicate! yathaa tathaa =
whatever manner; *vakshhyaami* = I shall tell; *tvaam* = you; *kaaryam* = is
to be done; *tvayaa* = by you; *bahavaH* = many; *doshhaaH hi* =
inconveniences indeed; *vane* = in the forest; *nibodha* = know; *taan* =
them; *me* = from me; *vadataH* = speaking.

**अनुवादः**

"Oh Sita, the delicate! Do whatever I tell you. There are many inconveniencs in the
forest. Know them from me."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘सीते! वनवासके लिये चलनेका यह विचार छोड़ दो, वनको अनेक प्रकारके दोषोंसे व्याप्त और दुर्गम बताया जाता है ॥ ५ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*5. siite* = Oh; Sita; *eshaa* = this; *matiH* = thought;
*vanavaasakR^itaa* = made about forest; *vimuchyataam* = be given up;
*abhidhiiyati hi* = It is indeed said; *vanam* = forest; *kaantaaram* = the
wilderness; *bahudoshhayutam* = is fraught with many dangers.

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, Sita! Let your thought made about forest be given up. It is indeed said that
forest with its wilderness is fraught with many dangers."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘तुम्हारे हितकी भावनासे ही मैं ये सब बातें कह रहा हूँ । जहाँतक मेरी जानकारी है, वनमें सदा सुख नहीं मिलता । वहाँ तो सदा दुःख ही मिला करता है ॥ ६ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

निन्न हितदृष्टियिन्दले नानु इदॆल्लवन्नु हेळुत्तिद्देनॆ. ननगॆ तिळिदन्तॆ काडिनल्लि सदा सुख सिगुवुदिल्ल. अल्लि सदा दुःखवे सिगुत्ता इरुत्तदॆ.॥6॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*6. etat vachaH* = this advice; *abhidhiiyate hi* = is expressed
indeed; *mayaa* = by me; *hita buddhyaa* = keeping your welfare in view; *sadaa
nagaanaami* = I do not ever consider; *vanam* = forest; *sukham* = as
comfortable; *sadaa* = always; *duHkhameva* = uncomfortable.

**अनुवादः**

This advice is given by me, keeping your welfare in view. I do not ever consider a
forest as comfortable. It is always uncomfortable."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

पर्वतगळिन्द धुमुकुव जलपातद शब्दवन्नु केळि, पर्वतगळ गुहॆगळल्लि इरुव सिंहगळु गर्जिसतॊडगुत्तवॆ. अवुगळ गर्जनॆ केळलु बहळ दुःखमयवागि इरुत्तदॆ. अदक्कागि वनवु दुःखदायकवागिदॆ.॥7॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*7. ninadaaH* = the sounds; *girinirjaharasambhuutaaH* = created
from water falls in hills; *simhaanaam* = from lions; *girikandharavaasinaam* =
residing in mountain caves; *duHkHaaH* = are unpleasant; *shrotum* = to hear;
*ataH* = that is why; *vanam* = forest; *duHkham* = is uncomfortable.

**अनुवादः**

"The sounds created from waterfalls in hills and from lions resding in mountain
caves are unpleasant to hear. That is why living in a forest in uncomfortable."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘वहाँ क्रोध और लोभको त्याग देना होता है, तपस्यामें मन लगाना पड़ता है और जहाँ भयका स्थान है, वहाँ भी भयभीत न होनेकी आवश्यकता होती है; अतः वनमें सदा दुःख-ही-दुःख है ॥ २४ ॥

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

अल्लि क्रोध मत्तु लोभवन्नु त्यजिसबेकागुत्तदॆ, तपस्सिनल्ले मनस्सन्नु तॊडगिसबेकागुत्तदॆ. भयविरुव स्थानदल्लियू निर्भयनागि इरबेकागुत्तदॆ. आद्दरिन्द अल्लि दुःखवे दुःख इदॆ.॥24॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*24. krodha lobhau* = anger; greed; *vimoktavyau* = are to be
abandoned; *matiH* = devotion; *kartavyaa* = is to be done; *tapase* = on
asceticism; *bhetavye* = what needs to be feared; *bhetavyam* = should not be feared;
*ataH* = hence; *vanam* = forest; *nityam* = is always; *duHkham* =
suffering.

**अनुवादः**

"Anger and greed are to be abandoned by the dwellers of forest. Devotion is to be
bestowed on asceticism. What needs to be feared, should not be feared. Hence, living in a forest is
a suffereing."
