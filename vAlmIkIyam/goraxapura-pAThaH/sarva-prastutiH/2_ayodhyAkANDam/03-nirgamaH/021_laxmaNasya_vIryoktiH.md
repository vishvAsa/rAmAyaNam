<details><summary>भागसूचना</summary>

21. लक्ष्मणका रोष, उनका श्रीरामको बलपूर्वक राज्यपर अधिकार कर लेनेके लिये प्रेरित करना तथा श्रीरामका पिताकी आज्ञाके पालनको ही धर्म बताकर माता और लक्ष्मणको समझाना
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

उवाच लक्ष्मणो दीनस्तत्कालसदृशं वचः ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

इस प्रकार विलाप करती हुई श्रीराम माता कौसल्यासे अत्यन्त दुःखी हुए लक्ष्मणने उस समयके योग्य बात कही— ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

ई प्रकार विलपिसुत्तिरुव श्रीराममातॆ कौसल्यॆय बळि अत्यन्त दुःखितनाद लक्ष्मणनु आ समयक्कॆ सरियागि इन्तॆन्दनु.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. lakshhmaNaH* = Lakshmana; *diinaH* = became dejected; *uvaacha*
= spoke; *vachaH* = the words; *tatkaala sadR^isham* = suitable for that time; *taam
kausalyyaam* = to that kausalya; *rama maataram* = Rama's mother; *tathaa* = thus;
*vilapantiim* = weaping.

**अनुवादः**

Lakshmana became dejected and spoke these words, properly befitting for that time, to
that Rama's mother Kausalya who was thus weeping.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘बड़ी माँ! मुझे भी यह अच्छा नहीं लगता कि श्रीराम राज्यलक्ष्मीका परित्याग करके वनमें जायँ । महाराज तो इस समय स्त्रीकी बातमें आ गये हैं, इसलिये उनकी प्रकृति विपरीत हो गयी है । एक तो वे बूढ़े हैं, दूसरे विषयोंने उन्हें वशमें कर लिया है; अतः कामदेवके वशीभूत हुए वे नरेश कैकेयी-जैसी स्त्रीकी प्रेरणासे क्या नहीं कह सकते हैं? ॥ २-३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

दॊड्डम्म! श्रीरामनु राज्यलक्ष्मीयन्नु त्यजिसि काडिगॆ होगुवुदु ननगू ऒळ्ळॆयदॆनिसुवुदिल्ल. महाराजरादरो ईग स्त्रीय मातिगॆ मरुळागि अवर प्रकृति विपरीतवागिदॆ. ऒन्दु अवरु मुदुकरागिद्दारॆ, इन्नॊन्दु विषयगळिगॆ वशीभूतवागिरुवरु. आद्दरिन्द मन्मथनिगॆ वशीभूतरागि आ राजरु कैकॆयन्तह स्त्रीय प्रेरणॆयिन्द एनु तानॆ माडलाररु.॥2-3॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. "nR^ipaH* = king; *vipariitasheha* = with perverted mind; *
vR^idhdhhashcha* = old aged; *pradharshhitaH* = who is outraged; *vishhayaiH* = by
sensual enjoyments; *samanimadhaH* = who is possessed of passion; *na bruuyaat* = can
he not talk; *kimiva* = of anything; *chodyamaanaH* = prompted (by Kaikeyi).

**अनुवादः**

"The king with perverted mind, of old age, one who is outraged by sensual enjoyments
and possessed of passion can talk any thing, prompted by Kaikeyi."

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga21/ayodhyasans21.htm)
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. "aarye* = Oh; *the venerable lady! atat* = this; *na rochate*
= is not liking; *mamaapi* = to me also; *itiyat* = as in the manner this; *raaghavaH*
= Rama; *vaakyaarasham gataH* = being influenced by the words; *striyaaH* = of a woman;
*gachchhet* = go; *vanam* = to forest; *tyaktvaa* = leaving; *raajya sriiyam*
= prosperous kingdom."

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, the honoured lady ! I also do not like Rama to be influenced by the words of a woman
and go to the forest, leaving the prosperous kingdom."

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga21/ayodhyasans21.htm)

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. na pashyaami* = I can neither see; *aparaadham* = offence;
*na pashyaami* = nor can I see; *doshshamapi* = even fault; *asya* = in him;
*tathaa vidham* = that can; *yena* = by any means; *nirvaasyate* = expel; *raaghavaH*
= Rama; *raashhTraat* = from state ; *vanavaasaaya* = to forest."

"I can not see any offence or cause for blame in Rama that can expel him from the state

I have not seen in this world any person, whether an enemy or an expellee, speaking ill
of Rama indirectly."

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*6. "kaH* = who; *avekshhamaaNaH* = observing; *dharmam* =
ethicalness; *akaaraNaat* = causelessly; *tyajet* = get rid of; *putram* = son;
*devakalpam* = who is equal to god; *R^ijum* = who is honest; *daantam* = who is
self-restrained; *vatsalam* = who is affectionate; *R^ipuuNaamapi* = even towards enemies?"

**अनुवादः**

"Can anybody observing ethicalness, causelessly get rid of a son who is equal to god
who is honest, who is self-restrained and who is affectionate even towards adversaries?"

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*7. "kaH* = which son; *anusmaran* = who remembers; *raja vR^ittam*
= royal usage; *kuryaat* = act; *hR^idaye* = with heart; *tat idam vachanam* =
such of these words; *raajJNaH* = of king; *upeyushhaH* = who got; *baalyam* =
childhood punaH = again?"

**अनुवादः**

"Which son, knowing royal usage, can agree to the words of this king who is behaving
as though he got childhood again?"
