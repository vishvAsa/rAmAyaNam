42. राजा दशरथका पृथ्वीपर गिरना, श्रीरामके लिये विलाप करना, कैकेयीको अपने पास आनेसे मना करना और उसे त्याग देना, कौसल्या और सेवकोंकी सहायतासे उनका कौसल्याके भवनमें आना और वहाँ भी श्रीरामके लिये दुःखका ही अनुभव करना

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

वनकी ओर जाते हुए श्रीरामके रथकी धूल जबतक दिखायी देती रही, तबतक इक्ष्वाकुवंशके स्वामी राजा दशरथने उधरसे अपनी आँखें नहीं हटायीं ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

वनद कडॆगॆ होगुत्तिरुव श्रीरामन रथद धूळु कण्डुबरुव तनक इक्ष्वाकुवंशद स्वामि दशरथनु तन्न कण्णुगळन्नु अल्ले नॆट्टिद्दनु.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. yaavat* = so long as; *rajoruupam* = the appearance of dust;
*adR^ishyata* = was seen; *tasya* = (while) Rama; *niryataH* = was going;
*taavat* = so long; *ikshhvaakuvaraH* = Dasaratha the best of Ikshvakus; *naiva
samjahaara* = did not withdraw; *aatmachakshhushhii* = his eyes.

**अनुवादः**

As long as the dust raised by the wheels of Rama's chariot could be seen, so long
did the king not withdraw his gaze from it.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

वे महाराज अपने अत्यन्त धार्मिक प्रिय पुत्रको जबतक देखते रहे, तबतक पुत्रको देखनेके लिये उनका शरीर मानो पृथ्वीपर बढ़ रहा था—वे ऊँचे उठ-उठकर उनकी ओर निहार रहे थे ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

आ महाराजनु तन्न अत्यन्त धार्मिक प्रिय पुत्रनन्नु नोडुत्तिरुववरॆगॆ मत्तु अवनन्नु नोडलिक्कागि शरीरवन्नु ऎत्तरिसिकॊण्डिद्दनु. तुदिकालिनल्लि निन्तु अवलोकिसुत्तिद्दनु.॥2॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. yaavat* = As long as; *raajaa* = the king; *pashyati*
= was seeing; *priyam* = his beloved; *putram* = son; *atyanta dhaarmikam* =
who was exceedingly virtuous; *taavat* = so long; *dharaNyaam* = the dust in earth;
*vyavardhateva* = appeared to be raising; *asya putra darshane* = for the purpose of
seeing his son.

**अनुवादः**

As long as the king was seeing his beloved son who was exceedingly virtuous, so long
did the dust in the earth appear to be rising for the purpose of seeing his son.

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. yadaa* = When; *rajo api* = the dust assuredly; *asya
raamasya* = of the Rama;na pashyati = was no longer seen; *tadaa* = then;
*bhuumipaH* = the king; *aartaHcha* = afflicted by; *vishhaNNaHcha* =
dejection; *papaata* = fell; *dharaNiitale* = on ground.

**अनुवादः**

When the dust raised by Rama's chariot was no longer visible, the unfortunate king
afflicted by dejection, fell insensible on ground.

आग अवनिगॆ आसरॆयन्नु कॊडलु अवन धर्मपत्नी कौसल्यादेवियु बलकैय बळिगॆ बन्दळु मत्तु सुन्दरी कैकेयियु आतन ऎडभागदल्लि बन्दुनिन्तळु.॥4॥

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. kausalya* = Kausalya; *aNganaa* = his wife;
*anvaagaat* = took place; *tasya dakshhiNam baahum* = on his right side .kaikeyiicha
= Kaikeyi ;sumadhyamaa = of charming limbs; *anvagaat* = took her place; *vaamam* =
on the left; *paarshvam* = side.

**अनुवादः**

When Dasaratha fell down, Kausalya his wife took her place on his right side to lift
him up and Kaikeyi of charming limbs took her place on his left side.

**पदच्छेदः**

*6. kaikeyi* = Oh;Kaikeyi! tvam = you dushTachaariNii = of wicked
conduct; *maa spraakshhii* = do not touch; *mama* = my; *aNgaani* = limbs;
*nahi ichchhaami* = I do not want; *drashhTum* = to see; *tvaam* = you; *na

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, wicked Kaikeyi! Do not touch my limbs. I do not wish to see you .You are no
longer my wife nor a relative."

**पदच्छेदः**

*7. aham* = I; *na* = am not; *ye cha* = of those;
*anujivanti* = who are; *dependent ;tvaam* = on you; *na* = nor; *te* =
are they; *mama* = on me. aham = I; *tyajaami* = discord; *tvaam* = you;
*tvaktadharmaam* = who left righteousness; *kevalaarthaparaam* = and interested in

**अनुवादः**

"I am not of those who are dependent on you nor are they anything to me. I discord
you, by whom righteousness has been discorded and who are solely interested in avariciousness."
