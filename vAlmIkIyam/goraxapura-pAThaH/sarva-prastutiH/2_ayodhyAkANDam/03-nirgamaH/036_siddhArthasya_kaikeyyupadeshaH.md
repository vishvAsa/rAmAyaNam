<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

तब इक्ष्वाकु कुलनन्दन राजा दशरथ वहाँ अपनी प्रतिज्ञासे पीड़ित हो आँसू बहाते हुए लम्बी साँस खींचकर सुमन्त्रसे फिर इस प्रकार बोले— ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

आग इक्ष्वाकुकुलनन्दन दशरथनु तन्न प्रतिज्ञॆयिन्द पीडितनागि कण्णीरु सुरिसुत्ता दीर्घवागि निट्टुसिरु बिडुत्ता सुमन्त्रनल्लि पुनः ई प्रकार हेळिदनु-॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. tataH* = then; *aikshhvaakaH* = Dasaratha; the scion of
Ikshvaku dynasty; *piiditaH* = squeezed; *pratiJNayaa* = by his promise;
*atra* = in this matter; *atiniHshvasya* = sighed heavly; *sabhaashhpam* =
with tears; *jagaada* = spoke; *idam* = the following words; *punaH punaH* =
again and again; *sumantra* = to Sumantra.

**अनुवादः**

Then, Dasaratha the scion of Ikshvaku dynasty, squeezed by his own promise in the
matter of sending Rama to exile, sighed heavily with tears and spoke the following words again and
agian to Sumantra.

</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

*chaturvidha balaaH* = consisting of (all the four limbs of the army; *ratna
sampuurNaa* = filled with the best of its kind; *pratividhiiyataam* = be arranged;
*kshhipram* = immediately.

**अनुवादः**

"Oh, Sumantra! Let a military body consisting of (all) the four limbs of the arms,
filled with the best of its kind, be arranged immediately"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

चित्ताकर्षक रूपिनिन्दले जीविसुव मधुरभाषिणियराद स्त्रीयरु हागू क्रय-विक्रय द्रव्यगळ प्रसारणमाडुवल्लि कुशलराद वैश्यरु राजकुमार श्रीरामन सैन्यवन्नु सुशोभितगॊळिसलि.॥3॥

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

*3. ruupaajiivaashcha* = let courtesans; *vaadinyaH* = well
versed in speaking; *vaNijashcha* = merchants; *mahaadhanaaH* = with great riches;
*suprasaaritaaH* = having exposure for sales; *shobhayantu* = bestow elegance;
*vaahiniim* = to the forces; *kumaarasya* = of the prince.

**अनुवादः**

"Let courtesans well versed in speaking and merchants with great riches having
exposure for extension of sales bestow elegance to the forces of the Prince"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

श्रीरामनन्नु आश्रयिसि जीवन नडॆसुववरिगॆ, यारॊडनॆ श्रीरामनु वीर्यप्रदर्शन माडुत्ता क्रीडिसुवनो आ मल्लरिगॆ अनेक प्रकारद धनकॊट्टु अवरन्नु श्रीरामनॊन्दिगॆ होगुवन्तॆ आज्ञापिसु.॥4॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*4. niyojaya* = appoint; *atra* = in this contingent;
*taanapi* = those too; *dattvaa* = by giving; *bahudhanam* = abundant money;
*teshhaam* = to them; *yecha* = who; *upajiivanti* = were dependent;
*enam* = on him(Rama) yaishcha = with whom; *ramate* = he took delight;
*viiryataH* = in gallantry.

**अनुवादः**

Appoint in this contingent those too, by giving abundant money to them who were
dependent on Rama and on whom he took delight in gallantry"
