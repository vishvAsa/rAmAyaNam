<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book II : Ayodhya Kanda - Book Of Ayodhya**

Bharata replies that it is an established practice for the eldest son to become a
king and requests Rama to come to Ayodhya and get crowned in the kingdom for the welfare of
Ikshvaku race. Bharata requests Rama to offer traditional libations of water to their deceased
father, who died wtih the thought of Rama alone, absorbed in his mind.

</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. shrutvaa* = Hearing; *raamasya* = Rama's; *vachanam*
= words; *bharataH* = Bharata; *pratyuvaacha ha* = replied (as follows); *kim
kariSyati raajadharma* = "What will the discharge of royal duties amount; *me* = to me;
*vihiinasya* = who is bereft; *dharmaat* = of that code.

**अनुवादः**

Hearing Rama's words, Bharata replied as follows: "What will the discharge of royal
duties amount to me, who is outside of that code?"

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*8. raaghavaH* = O; Rama!; *aahuH khalu* = people indeed tell;
*iti* = that; *dattam* = the one given; *priyeNa* = by an affectionate son;
*bhavati* = becomes; *akSayyam* = measureless; *bhavaamshchaiva* = you alone;
*priyaH* = are beloved; *pituH* = to our father.

**अनुवादः**

"O, Rama! It is said that the one given by an affectionate son becomes measureless
and you undoubtedly are beloved to our father."

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

नम्म तन्दॆयवरु निन्निन्द अगलुत्तले शोकदिन्द रुग्णरादरु मत्तु निन्न शोकदल्ले मग्नरादरु. निन्नन्ने नोडुव इच्छॆयिन्द, निन्नल्ले नॆट्टिरुव बुद्धियन्नु तॊडॆयलारदॆ, निन्नन्ने स्मरिसुत्ता स्वर्गस्थरादरु.॥9॥
