<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book II : Ayodhya Kanda - Book Of Ayodhya**

Guha reports to Bharata, how Rama and his party broke their journey at
Shringaverapura and narrate to him the dialogue that took place between Lakshmana and himself
during that night. Guha further tells him how Rama and Lakshmana matted their hair into a thick
mass crossed the holy river with Seetha and left for the hermitage of Sage Bharadwaja.

प्रभाते विमले सूर्ये कारयित्वा जटा उभौ ।  
अस्मिन् भागीरथीतीरे सुखं सन्तारितौ मया ॥ २४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘प्रातःकाल निर्मल सूर्योदय होनेपर मैंने भागीरथीके तटपर (वटके दूधसे) उन दोनोंके केशोंको जटाका रूप दिलवाया और उन्हें सुखपूर्वक पार उतारा ॥ २४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

प्रातःकाल निर्मल सूर्योदयवादाग नानु भागीरथिय तीरदल्लि आलद हालन्नु तन्दुकॊट्टॆ. इब्बरू जटॆयन्नु कट्टिकॊण्ड मेलॆ नानु अवरन्नु नदि दाटिसिदॆ.॥24॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*24. suurye* = (When) the sun; *prabhaate* = rose;
*vimale* = brightly; *ubhau* = the two of them; *got kaarayitvaa jaTaaH* = got
their locks of hair matted; *asmin* = at the banks of River Ganga; *samtaaritau* =
and were ferried; *sukham* = safely; *mayaa* = by me.

**अनुवादः**

"When the sun rose brightly in the next morning Rama and Lakshmana got their locks
of hair matted into a thick mass at the banks of River Ganga and they were safely ferried by
me."

<details><summary>मूलम् - VR</summary>

जटा धरौ तौ द्रुम चीर वाससौ ।
महा बलौ कुन्जर यूथप उपमौ ।
वर इषु चाप असि धरौ परम् तपौ ।

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

‘सिरपर जटा धारण करके वल्कल एवं चीर-वस्त्र पहने हुए, महाबली, शत्रुसंतापी श्रीराम और लक्ष्मण दो गजयूथपतियोंके समान शोभा पाते थे । वे सुन्दर तरकस और धनुष धारण किये इधर-उधर देखते हुए सीताके साथ चले गये’ ॥ २५ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

तलॆयल्लि जटॆयन्नु धरिसि वल्कल हागू नारु बट्टॆयन्नुट्टु महाबली, शत्रुसन्तापि श्रीराम मत्तु लक्ष्मणरु ऎरडु यूथपति गजगळन्तॆ शोभिसुत्तिद्दरु. अवरु सुन्दर बत्तळिकॆ मत्तु धनुस्सुगळन्नु धरिसिकॊण्डु आ कडॆ-ई कडॆ नोडुत्ता सीतॆयॊन्दिगॆ हॊरटुहोदरु.॥25॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*25. tau* = those; *paramtapau* = Rama and Lakshamana;
*mahaabalau* = possessing great strength; *jaTaadharau* = wearing matted locks of
hair; *drumachiiravaasasau* = and clothes made of bark; *kuN^jara yuutha popamau* =
like unto the leaders of a herd of elephants; *vareSu chaapaasidharau* = wielding excellent
bow; arrows and sword; *vyapekSamaaNau* = looking back at me; *gatam* = went away;
*siitaayaa saha* = with Seetha.

**अनुवादः**

"Rama and Lakshmana, who were capable of tormenting their enemies, possessing great
strength, wearing matted locks of hari and clothes made of bark, like unto the leaders of herd of
elephants, wielding excellent bow, arrows and sword, looking back at me, went away with Seetha.

स्रोतः: [valmikiramayan.net](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga86/ayodhyasans86.htm)
</details>
