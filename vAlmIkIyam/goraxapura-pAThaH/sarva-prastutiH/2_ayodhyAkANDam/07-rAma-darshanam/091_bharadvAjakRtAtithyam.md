**Book II : Ayodhya Kanda - Book Of Ayodhya**

Having been instructed by Bharadwaja to bring the army to his place, Bharata allowed
his army also to come to the hermitage. Then, Bharadwaja invokes Visvakarma, the divine architect
and requests him to arrange for a befitting hopsitality to Bharata,

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

जब भरतने उस आश्रममें ही निवासका दृढ़निश्चय कर लिया, तब मुनिने कैकेयीकुमार भरतको अपना आतिथ्य ग्रहण करनेके लिये न्यौता दिया ॥ १ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

रात्रियल्लि आश्रमदल्ले तङ्गलु निश्चयिसिद भरतनन्नु भरद्वाजरु आतिथ्यक्कागि आमन्त्रिसिदरु.॥1॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*1. kR^ita buddhim* = (Seeing that he) had resolved;
*nivaasaaya* = to halt; *tatraiva* = at that place; *saH* = that;
*muniH* = sage; *tadaa* = then; *nyamantrayat* = prepared to offer;
*bharatam* = Bharata; *kaikeyiiputram* = the son of Kaikeyi; *aatithyena* =
the full honours due to a guest.

**अनुवादः**

Seeing he had resolved to halt at that place for the night, the sage Bharadwaja
prepared to offer Bharata the son of Kaikeyi the full honours due to a guest

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

यह सुनकर भरतने उनसे कहा—‘मुने! वनमें जैसा आतिथ्य-सत्कार सम्भव है, वह तो आप पाद्य, अर्घ्य और फल-मूल आदि देकर कर ही चुके’ ॥ २ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

इदन्नु केळि भरतनु हेळिदनु - मुनिगळे! वनदल्लि साध्यवागुव आतिथ्य सत्कारवागि नीवु अर्घ्य-पाद्य हागू फलमूलगळन्नु कॊट्टु माडिबिट्टिरुविरि. अदे दॊड्ड सत्कारवायितु.॥2॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*2. bharatastu* = Bharata on his part; *abraviit* = said;
*enam* = to Bharadwaja; *paadyam* = water to wash my feet; *arghyam* = and
hands; *kR^itam name* = have indeed been presented; *bhavataa* = by you;
*tathaa* = and; *idam aatithyam* = this hositality fitting for a guest; *yat*
= which; *upapadyate* = is available; *vane* = in a forest (was made available to
me).

**अनुवादः**

Then, Bharata said to Bharadwaja as follows: "Have you not presented me with water
to wash my hands and feet and offered me this hospitality fitting for a guest in this forest?"

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

हीगॆ हेळिद भरतनल्लि भरद्वाजरु नगुत्ता हेळिदरु - भरतने! नन्न कुरितु निनगॆ प्रेम इरुवुदन्नु नानु बल्लॆनु; आद्दरिन्द नानु एनन्नु कॊडुवॆनो अदरिन्दले नीनु सन्तुष्टनागु.॥3॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*3. atha* = thereafter; *bharadwaajaH* = Bharadwaja;
*prahasanniva* = smilingly; *uvaacha* = spoke; *bharatam* = to Bharata (as
follows); *jaane* = I know; *tvaam* = you; *priitisamyuktam* = are of a
friendly disposition; *tvam* = you; *tuSyeH* = are please; *yena kena chit* =
with whatever is offered to you.

**अनुवादः**

Then, Bharadwaja smilingly replied to Bharata as follows: "I know you possess a
friendly disposition and you are pleased with whatever is offered to you."

*kartum* = to offer; *bhojanam* = food; *tava* = to your; *etasyaaH* =

*priitiH* = satisfaction; *yathaaruupaatathaa* = in every suitable way.

**अनुवादः**

"O, Bharata the excellent among men! I wish to offer food to your army. You ought to

"O, warrior! Why did you come here, leaving your army at a distance? Why did you not
