<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy - Intro</summary>

**Book VI : Yuddha Kanda - Book Of War**

The battle between Rama and Ravana starts. Indra sends a chariot, an armour, some
arrows and a powerful spear. A tumultuous and thrilling battle ensues between Rama and Ravana. A
spear hurled by Ravana is thwarted by a powerful spear (sent by Indra) of Rama. Then, Rama strikes
Ravana's horses with arrows. He also pierces the chest region and the forehead of Ravana with his
fierce arrows. Ravana gets severely hurt.

*1. shrutvaa* = hearing; *tat vaakyam* = those words;
*uktam* = spoken; *lakShmaNena* = by Lakshmana; *raaghavaH* = Rama; *para
viiraghnaH* = the annihilator of his enemies; *aadaaya* = taking hold; *dhanuH* =

**अनुवादः**

Hearing those words of Lakshmana, that valiant Rama, the annihilator of his enemies,
taking hold of his bow, fixed an arrow on the bow-string.

<details><summary>अनुवाद (हिन्दी)</summary>

इसके बाद श्रीरामचन्द्रजीने सीधे जानेवाले महावेगवान् वज्रतुल्य पैने बाणोंके द्वारा रावणके अत्यन्त वेगशाली घोड़ोंको घायल कर दिया ॥ ६७ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>अनुवाद (कन्नड)</summary>

बळिक श्रीरामचन्द्रनु वेगवन्तवाद वज्रदन्तह हरित बाणगळिन्द रावणन अत्यन्त वेगशाली कुदुरॆगळन्नु गायगॊळिसिदनु.॥67॥
</details>

<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*67. saa* = that spear; *kShiptaa* = hurled (by Rama);
*nipapaata ha* = fell; *tasmin raakShasendrasya shuule* = on that Ravana's spear;
*bhinnaH* = torn down; *shaktyaa* = by Rama's spear; *mahaan shuulaH* = the
large spear of Ravana; *nipapaata* = fell (on the ground); *hata dyutiH* = with its
lustre extinguished.

**अनुवादः**

That spear, hurled by Rama, fell on that Ravana's spear. Torn down by Rama's spear,
the large spear of Ravana fell on the ground, with its lustre extinguished.
