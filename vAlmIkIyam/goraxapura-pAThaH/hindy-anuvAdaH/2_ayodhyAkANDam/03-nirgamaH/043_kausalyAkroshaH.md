<details><summary>Murali Krishnamurthy Kopalle Murthy</summary>

**पदच्छेदः**

*7. vane* = in the forest; *kaanu* = what; *avasthaa* =
fate; *bhavishhyati* = will befall (them);adR^ishTa duHkhaanaam = who had never seen
suffering (before); *tyaktaanaam* = who have been sent; *tvayaa* = by
you;vanavaasaaya = to a forest life;anumate = as per the wish; *kaikeyyaaH* = of
Kaikeyi.

**अनुवादः**
